Robert Pattinson has been confirmed as the fresh James Bond, prompting reactions from fans across the world. In the late night monologue above, Jimmy Kimmel adds his thoughts to the mix.

Industry observers note that "How is it that the new James Bond is only the second biggest spy story in the world right now?" the host jokes. "Hollywood is abuzz with the news that Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. And what a career: He's been in Harry Potter, Twilight, Batman, and now James Bond. I think we can officially declare Team Jacob the loser now, right?".

Kimmel goes on to play a parody of the announcement video released on social media, featuring Guillermo as a backup 007.

"Congratulations Guillermo," he notes. "I knew you'd bounce back from Dancing with the Stars.".
