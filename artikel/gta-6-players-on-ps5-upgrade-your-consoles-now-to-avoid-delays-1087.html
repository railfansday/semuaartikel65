Players poised to fire up their PS5 consoles and play Grand Theft Auto VI face one final hurdle to clear.

Sony has confirmed that a firmware revision is required before playing GTA 6, regardless of whether they are playing from Newly introduced Zealand or elsewhere in the world.

The upgrade, dubbed 26.07-14.23.00, makes "security fixes to the system software," according to Sony's official patch notes.

The enhancement is 1.267 GB, according to IGN, so it might be wise to do the update as soon as you can to avoid delays in your return to Vice City. After a 200+ GB preload for the game itself, it's relatively small, but who knows how many people will be trying to download it simultaneously as launch time nears.

It has been revealed that GTA 6 releases at midnight local time on Nov. 19 for gamers across the world.
