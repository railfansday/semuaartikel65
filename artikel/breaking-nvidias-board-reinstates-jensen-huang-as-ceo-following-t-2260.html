According to recent reports, the Nvidia board of directors has voted unanimously to reinstate the embattled Jensen Huang as the firm's CEO, the board announced in a surprise statement Monday morning. The move follows President Donald Trump publicly calling for Huang's return as CEO.

Huang famously resigned on Nov. 12 following reports from the Upcoming York Times and the Financial Times that he had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. In the days that followed, revelations of two more alleged extramarital affairs — with Nvidia employees, no less — emerged, seemingly sealing the CEO's fate.

According to recent reports, on Nov. 12, Huang unveiled he was resigning his position as CEO, a role he'd held since he co-founded the company in 1993.

In reinstating Huang, the board cited "input from senior levels of the United States government...that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk."
