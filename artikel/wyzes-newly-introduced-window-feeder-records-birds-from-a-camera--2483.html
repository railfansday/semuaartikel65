A year ago, Wyze introduced a $35 security camera that stuck to the inside of a window so you could keep an eye on what was happening outside your home without a complicated installation. Today the tech giant unveiled its new Window Bird Cam, which expands on that idea by pairing a similar window-mounted camera with a feeder that attaches to the outside of the glass. Alongside the kit, Wyze is also launching a new subscription service called Bird Watch, which adds identification capabilities and features that will benefit busy yards.

In a recent development, the Wyze Window Bird Cam is available today through the firm’s online store for $69.98 and includes the outdoor feeder with suction cups for mounting, a small tray for holding seeds, and a cover for protection from the elements. Wyze is also selling an optional $14.98 accessory called the Window Bird Cam Perch that gives birds a ”natural landing spot right in the camera’s portrait sweet spot,“ plus a fruit fork, suet holder, and a jelly cup to attract more visitors.

By installing inside your home and plugging into a USB power source (using an included 10-foot cable) the Bird Cam doesn’t need a battery, doesn’t need to be weatherproof, and will be well in range of your Wi-Fi network. Instead of the wide-angle view offered by most security cameras, the lens on the Bird Cam is designed to focus at a distance of 4 to 8 inches so birds look crisp while the rest of your yard is softly blurred.

Out of the box, the Bird Cam will detect motion and provide instant bird arrival notifications while 30fps video is recorded locally to a microSD card up to 512GB. You can also watch and share a livestream from the camera through a mobile device. The arrival notifications do have a five-minute cooldown so if you want to ensure you don’t miss any visitors, you’ll need to upgrade to Bird Watch for $6.99 per month, or $69.99 per year. The subscription adds species identification with a confidence score and access to an interactive bird wiki, full-length videos of bird visits stored in the cloud for 60 days, and no cooldown period between notifications.
