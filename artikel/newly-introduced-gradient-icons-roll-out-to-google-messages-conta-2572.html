📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google this week completed the rollout of its latest gradient icons with updates for Messages and Contacts.

The icon shapes remain the same for all three communication apps, though they are slightly larger and better fill the circular backgrounds. Contacts is still a head and body, while Messages remains overlapping chat bubbles and Phone a handset. This redesign is not particularly disruptive.

It has been revealed that A lighter shade of blue is leveraged, while all feature a gradient effect. This results in the logos feeling less heavy and a bit more modern.

Industry observers note that google describes the newly introduced Messages icon as “sharper, crisper, and clearer than ever before.” The SMS/RCS client did not roll out any other changes with the logo redesign this week. The significant homepage redesign is still in the beta channel.

It has been revealed that google Contacts was the last Android icon to be refreshed, with version 4.92.26.x rolling out on Wednesday evening. The Phone by Google patch rolled out on Monday to stable owners.

It took two weeks from announcement to full availability across the three apps.
