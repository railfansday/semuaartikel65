The launch of the Google Fitbit Air drew widespread attention — even though Whoop was always there.

Unfortunately, we haven't seen any deals on the popular Fitbit Air this Black Friday, but given its recent release and already budget-friendly price, it's not all that surprising. Sources indicate that that's right, the Luna Band is one of the rare examples of a fitness tracker that doesn't require a monthly subscription fee to unlock its full potential. There's also the Fitness Age feature, taking your fitness data and comparing it to what is typical for a person of your age.

Though pricing history is absent, and $30 doesn't seem like a huge saving overall, the Luna Band is nearly $200 cheaper than Whoop's flagship tracker. Plus, you don't need to pay extra for a monthly subscription to make the most of its smarts — so it's a no-brainer, really.
