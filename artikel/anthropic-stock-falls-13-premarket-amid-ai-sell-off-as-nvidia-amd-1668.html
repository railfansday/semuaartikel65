Anthropic stock falls 13% premarket amid AI sell-off as Nvidia, AMD also sink

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic fell 13% on Friday morning after a historic Nasdaq debut, as AI fears continued to fuel a sell-off, with Nvidia and AMD lower.

Anthropic soared 17% in its first day of trading on Thursday, after the manufacturer completed the biggest initial public offering on record, topping SpaceX's historic debut in June. The stock closed at $97.17 per share.

Investors had hoped that Anthropic's debut would revitalize the AI trade, which has come under pressure in recent months amid fears over capex spending.

The sector has also had to contend with the fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia CEO, alongside concerns that AI chip sales to Asia could slump as Washington looks to crack down on an export-control loophole.

On Thursday, short seller Hunterbrook Media alleged in a report that firmware sold by chipmaker AMD had turned up in U.S.-blacklisted companies. AMD stock closed down more than 7%. The chipmaker told CNBC it complies with U.S. law.

The House Foreign Affairs Committee also released documents showing that a subagency of the Commerce Department approved the sale of more than 879,000 AI chips to Chinese companies, highlighting a potential sales slowdown under stricter export controls.

— CNBC's Tobias Burns also contributed to this report.
