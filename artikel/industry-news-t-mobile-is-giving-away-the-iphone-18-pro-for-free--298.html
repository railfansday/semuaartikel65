TL;DR: The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free when you switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan. You can also get the iPhone 18 Pro for free if you trade in an eligible phone in any condition on a T-Mix plan.

It has been revealed that black Friday deals don't actually drop on Black Friday. Instead, we get heaps of early deals throughout November and even earlier. That's good news for anyone who desperately needs a tech upgrade without wanting the stress of the main event.

According to recent reports, why wait when T-Mobile is offering a stunning early Black Friday iPhone deal? We've rounded up all the best Black Friday deals from T-Mobile, but the standout option is probably the free Apple iPhone 18 Pro.

For a limited time, you can get your hands on the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free with T-Mobile. To qualify for this deal, you need to switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan. Alternatively, you can get the iPhone 18 Pro for free if you trade in an eligible phone in any condition on a T-Mix plan. It's that simple, folks.

According to recent reports, the full savings are distributed as monthly credits over a 36-month payment plan. If you leave T-Mobile before the end of the three-year contract, you will be charged the remaining balance. You also need to pay local sales tax on the phone's full retail value upfront, plus a standard $35 device connection fee per line at checkout. You can't escape those charges, even with these "free" deals.

Score the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free this weekend with T-Mobile.
