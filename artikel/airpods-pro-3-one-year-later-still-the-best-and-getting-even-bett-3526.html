Sources indicate that it’s been just over a year since Apple released AirPods Pro 3, and I can confidently say I’ve worn them every day since. After a year of heavy use, I’m even more convinced that AirPods Pro 3 are the best money you can spend on Apple gear (especially at current sale pricing).

It has been revealed that while the changes to AirPods Pro 3 fit have been controversial to some users, I’ve been thoroughly impressed over the last year. I find them incredibly comfortable, and I absolutely notice the improvement in noise cancellation enabled by the updated in-ear fit. The sound quality, of course, is also excellent, and is made better by the updated shape.

One feature I haven’t found myself using (or noticing) very often is the heart rate sensor. This is probably because I always wear an Apple Watch. Apple notes that AirPods Pro 3 and Apple Watch can work together to get you the most accurate heart rate data possible. I assume this happens in the background, but I never notice or think about it.

An interesting twist to AirPods Pro 3 is that they actually have worse battery life on paper than AirPods Pro 2.

I haven’t noticed the overall lower battery life at all. On long travel days, however, I absolutely appreciate the longer battery life from the buds themselves. I can now comfortably wear them from my departure airport to my arrival airport on most travel days. And I don’t have any issues just placing my AirPods Pro 3 on a Qi charger on my desk to refuel.
