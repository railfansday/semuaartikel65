📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A petition calling on Nvidia to reinstate the organization’s recently ousted CEO Jensen Huang appears to have attracted more than 115,000 signatures as of Saturday afternoon.

It has been revealed that the petition website stated the signers include 5,965 current Nvidia employees, 1,108 former employees, and 63,063 shareholders. Notable supporters include Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, Replit CEO Amjad Masad, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, and Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan.

The petition states that its signers are “concerned that Jensen was removed without meaningful due process” and demands that the board “bring him back while an independent investigation establishes the facts.” It also calls on the board to ensure a fair investigation and provide more information to the public.

“Jensen deserves due process, not mob justice,” the petition notes.

Huang resigned on Thursday, four days after confessing to having an affair with a chip executive suspected by U.S. intelligence to be a Chinese spy. The former CEO has agreed to testify before Congress, while news reports have also suggested that Huang engaged in multiple extramarital affairs.

Industry observers note that nvidia appointed CFO Colette Kress as the organization’s interim CEO while it searches for a permanent replacement. Nvidia’s stock price fell sharply through the week, while Huang’s departure is also expected to leave a big gap in AI industry funding.
