In a recent development, A good pair of earbuds with a new phone has become pretty common these days. It's like another accessory people can't seem to live without. Buying a new phone? Grab a pair of earbuds and maybe a watch. But good sound can be hard to come by.

Samsung debuted its Galaxy Buds 4 Pro this year, and Amazon has already slashed them by almost 30% in a slew of Early Black Friday deals.

According to recent reports, the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro offer a slight redesign of Samsung's previous aesthetic with silicone tips and a stabilizer to keep them in your ears. The Buds 4 Pro offer a sharp increase in audio clarity, along with voice and gesture controls.

Recent findings suggest that the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro made waves this year, and they're ✅ recommended if you want earbuds with detailed sound. The Buds 4 Pro's two drivers support the SSC UHQ codec, which refines audio beyond the Buds 3. What's more, the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro offers a newer, wider woofer that covers 20% more space (meaning more room for clear, powerful sound). The device's ANC (active noise cancellation) has improved over the Buds 3 for an uninterrupted experience.

On the flip side, you can ❌ skip this deal if you often have trouble finding eartips that fit. The Galaxy Buds 4 Pro don't offer a great range of options for our ears. Some eartips might fit better than others, or you might have a completely opposite experience. It's become a gripe for us, too. Additionally, you can also avoid these buds if you need to upgrade your phone.

According to recent reports, much of the Buds 4 Pro's performance and features come from newer software. If you don't have one of the latest Samsung Galaxy or Android phones with the latest software, you'll miss out.

The Galaxy Buds 4 Pro were a welcome upgrade. Samsung packed up to 30 hours of battery life into the buds (with ANC off), along with six mics. Calls should capture your voice without much interruption. However, the sound-quality upgrades are what truly won consumers over this year. The Buds 4 Pro accounted for 90% of units sold this year in Samsung's Galaxy Buds 4 series. The device crossed over 100,000 units sold after launch.

It has been revealed that consumers enjoyed that "Pro" meant something with these buds. After spending some time with the buds, we couldn't help but appreciate the subtle changes. Samsung seemed to have paid enough attention to sound quality to make it crisp and clear. While you're speaking, the buds lower their volume, so your conversation and audio stay uninterrupted (if you want). What's more, Samsung placed its touch controls on the body of the earbuds, not the stem.

Also, the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro Black Friday deal drops the price to that of the regular Galaxy Buds 4 option. It's hard to ignore, given all the upgrades.
