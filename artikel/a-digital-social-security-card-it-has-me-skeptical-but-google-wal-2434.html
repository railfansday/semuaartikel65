A digital Social Security card? It has me skeptical, but Google Wallet might do it

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

This feature might raise the most concerns about personal safety/privacy. A report claims Google Wallet is working on bringing digital support for our most precious identifier.

Together with AssembleDebug, Android Authority reports that Google Wallet could soon support digitizing a user's Social Security card. This is a most intriguing development. According to the tipster, there is code referencing "Social Security Number" in a recent Play Services version. As with your actual SS card, it would display your full name, card number, and DOB (date of birth).

Industry observers note that on the other hand, the tipster claims Google Wallet also references digitizing more than just your Social Security card. It may also support RSDI benefits (Retirement, Survivors, and Disability Insurance). It's been speculated that your wallet would hold any eligible benefits, benefit amounts, income, and the like. In theory, as the tipster also stated, this could help make a user's deeply personal info more accessible when out.

Industry observers note that you wouldn't have to lug your physical cards around. You'd only have to produce your phone. However, this is a critical security concern. The publication mentions that there's no sign (since these are code references) that Google is working with the SSA (Social Security Administration). We'll have to wait and see if anything progresses, and whether Google posts anything official about this if it is on a path to launch.

There's a lot to consider (and say) about this rumor, and we'll touch on that in a moment.

Industry observers note that however, we can't help but mention Google Wallet's recent update. The tech giant brought Gemini into Wallet, letting users connect the AI directly to their money. There is no typo there. If users want, they can involve Gemini in their transactions. The AI is limited. Gemini can only analyze your purchase history, create budget intends, and "visualize cash flow." In this regard, the AI is more like a digital accountant (I guess).

Gemini can't make purchases for you. This is no different from the AI shopping for you and then prompting you to open the app to complete the purchase. The thing is, as we found with our community on Facebook, trust is a key factor. And this feature that's been reportedly coming up for Google Wallet is no different.
