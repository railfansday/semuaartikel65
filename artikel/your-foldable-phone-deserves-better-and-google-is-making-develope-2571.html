Your foldable phone deserves better, and Google is making developers deliver

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that google wants Android apps to do more than simply stretch across a bigger screen. Starting in April 2027, developers will have to meet newly introduced adaptive quality standards to keep their apps eligible for publishing and updates on Google Play.

Sources indicate that the move adds to the pressure on developers to take foldable phones seriously and not to treat their larger displays as an afterthought. The newly introduced rules establish Tier 3, or Adaptive Ready, as the minimum requirement for standard apps. That means apps have to use the available screen without letterboxing or compatibility modes.

Google's announcement also says those apps must keep important information like scroll positions, text inputs, and media playback when users rotate, resize, fold, or unfold their devices. Support for multi-window environments is also mandatory.

The tech giant is also working to make store listings do more. Developers may already upload dedicated foldable screenshots in the Play Console, but from April 2027 latest apps and app updates will be required to upload three foldable screenshots, on top of the usual two phone screenshots. These should be of the app running on different foldable screen configurations: square 1:1 inner displays, 4:3 inner displays, and folded outer screens.

In a recent development, apart from basic compatibility, Google has two higher-quality tiers for developers willing to put in extra work. Tier 2, or Adaptive Optimized, includes support for accessories like keyboards, mice, trackpads, and styluses and responsive multi-pane layouts. Tier 1, or Adaptive Differentiated, goes even further, adding support for tabletop and book postures, camera continuity, interactive picture-in-picture, and multiple app instances.

Sources indicate that there’s something for developers in it too. Apps that reach Tier 1 or Tier 2 are eligible for premium Play Store badges, improved merchandising, and editorial features. In essence, Google is trading more visibility for apps that make better use of expensive hardware.

According to recent reports, games have a slightly different timeline and expectations. Foldable screenshots become mandatory in April, and its Tier 3 requirements kick in starting in August 2027. Higher tiers will also consider graphics quality, performance, controller support, and frame-rate stability.
