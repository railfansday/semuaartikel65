Sources indicate that if you've been looking for a compact Android tablet that can compete with Apple's iPad mini, the Lenovo Legion Tab Gen 5 is arguably one of the best options out there. While the tablet is a little expensive at its usual $900 price, Lenovo's flagship gaming tablet is now down to just $600, saving you a massive $300 (33%).

Industry observers note that the Lenovo Legion Tab Gen 5 packs flagship-level performance into a compact tablet with an 8.8-inch 165Hz display, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, 12GB of RAM, 256GB of storage, and a massive 9,000mAh battery. It's now $300 off ahead of Black Friday.

✅Recommended if: You want a compact Android tablet with flagship performance, a high refresh rate display, and excellent battery capacity for gaming, watching movies, or traveling.

❌Skip this deal if: You prefer a larger screen for productivity, specifically want an OLED display, or only need a basic tablet for browsing and streaming.

One of my favorite things about the Lenovo Legion Tab Gen 5 is its form factor. There aren't many compact Android tablets on the market that offer flagship-level specs, but the Legion Tab Gen 5 manages to pack some pretty powerful hardware into a device with an 8.8-inch display.

And while it uses an IPS LCD panel rather than an AMOLED display, it supports a refresh rate of up to 165Hz, which is considerably higher than the 60Hz display you get on the iPad mini.

But the biggest reason to consider the Legion Tab Gen 5 is its performance. The tablet is powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, paired with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of onboard storage. Even though it's no longer Qualcomm's latest flagship chipset, it's still one of the most powerful processors you'll find in an Android tablet.

Lenovo has also managed to pack a massive 9,000mAh battery into this compact tablet, which should give you plenty of gaming time without having to constantly reach for a charger.

In a recent development, and even if you're not particularly into gaming, I think the Legion Tab Gen 5 makes for a pretty great travel companion. Its 8.8-inch display is massive enough to comfortably watch movies, read books, or browse the web on a long flight, but the tablet itself is still compact enough to carry around without taking up much space in your backpack.

On the whole, if you've been waiting for a proper Android alternative to the iPad mini that doesn't compromise on performance, the Lenovo Legion Tab Gen 5 is definitely worth checking out. Its usual $900 price makes it a little difficult to recommend, but at $600, it's a much more compelling option.
