It's almost that time of year again. The time when Spotify reminds you exactly how many hours you spent listening to the same five songs, exposes your most embarrassing musical obsessions, and gives you a colorful little slideshow to share with everyone you know.

Yes, Spotify Wrapped season is upon us.

It has been revealed that every December, Spotify turns a year's worth of listening data into a personalized recap of your musical habits, from your most-streamed artists and songs to the total number of minutes you spent listening. Over the years, Wrapped has become something of an annual internet holiday, flooding social media feeds with colorful graphics, questionable listening habits, and people desperately trying to explain why a certain artist made their top five. (Listen, BTS released a newly introduced album this year. What did you expect me to do?).

And with November winding down, there's one question on everyone's mind: When does Spotify Wrapped 2026 come out?

In a recent development, spotify has not officially unveiled a release date for Wrapped 2026. However, based on previous years, we can expect it to arrive in late November or early December.

Sources indicate that notice a pattern? Spotify has released Wrapped on a Wednesday for five consecutive years. If that tradition continues, Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026, seems like a reasonable prediction. But that's just an educated guess, not a confirmed date.

Recent findings suggest that spotify has already started teasing this year's rollout. On Nov. 5, the streaming platform published a blog post encouraging listeners to prepare for Wrapped by updating their apps and reviewing which music influences their listening profiles.

In other words, the countdown is officially on.

Keep in mind that some Wrapped features have eligibility requirements, so not every listener will necessarily receive the same experience.

If you're hoping to squeeze a few more streams of your favorite artist into this year's results, you might be wondering whether Spotify is still counting. Despite a persistent internet rumor, Spotify doesn't automatically stop tracking your listening habits on Oct. 31.

For Wrapped 2025, Spotify confirmed that its listening-data window extended from January through mid-November, just a few weeks before the annual recap released.

In a recent development, the company hasn't confirmed the exact cutoff for Wrapped 2026, so it's unclear whether your late-November listening will be included in the final tally. Of course, that won't stop me from listening to the Grand Theft Auto VI soundtrack on repeat.

Industry observers note that spotify also lets consumers exclude individual tracks and playlists from their taste profiles, preventing them from influencing certain Wrapped results. So if your sleep playlist or that one song you played on a loop for a week doesn't exactly represent your musical identity, you have some control over what counts.

Spotify has yet to reveal this year's fresh Wrapped features, but the platform has a history of finding increasingly creative ways to interpret our listening habits.

Previous editions have assigned users a Listening Personality, matched them with a Sound Town based on their musical preferences, and even estimated their Listening Age. In 2025, Spotify introduced additional personalized insights and interactive features that went beyond the usual top artists and songs.

Whatever Spotify has planned for 2026, you can probably count on plenty of statistics, a few surprises, and at least one result that makes you question everything you thought you knew about your music taste.
