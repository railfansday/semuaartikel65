When I'm in a rush to leave the house, there's only one pair of earbuds that I shove into my pocket without a second thought: the Beats Solo Buds.

They're extremely compact and lightweight thanks to a sophisticated design that ditches the usual clunky earpod case battery and instead crams 18 hours of juice into each bud on a single charge, so they're easy to take on the go.

Industry observers note that better still, their Fast Fuel quick-charging feature means that you can squeeze an hour of playback out of a single hour of charge – and this is something I rely on all the time. I plug the buds directly into my phone's charging port when they're dead for a quick charge when I need a bit of extra juice. It's a real lifesaver and means that, as long as I carry a small cable, I never have to worry about being without my tunes.

Sources indicate that at just £39 (was £79), thanks to a mega EE discount, they're also simply incredible value and pack in plenty of that trademark Beats sound despite the small form factor. Beats may be owned by Apple, but that doesn't mean the Solo Buds don't play nice with Android. They support Google Fast Pair, and even have an Android companion app. (If you are an Apple user, they work perfectly with that ecosystem, too.).

These compact earbuds are a steal at under £40. They come in a wide range of attractive colors (though the translucent red is my personal favorite) and are both practical and stylish. EE is even throwing in free delivery.

✅Recommended if: You're after a seriously compact pair of earbuds that you won't need to worry about keeping charged.

According to recent reports, the Beats Solo Buds are some of the smallest and lightest wireless earbuds I've ever used when in their case, making them easy to take on the go. The Fast Fuel feature also makes them very quick to top up with charge, even when you're out and about.

Recent findings suggest that ❌Skip this deal if: You want to spend more and get better sound. Although I think the Beats Solo Buds can hold their own in the under £100 audio space with their poppy and bassy sound, the brand doesn't exactly have a reputation for audiophile quality.

If you want to spend more on something that sounds even better, you have plenty of options to choose from in the ongoing sales.
