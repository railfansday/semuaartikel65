We're always on the hunt for the latest offers on all things audio, and when it comes to Black Friday sales, I'm all ears.

We're still a few days away from Black Friday, and the tsunami of audio deals has already drowned us. But we're not complaining, because among this we've come across some great offers on the best headphones tried and tested by us.

Industry observers note that whether you're only just getting into the world of audio or you're looking to take your headphone game to the next level, there's something out there for everyone across top dog brands Sony, Bose, and Beats — just to list a few. And I get it, it can be overwhelming trying to cherry-pick deals all by yourself, but that's what we're here for.

Over the next few days, we'll be keeping our eyes peeled for fresh deals and any price drops that come our way, and we'll be updating this hub as we go along — so stay tuned.

We gave the Samsung Galaxy Buds 4 Pro a respectable 4.5 stars, and there's a reason for it. A remarkable upgrade from its predecessors, the audio is even more detailed, with onboard gestures and voice controls, all masked by a fresh redesign. Though you may make the most of its full functionality better with a newer Samsung device, that certainly shouldn't put you off.

Sony topped its XM5 headphones, which amazed us, by injecting better ANC performance, a battery life that lasts through long work sessions or journeys, and even bringing back the fan-favorite folding ear pads. It's still a little steep even with the price drop, but one we can't complain about for the latest model in the range.

If you're sticking to a budget this Black Friday, Beats' sub-$100 headphones are a solid, affordable option, but ones that don't compromise on great audio features. With 40mm drivers in each ear cup, they offer bold ANC and dynamic head-tracking Spatial Audio, topped with Dolby Atmos support for an even more immersive listening experience.

Since we last checked, Sony's previous-gen XM5s have dropped massively, saving you over half. Though these don't have folding ear pads, they make up for it with a versatile audio experience, packing excellent ANC and Ambient settings as well as incredible comfort. If these were priced like this when I first bought them, I'd jump at the chance.

Bose doesn't play around when it comes to audio, and a below-retail price is always welcome. The QuietComfort Ultra model comes with some smart upgrades: the revamped on-head detection and the AI-powered ActiveSense ANC algorithm, which could seriously give Sony a run for its money.

For a set of headphones that was released just this year, it's not a terrible deal at all, especially when they pack 135 hours of playback with ANC switched off, with support for Hi-Res Audio Wireless with the LDAC codec to optimize high-quality music streaming. Just a few days ago, these headphones were $2 cheaper, so it seems that the price is slowly creeping back up as we get closer to Black Friday — so it's wise to act swiftly.

Recent findings suggest that google's own earbuds are cheap and cheerful on a regular day, and they're under $100 for Black Friday. Easy and simple in their nature, Google Pixel Buds are ideal for audio novices who want a pair of earbuds that are affordable but deliver on performance at mid-volumes. Though they're not up to par with the big dogs of audio, they're a good choice for casual listeners.

Open-ear headphones are growing in popularity, and Black Friday is your perfect shot at scoring some if you want to hop on the trend train. We dubbed them the best open-ear headphones out there because of their immersive audio, fast charging, and overall simplicity. $50 isn't a massive price drop, but we're crossing everything for another one come Black Friday.
