Breaking: The Oppo Reno 17 and 17 Pro will be unveiled next week, but the specs have already leaked — here’s what you need to know

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Oppo is set to unveil its next mid-range phones in China next week, but the specs may have already leaked. According to reliable Weibo tipster Digital Chat Station, the Oppo Reno 17 series will consist of two models: the base Reno 17 and an upgraded Reno 17 Pro.

Industry observers note that interestingly, the cheaper of the two phones will apparently have the larger screen, coming in at 6.56 inches at a 1.5K resolution (the Reno 17 Pro's display, meanwhile, is a 6.32-inch 1.5K panel). On top of that, they claim the Reno 17 will be powered by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 SoC – a mid-range, 8-core chipset already found in this year's Oppo Reno 16 as well as similarly priced competitors like the Honor 600 and Nothing Phone (4a) Pro.

Recent findings suggest that as for the cameras, the Reno 17 will reportedly have a 50MP front selfie camera and a three-sensor rear camera setup featuring a 50MP main shooter, 50MP ultra-wide, and 50MP 3.5x periscope telephoto lens. If the leak is indeed accurate, the lack of a significant chip upgrade this generation is a shame, but it seems buyers of this newer model can at least look forward to improved battery life.

Digital Chat Station claims the Reno 17 will have an 8,000mAh cell with support for speedy 80W charging, a major upgrade over the Reno 16's 6,000mAh battery that should be able to handle multiple days of use on a single charge more comfortably — though it's yet to be seen whether the UK and European models will have the same capacity as the Chinese model.

It has been revealed that aside from the difference in screen size, the Pro model will allegedly offer an upgraded 200MP main camera and a significantly more powerful MediaTek Dimensity 8550 Super processor. The battery of the Pro seems slightly smaller than that of the base model, coming at 7,200mAh with the same 80W fast charging.

Both the Reno 17 and Reno 17 Pro are noted to have metal bodies and feature optical fingerprint sensors. There's no word on RAM, storage capacity, or color options yet. We'll be watching the Chinese release closely for more details.
