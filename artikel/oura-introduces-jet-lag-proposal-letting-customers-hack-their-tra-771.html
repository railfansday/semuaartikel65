Oura Ring owners who are always on the go are about to benefit from a feature to combat jet lag. Jet Lag Plan is a new Oura feature that creates a plan to navigate time changes before, during, and after travel.

According to recent reports, we have all been there with travel. As much fun as the vacation is, if you're dealing with time zone changes, jet lag can totally kill the vibe. You don't want to waste a moment in a latest place by taking a nap back at the hotel. Oura wants to help its Oura Ring users combat jet lag with its latest feature. The new feature is built off of a National University of Singapore study that used 1.5 million nights of sleep data from de-identified Oura users. Sources indicate that jet Lag Plan builds a plan designed just for you. It uses your flight itinerary along with your sleep patterns and chronotype to craft a series of recommendations. It produces a personalized schedule around sleep, light exposure, activity, and caffeine for journeys that cross three or more time zones.

The feature uses Oura's AI-powered support, Oura Advisor, to adjust the proposal as travel changes, in case things don't go as expected. Jet Lag Plan will be available globally to people with Gen 3 or newer hardware on both iOS and Android, as long as users have their language set to English.

So if you're traveling for the holidays, don't let jet lag stop you from your vacation or family time.
