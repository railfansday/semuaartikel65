Robot vacuums and mops have come a long way from that old Roomba you’ve been using, and having tested a lot of different options, the Narwal Freo 20 lands the title of my favorite options this year thanks to its thoughtful design, excellent performance, and solid value – and Black Friday makes it an even better deal.

Narwal Freo 20 is built on the same basic foundation as the Narwal Flow 2 I reviewed earlier this year. It has an all-in-one dock with heated washing and drying for the mops and an auto-empty dustbin, plus a robot with powerful obstacle avoidance.

It has the same track mop for deep cleaning of your floor, the same 31,000 Pa of suction power, and uses the same solid Narwal app for mapping and controls.

What’s the difference? There are only a few.

Recent findings suggest that I think the Flow 2 has a much nicer design (especially in its darker color that blends in better to homes that aren’t all white). It has some useful lighting effects to keep you in the loop at a glance, and Flow 2 has a detergent dispenser for the mop. But, aside from that, Freo 20 matches the flagship model in almost every important way, and it’s proven that in cleaning my home for the past few weeks.

It also has a couple of perks that the Flow doesn’t, mostly on the dock.

Narwal Freo 20 has deep water tanks that give you more time between changing out the water. For an all-in-one system with both mops and an auto-empty dustbin built into the dock, I’ve never seen water tanks this size and I love it.

I’m easily going at least a week or even up to two before the clean water tank is empty (though you should definitely empty the dirty tank sooner than that, trust me). The dustbin is hidden in a door inside of the water tank compartment.

Industry observers note that the dock also has a single button up top for starting a run without using your phone (or the voice commands, which I wish no robot vacuums had). That’s a very small thing, but it’s been a really nice addition for starting a cleaning run of my home as I head to bed for the evening.

Despite its flagship-tier performance, though, Narwal Freo 20 is a really good value.

It has been revealed that normally, it sells for just over $1,000, but you can get it around Black Friday for $799, and I think it’s one of the best values you’ll find for that price. Flow 2 is also heavily discounted right now, but I’d still rather save a couple hundred dollars on Freo 20 – they’re really that close in terms of actual experience.
