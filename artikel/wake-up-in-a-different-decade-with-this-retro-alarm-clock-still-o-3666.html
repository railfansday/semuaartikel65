Give your mornings a lil retro reboot with an alarm clock that looks like it came from an office desk circa 1985. The Maclock Retro PC Alarm Clock is on sale for $39.99 (reg. $69.99) during our extended Black Friday promotion.

It has been revealed that this fun, nostalgic alarm clock brings a little vintage-computer personality to your home or workspace, complete with a cream-colored case, a rainbow accent stripe, and a miniature disk slot. It’s a playful throwback that also happens to handle the not-so-fun business of waking you up.

The pixel-style display shows the time, date, day, and temperature, with options to switch between a calendar view and a pixel-smile mode. You can choose between 12-hour or 24-hour time, set the temperature to Celsius or Fahrenheit, and adjust the screen’s backlight using the wheel on the side. For the alarm, there’s one available time with five settings: One-Time, Weekdays, Weekends, Daily, or Off. A touch-sensitive button on top gives you an extra 10 minutes of sleep for those mornings when you’re not quite ready to face the day.

According to recent reports, despite its old-school appearance, the Maclock has a rechargeable 1,200 mAh battery and USB-C charging. It can last up to 60 days displaying the time with the backlight turned off, and takes about two hours to recharge. Measuring just 3.62 by 3.15 by 4.45 inches and weighing about half a pound, it’s compact enough for a nightstand, desk, or reading nook.

It could also make a thoughtful holiday gift for a tech-loving coworker or someone who’s notoriously difficult to shop for. Just know that the clock uses a single beep for its alarm, and its day and calendar display is in English only.
