Smart video doorbells used to be quite expensive, but unlike smartphones, which have gotten considerably more expensive over the past year, video doorbells seem to have gone in the opposite direction. You can get a pretty good one for surprisingly little money these days, with even some of the cheaper models offering features like 2K video recording, night vision, and two-way audio.

Recent findings suggest that and thanks to the ongoing Black Friday sale, they've gotten even cheaper. In fact, some of the biggest names in the smart home space, including Ring, Arlo, and Blink, are offering discounts of over 50% on their video doorbells right now. I've rounded up five deals worth checking out, including a couple of options that don't require a monthly subscription to save your recordings.

What's even better is that four of them cost less than $40/.

Sources indicate that if you're looking for an affordable way to get started with smart home security, it's hard to argue with this $22 Blink video doorbell. It offers 2K video quality with a head-to-toe view, night vision, and noise cancellation for clearer conversations. Since it's a wired doorbell, you don't have to worry about recharging it either. You'll need a compatible Alexa device or a separately sold Blink Chime for in-home doorbell alerts, though.

If you'd rather get a wireless video doorbell, Arlo's second-generation model is available for just $28. It offers 2K HDR video, two-way audio, and night vision, so you can see and speak to whoever's at your front door even at night. There's also support for person and package detection, though you'll need an Arlo Secure subscription to access those advanced features.

Sources indicate that at just $38, the Tapo D205 is a great option if you don't want to pay for a subscription or a separate storage hub. It supports local recording via a microSD card of up to 512GB and offers AI-powered person detection at no extra cost. You also get a 2K camera with a 160-degree field of view, night vision, two-way audio, and a built-in 5,200 mAh battery for up to 180 days of use.

It has been revealed that if you already have a couple of Amazon Echo devices around your house, this Ring video doorbell is probably the one I'd recommend. It features video with up to 6x zoom, two-way audio, and motion alerts. You can also receive doorbell notifications through your Echo speakers or pull up the live camera feed on an Echo Show.

It has been revealed that the Eufy E340 is a little more expensive than the other video doorbells on this list, but it comes with two cameras: one to see who's at your front door and another to keep an eye on your packages. You also get 2K video and 8GB of built-in local storage without a monthly subscription. Plus, you can use it as either a wired or battery-powered doorbell.

Personally, I'd spend a little extra on a video doorbell that supports local storage. It's nice being able to go back and check what happened outside your house without having to pay for yet another monthly subscription.

Industry observers note that the Tapo D205 is particularly appealing in that regard at just $38. That noted, if you regularly get packages delivered to your doorstep, the dual-camera setup on the Eufy E340 might be worth the extra money.
