Wired earphones never died, they were simply overshadowed — at least that's what the team at retro-revival tech company Commodore say.

Hot on the heels of its Callback 8020 flip phone, Commodore has stepped back into the world of audio with its newly introduced Talkback Professional In-Ear Monitors, which the manufacturer describes as another step in its goal to herald 'the return of intentional technology'. Their price tag is also more-than-reasonable at just ($50 / £38.05).

Just like its anti-doomscrolling and de-Googled mobile device, Commodore's new wired earbuds embrace everything we loved about tech from the '00s, with a touch of nostalgia for anyone that remembers Commodore in its 1980s computing heyday. Even its release campaign screams 'frutiger metro'. If you're unfamiliar with the term, just think about classic iTunes ad campaigns from 2006.

Sporting a timeless transparent look, Commodore's Talkback IEMs pack a 3.5mm audio jack and silicon-cushioned earbuds that hook over your ears for added support. There's nothing worse than wearing a pair of the best wireless earbuds, only to have them slip out while you're commuting, working out, or just enjoying some downtime.

Taking inspiration from the past, the earbuds come fitted with an in-line microphone, volume and play/ pause buttons, taking the chore out of reaching for your phone to control every aspect of the playback experience.

But beyond its nostalgic aesthetics, Commodore is looking to provide a bold listening experience at that modest price point.

Partnering with engineers from the audio behemoth Fiio, Commodore wants to ensure that audio isn't compromised, designing its wired IEMs for 'for both casual customers and keen audiophiles alike'. According to Commodore, its wired earbuds are fine-tuned to offer clarity and detailed audio, all paired with controlled bass and a balanced mid-range to really enhance its immersive audio capabilities.

According to recent reports, although Bluetooth earbuds can also offer crisp audio along with the convenience of a wireless experience, wired audio accessories tend to be favored more for getting the most of out of your hi-res/ lossless music streaming. They significantly reduce latency (useful when gaming or keeping an eye on lip-sync issues in videos), and direct wired connectivity reduces Bluetooth compression, allowing you to listen to audio close to how it was initially uploaded.

The Commodore Talkback Professional In-Ear Monitors are available to pre-order now, but the organization hasn't disclosed an official release date yet.
