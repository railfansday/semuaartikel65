The arrival of the latest flagship Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max might be dominating the headlines, but the Chinese smartphone giant also just lifted the curtain on a range of accessories that are worth paying attention to.

If you're a fan of open-ear audio, then the newly introduced Xiaomi Clip earbuds are going to be the start of the new reveals. They're the brand's first clip-on model, and weigh just 5.5g per earbud.

If you're not familiar with this relatively recent trend, clip-on earbuds attach to your ears with a gentle clamping mechanism rather than being pushed inside your ear canal like conventional buds. This makes them highly comfortable and leaves your ear canals open to allow in background noise like traffic or conversations.

They're not a good fit for a loud commute, but such designs have become a popular choice for fitness or scenarios where you want to maintain full situational awareness while still being able to enjoy some tunes.

Xiaomi promises that the Clip earbuds will feel like they're "barely there" while in use, and accommodate a wide range of ear shapes thanks to their flexible shape-memory alloy bridge material.

In a recent development, fashion is clearly a focus, and the Xiaomi Clip earbuds are offered in four distinct colorways: Sand Gold, Pearl White, Graphite Black, and Frost Purple. They all come with a compact charging case that's designed to look something like a jewelry box.

As for the more technical specs, each bud contains an 11mm dynamic driver. They offer Hi-Res certified sound and support the modern LHDC 5.0 codec. According to Xiaomi, the battery life comes in at 9 hours of music playback, with an additional 29 hours offered by the charging case.

They cost £129.99 and are available via the Mi website.

There's no better companion for a new Android smartphone than a compatible smartwatch, and Xiaomi has you covered there too with the new Watch S5 41mm.

It has been revealed that this £199.99 smartwatch boasts a 1.32 AMOLED display with up to 3,000 nits of peak brightness. It runs Xiaomi HyperOS 4 and is the first Xiaomi Watch S model compatible with Google Wallet – so you can conveniently pay with your wrist without the need for Xiaomi Pay.

It comes packed with a range of sensors, including a heart rate sensor with blood oxygen sensing capabilities, an accelerometer, gyroscope, ambient light sensor, and temperature sensor. Xiaomi also states that the watch can last up to 14 days on a single charge.

Sources indicate that it's available in three color options: Black, Light Blue, and Ceramic White. Black and Light Blue both offer stainless steel bezels and rubber straps, while Ceramic White has a ceramic bezel and comes with a leather strap.

Sources indicate that finally, Xiaomi is refreshing its affordable smart band. The Xiaomi Smart Band 11 starts at just £54.99 and offers a bright 1.72-inch AMOLED display and the ability to read notifications and see call alerts, in addition to tracking your fitness.

There is a wide range of color options available, with different build materials depending on what you pick. The standard Midnight Black, Glacier Silver, Mystic Rose, and Moon Gold models feature an aluminum frame.

If you want a band with NFC capabilities, you can also pick up the crisp white ceramic edition or a full-metal unibody model in lavender.
