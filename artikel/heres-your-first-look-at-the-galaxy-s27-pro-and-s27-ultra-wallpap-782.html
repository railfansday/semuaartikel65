Samsung's Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra are still a few months away from launch, but their official-looking wallpapers have already leaked online.

Sources indicate that we've already seen plenty of leaks surrounding the Samsung Galaxy S27 series. A recent leak suggested that the Galaxy S27 Ultra could come with a major camera downgrade, while previous reports have also pointed to Samsung adopting a horizontal camera plateau design for the Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra.

And now, a new leak has given us a closer look at the wallpapers Samsung could ship with these devices.

According to recent reports, sammyGuru has shared the wallpapers of the Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra ahead of their launch, and they look quite similar to the Galaxy S27 and Galaxy S27 Plus wallpapers that leaked earlier. They feature the same ribbon-like pattern resembling the letter S.

According to recent reports, interestingly, the wallpapers are available in the same five colors as the previously leaked ones, including Navy Blue, Light Blue, Burgundy, Light Pink, and Pearl White. This could also give us an early idea of the colors Samsung is planning to offer for the Galaxy S27 Ultra and Galaxy S27 Pro, although nothing has been confirmed just yet.

You can download the watermark-free versions of these wallpapers directly from SammyGuru's website.

It's also worth noting that tipster Ice Universe previously revealed that the base model of the Galaxy S27 Ultra could be available in Black, Blue, Light Pink, and White, while Burgundy could be an exclusive color option for a higher-storage variant.

Of course, Samsung hasn't officially confirmed any of these designs or color options, and things could still change before the Galaxy S27 series rolls out next year. But if you like the look of these latest wallpapers, you don't have to wait until 2027 to try them on your current phone.
