Google’s AI feature in Search gets its own incognito mode

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google’s search engine has long offered an incognito mode for all those search queries you don’t want logged or associated with your account. Now, Google’s AI Mode in Search is getting a similar feature. In a blog post, the company announced a latest feature that brings “temporary threads” to AI Mode, where the conversations you have won’t be resurfaced later in areas like your search history or recommendations.

Many leading AI chatbots already offer the option for temporary chats, Google Gemini included. However, there was not yet an option to have a temporary chat when you’re searching Google and are dropped into its conversational interface, known as AI Mode.

Google states that people won’t have to adjust their settings to take advantage of the latest option. Instead, they’ll be able to click a temporary threads icon in the upper right corner of AI Mode to engage in a temporary chat. This essentially works the same as a temporary chat would in the stand-alone Gemini chatbot.

The option could be convenient to use at those times when you start digging deeper into a particular topic via AI Mode and realize that you don’t want the discussion to become a part of your search history because of its personal nature.

Sources indicate that once the temporary thread is started, the session becomes detached from your account, Google says. This means that the responses the AI provides won’t be informed by past preferences, nor will they impact future personalization. That could also be useful for those times when you want to learn about something as more of a one-off, but don’t want Google to start to think of the topic as one of your main interests.

When you close out of the thread, you will return to searching as usual.

The company states the option to engage in temporary chats in AI Mode is rolling out globally on desktop and mobile across all supported languages.
