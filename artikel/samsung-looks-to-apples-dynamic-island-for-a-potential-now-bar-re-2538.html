For years, Apple has taken pages out of Samsung's book when developing its own devices — just look at the iPhone Duo, which arrived seven years after Samsung's first foldable. But oh, how the tables have turned.

Samsung is busy preparing for its next smartphone lineup, the Galaxy S27 Series, and a new leak points to a possible redesign for the Live Notifications view in the Now Bar, which could be moving front and center where the front camera sits. SamMobile spotted the changes while sifting through the One UI 9.5 software.

Recent findings suggest that as it stands, the Now Bar (which displays real-time tools such as timers, map directions, and other ongoing activities) sits in the top-left corner next to the time display. If it looks familiar to you, you're not alone — numerous users have compared it to Apple's flagship Dynamic Island feature, which was introduced with the iPhone 14 Pro back in 2022.

While the Now Bar is projected for a redesign, its lock screen version is set to remain in its usual position at the bottom-center of the smartphone display.

It has been revealed that however, it's not just the design that Samsung is drawing inspiration from Apple. Instead of the current up-and-down swipe navigation you use to view your Live Notifications, the leak reveals a new left-to-right swipe function when flicking through your ongoing activities in its new pill-shaped form, just like the Dynamic Island.

Recent findings suggest that but where Samsung could take it a step further is with a fresh Now Bar settings page, which was also spotted in the leaked firmware. As well as the redesign, SamMobile also found 'Moments effects', a new page within the Now Bar where you can download add-on packs that show basic animations.

Right now, only the 'Sports' pack is listed as an available add-on, which displays the prompt "Celebrate your favourite teams’ scores and wins. Available for select sports supported by Google Sports". When a team you're tracking via Google Sports scores or wins, it triggers a fun animation that appears in the Now Bar. Though this feature was softly introduced in the One UI 9.5 Beta 1, the leak suggests Samsung has plans to add more categories in the future.

In a recent development, these at-a-glance notification centers are pretty much the fresh norm with every smartphone release. Though Apple arguably pioneered it a few years ago, countless tech brands have followed suit with their own versions, so it was only a matter of time before Samsung had to bite the bullet.

There's no doubt the Now Bar redesign will be a welcome quality-of-life upgrade, but we won't know anything for certain until Samsung decides when it's the right time to divulge more information.
