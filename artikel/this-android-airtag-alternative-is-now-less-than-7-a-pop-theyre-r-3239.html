If you're like me, you put your keys down the second you get into the house and immediately forget where you left them.

That's where smart tags come in: these little trackers tell you exactly where you've put whatever one is attached to and will guide you straight to its location. They can even emit an alarm sound, making it easier to find something that you might have stowed away in a pocket or desk drawer.

Apple's AirTag is a popular choice, but right now mobile accessory maker Ugreen is selling a four-pack of its Android alternative for just $26.99 (down from $33.99) at Amazon as part of its Black Friday sales. That's right, a pack of not one or two but (count 'em) four standalone smart tags that you can attach to your keys, stow in your luggage, and so on. That works out to just under $7 per tag!

The Finder Duo Air Tracker Tags are fully compatible with Google's Find Hub, so you don't need to worry about Android compatibility. Despite being cheaper, they also boast some advantages compared to other trackers. Namely, batteries that you can recharge via USB Type-C and a louder speaker to make them even easier to locate when you need to use the alarm.

In a recent development, these little smart tags are perfect if you keep losing your keys or otherwise want to keep track of your stuff. They're rechargeable via USB-C and fully compatible with Google's Find Hub.

✅Recommended if: You want affordable smart trackers that work well with an Android phone to keep track of your keys or other small objects.

It has been revealed that ❌Skip this deal if: You don't think the trackers will be useful, or you don't need four. If you can't think of anything that you would be likely to lose, then chances are these trackers are not a good investment.

Similarly, if you would rather spend the same amount or more on just a single tracker, then there are other options available.
