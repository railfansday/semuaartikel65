TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Jumpstart Booster Box is on sale for $119.98 at Amazon, down from its $139.48 list price.

In a recent development, we're keeping a close eye on the best trading card deals this Black Friday. For those shopping for a more beginner-friendly way to get into the Marvel Super Heroes trading card crossover, you can now pick up Jumpstart Boosters for a record-low price.

Recent findings suggest that as of Nov. 28, you can get your hands on the Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Jumpstart Booster Box for just $103.85 at Amazon. With this latest markdown, which takes $16.14 off the box's typical $119.99 price (13% off), you’ll now be paying only $4.33 per booster.

Paying even less than the previous Amazon record of $104.90 we spotted just over a week ago, it's also now more affordable than buying through TCGplayer, where the least expensive listing totals $105.21 after shipping — despite a market price of $97.16.

Keep in mind that the discounted box comes courtesy of third-party seller Kings Games, but Amazon is still handling delivery.

For Marvel lovers who haven't yet ventured into MTG, Jumpstart does away with the homework of deckbuilding by letting you simply pick two packs, shuffle, and then you’re ready to go. Each 20-card booster has one of 51 possible themes, with even the required Lands already accounted for — making getting into learning the trading card gameplay that much easier.
