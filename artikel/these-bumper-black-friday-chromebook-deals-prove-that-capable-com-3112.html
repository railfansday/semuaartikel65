Chromebooks offer one of the most affordable and reliable avenues into computing. These budget-friendly laptops are powered by Google's ChromeOS, a lightweight operating system built around the Google Chrome browser. Unlike Windows, which can struggle massively on cheaper devices, ChromeOS is highly optimized and run smoothly even on low-end specs.

Industry observers note that this makes Chromebooks an excellent pick for those with more basic computing needs, or who just want a laptop that works well and doesn't cost a fortune. Although they are limited to browser-based apps, there are loads of advantages to a Chromebook too.

They boot quickly and are exceptionally secure: in fact, Google claims that no Chromebook has ever been infected with a virus. They also pair fantastically with an Android phone, with a level of cross-functionality that Google people will seriously appreciate.
