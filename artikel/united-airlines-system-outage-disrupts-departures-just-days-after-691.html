📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that less than a week after an issue with air traffic control communications and radar caused the FAA to halt takeoffs nationwide, United Airlines departing flights are being disrupted by an outage. At 6:13PM ET, United said the problem has been fixed:.

Earlier today we experienced a technology-related matter that impacted some departing flights. The issue has been resolved. Please check the latest status of your flight at united.com or the United app.

The outage is also coming after last week’s global Microsoft Outlook shutdown, and the great-bank-outage-that-wasn’t earlier Monday afternoon. According to posts on social media, a number of people onboard the planes have heard from their pilots that a systemwide IT outage for United has caused problems with a weight and balance calculator that has stopped them from getting clearance to take off.

United tweeted earlier that “We are troubleshooting a technology problem affecting departing flights. There is no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights.”.

Update, November 16th: Added note that United says the problem has been resolved.
