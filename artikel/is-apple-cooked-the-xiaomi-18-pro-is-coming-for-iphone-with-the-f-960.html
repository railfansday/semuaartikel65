Industry observers note that after we missed out on the Xiaomi 17 Pro and Pro Max last year, the Chinese smartphone giant is finally bringing its dual-screen flagships to the West. It's just launched the latest Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max, and I would be pretty worried if I were a certain Cupertino-based competitor.

Both phones look like an all-round excellent package, with top-of-the-line specs including the brand-fresh Snapdragon 8 Elite chipsets.

According to recent reports, the Xiaomi 18 Pro is the model for those after a more compact, but still powerful, phone, with its 6.4-inch 120Hz AMOLED front display and 6,000mAh battery. It's powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chipset, paired with 12GB of RAM and either 256GB or 512GB of storage.

Recent findings suggest that the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, meanwhile, bumps the main screen size up to 6.9 inches and upgrades the chipset to the absolutely top-of-the-line Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 – currently the fastest Android SoC on the market. The base model offers 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, but there are also more expensive 12+512GB and 16+1TB versions available.

The bigger design also makes space for a much larger battery, which comes in at 7,050mAh with 100W wired HyperCharge support (the base 18 Pro, in contrast, only supports 90W wired HyperCharge).

The front displays of both phones support Xiaomi's new privacy display technology, which is effectively the organization's take on Samsung's Privacy Display feature and helps obscure sensitive information from prying eyes.

Both the 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max boast similar camera setups, with a 200MP main shooter and 200MP periscope telephoto lens alongside a 50MP ultra-wide. As with other recent Xiaomi devices, the cameras were co-engineered with Leica and offer a range of photo styles inspired by the camera maker.

Recent findings suggest that for me, the real highlight, however, is the innovative rear display situated on the back of both models. It offers a range of handy features, from controlling media playback to offering a real-time view of the cameras to make selfies easier.

You can fully customize it with a range of watch-like faces as well (including some options with cute 3D animal mascots) and even use generative AI to create your own apps right from the settings menu.

It's seriously cool and seems like a genuine reason to pick the 18 Pro or 18 Pro Max over other flagship models.

All of this doesn't exactly come cheap, however. The Xiaomi 18 Pro starts at £1,199 (that's roughly $1,600), and the 18 Pro Max starts at £1,399 (around $1,850) for the 12+256GB model.

They both come in four color options: Black, White, Velvet Red, and Cosmic Blue.
