Is wide the next phone craze? The new Huawei Osta O1 Pro looks like a fat iPhone Air

It has been revealed that last week I reported that Huawei was set to launch a newly introduced sub-brand called OSTA, with its first phone being an interesting wide-screen model called the OSTA O1 Pro. As of today, everything has been made official — and the OSTA O1 Pro is available to order now via the Huawei VMALL site in China.

As reliable Weibo tipsters suggested, the OSTA O1 Pro does indeed boast a unique wide form factor. Thanks to a newly uploaded spec sheet, we now know that it has a 6.39-inch display with a 120Hz refresh rate, starts at 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, and is powered by the Kirin 9030S chipset – the same mid-range chip as the standard Huawei Pura 90.

The phone has a 7,000mAh battery, with support for 66W SuperCharge and 5W wired reverse charging, as well as 50W wireless charging when using a compatible charger. As for the cameras, the OSTA O1 Pro has a 200MP main shooter paired with a 50MP telephoto lens. There doesn't seem to be a dedicated ultra-wide or macro lens present. There is, however, a 50MP selfie camera on the front of the phone.

Sources indicate that the OSTA O1 Pro is available in three colours: Starry Night Black, Nebula White, and Starlight Purple (though the latter is only offered in 16GB+512GB and 16GB+1TB configurations). Prices start at 5699 CNY for the most basic model, which comes to roughly $855 or £650.

It has been revealed that the OSTA O1 Pro would be a pretty unexceptional mid-range phone, were it not for that unique wide-screen format. It almost looks like an iPhone Air, but fatter and more... compressed. That's not a bad thing either, as I think wider formats like this definitely have a place.

According to recent reports, the popularity of the Samsung Galaxy Fold 8 (which is similarly stout) shows that Android owners are certainly interested in trying form factors that are a bit more out there than the usual candy bar shape. The design of the OSTA O1 Pro also just looks like it would be very comfortable in the hands and fit comfortably in most pockets.

Wider aspect ratios have advantages when it comes to multitasking too, as they make it easier to view multiple windows side by side when using the phone horizontally.

The major question is whether the OSTA O1 Pro will make it to the West in any official capacity and, sadly, I don't think it's likely. Huawei pulled out of the US market following a US government ban in 2022 and has largely stopped selling its handsets in the UK as well. The only phones at the moment listed on the Huawei UK site are the Mate XT Ultimate Design and Mate X6 foldable, neither of which is actually available to buy on the store.

That remarked, we might still see other manufacturers come out with their own wide-screen model – especially if the OSTA O1 Pro proves popular with Chinese consumers.
