📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Patch: The ground stop has been lifted, and domestic flights resumed late Friday.

For the second time since 9/11, all air travel in the United States has been temporarily halted.

In a recent development, the Federal Aviation Administration issued a rare nationwide ground stop on Friday afternoon, leaving countless travelers stranded at airports across the U.S. Officials, including Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy, attributed the stoppage to a communications issue whose cause is unknown at the time of publication.

Of course, the question on many people's minds right now is: When will the ground stop be lifted? Unfortunately, we do not know the answer to that right now. CNN reported that Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said the government has "not discussed any timeframe.".

“It’s going to take some time,” Mullin stated, per CNN. “And so this isn’t going to just be resolved and turned around within the hour.”.

That means this could very well last through Friday evening, at least. For reference, the last nationwide ground stop in 2023 lasted about 90 minutes, but Mullin's rhetoric seemed to suggest this one could take longer to resolve.
