I spent hours tracking down the 12 best Android tablet deals this Black Friday, with prices starting at under $100

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Black Friday is almost here, and that means savings on all things Android. In my eyes, there's simply no better time to invest in a new Android tablet as there are so many great deals about.

You might be wondering whether you actually need a tablet and, as a frequent Android tablet user myself, I have some advice. If you watch a lot of online content (whether that's YouTube videos or TV series via services like Netflix) they can be a seriously worthwhile purchase as the bigger screens lead to a much more cinematic viewing experience. I find them great to travel with too, as using a tablet for my media needs keeps my phone battery topped up while I'm out and about.

The more powerful, productivity focused models can also work as full-on laptop replacements. I frequently pack a lightweight tablet and keyboard case when I set off for work trips, as it offers a convenient way to draft stories without the hassle of lugging around my heavier (and much more costly to lose) laptop.

If you're thinking about getting in on the Android tablet action, then these are the models I recommend from the ongoing sales.

This compact Lenovo tablet is designed for basic day-to-day tasks like responding to emails or browsing the web. It's not particularly powerful, with an older Helio G85 chip, and has just 4GB of RAM and 64GB of storage. Still, these are the kind of specs you should expect for under $100 - and you do at least get a free case here.

The Amazon Alexa Tablet 8 is a step up from the Lenovo model. It's more powerful, with a more premium aluminum design, though it is still limited by its 64GB storage capacity and 4GB of RAM. There are multiple configurations available, however, and I would recommend paying $30 more for the 128GB version.

Industry observers note that the TCL Tab 10 Gen 4 is a good pick if you want a budget-friendly tablet with a display that's a bit larger and crisper than the Lenovo and Amazon alternatives. The 10.1-inch panel has a 1920×1200 resolution for an improved viewing experience, and it comes with a microSD slot so you can upgrade the 128GB of built-in storage to keep more movies or TV indicates downloaded.

The TCL Tab A1 Plus upgrades the display even further with a 12.2-inch 2.4K resolution panel at a responsive 120Hz refresh rate. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 processor and 6GB of RAM also hit the sweet spot between price and performance. This is another model with a microSD card slot, so you can upgrade the 128GB of storage down the line.

The Samsung Galaxy Tab A11+ doesn't have the greatest specs for its price, with a 90Hz 1920x1200 screen and MediaTek Dimensity 7300 SoC paired with 6GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, but it does offer that beloved Samsung software and is worth it if you want to get into the ecosystem.

I think Lenovo's tablets are seriously underrated. I've tested plenty, and they've always delivered premium build quality and good performance for the price. This MediaTek Dimensity 6300-powered tablet has a 2.5K 90Hz screen and is fitted with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. It's also bundled with a free case and compatible stlyus pen, making it perfect for students.

The Amazon Alexa Tablet 11 offers a larger, higher-resolution 11-inch 2.5K display compared to the Tablet 8. It's got 8GB of RAM and a premium aluminum design. Storage starts at 64GB (on sale for $199.99), but I would seriously recommend buying the 128GB version I've spotlighted here instead, even though it's a little more expensive, so you don't constantly run out of space.

It has been revealed that the NXTPAPER 11 Plus might look like an ordinary tablet, but it actually features TCL's unique NXTPAPER display tech. This lets you transform the screen from a regular tablet display to something that performs more like an e-reader at the press of a button — making this ideal for books and comics.

Samsung's mid-range Galaxy Tab has received a significant price cut for Black Friday, and it's a strong pick. You're getting a 10.9-inch 90Hz LCD screen, 128GB of storage with the option to expand it with a microSD card, a large 8,000mAh battery, and 6GB of RAM. This bundle includes an S Pen stylus too.

If you can splash out a bit more for the S10 FE, you'll get a higher-resolution display and a faster processor. Other additions, like a fingerprint reader, make for a more convenient user experience. As with the S10 Lite, you're also getting an S Pen stylus here.

Industry observers note that amazon's most high-end tablet is designed for serious productivity. It's got a speedy 8-core chip, 8GB of RAM, and a 2.8K resolution 120Hz display. This all comes in a sleek aluminum form factor that's lightweight and easy to transport.

This upgraded Alexa Tablet 12 Pro comes with Amazon's fresh Nanomatte display coating, which is designed to cut down glare and make it easy to see what you're doing in even the brightest of environments.
