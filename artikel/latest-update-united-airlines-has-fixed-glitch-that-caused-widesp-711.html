Latest Update: United Airlines has fixed glitch that caused 'widespread system outage'

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The glitch was seemingly unrelated a separate problem that caused the recent nation-wide FAA ground stop.

Recent findings suggest that air travelers may be seeing more disruptions today, but the problems appear to be limited to a single tech giant. United Airlines noted on X in response to several customers' posts that it was aware of "widespread system outage." The company has given that reply to multiple passengers, however there is no standalone announcement on the United Airlines X account at this time.

"We are troubleshooting a technology concern affecting departing flights," a spokesperson from United told Engadget. "There is no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights. There is no indication that this is a cybersecurity-related issue.".

It has been revealed that posts on social media from United passengers suggest that they've been told that a computer system for weights and measures is down. One customer's pilot reportedly mentioned that the problem is unrelated to the ground stop from the US Federal Aviation Administration at the end of last week.

United later confirmed it had addressed an unspecified "technology issue" that caused the delays, but didn't elaborate on a cause. "We have resolved a technology issue that delayed flight departures," the manufacturer said. "While some flights were delayed, there was no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights.".
