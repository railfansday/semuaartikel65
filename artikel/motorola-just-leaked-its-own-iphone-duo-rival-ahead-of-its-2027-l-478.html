Motorola has seemingly confirmed that it's working on a new wide foldable smartphone for 2027, which could take on Apple's iPhone Duo.

Apple's iPhone Duo seems to be the talk of the town right now, with several smartphone brands looking to jump on the wide foldable bandwagon. Samsung has already introduced the Galaxy Z Fold 8, while Oppo is also rumored to be working on something similar. And now, it looks like Motorola is preparing to join the competition with its own wide foldable called the Razr Flex.

Sources indicate that as spotted by Android Authority, Motorola has listed the upcoming Razr Flex 2027 on its Australian support website. The device appears alongside other Razr smartphones in the company's security enhancement section, suggesting that Motorola is indeed working on a new smartphone under the Razr Flex branding.

Interestingly, the listing also includes 2027 in the device's name, which strongly suggests Motorola looks to launch the Razr Flex next year.

Industry observers note that that noted, apart from the name, Motorola's support website doesn't reveal much about the upcoming phone. There are no official images, specifications, or other details about what the Razr Flex will actually look like.

However, previous leaks have already given us a pretty good idea of what to expect. The Razr Flex will reportedly feature a relatively small 4.9-inch cover display alongside a larger 6.9-inch OLED inner screen with up to a 165Hz refresh rate.

Under the hood, the phone could be powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset, paired with 12GB of RAM and a 5,000mAh battery. Similar to the iPhone Duo, Motorola is also expected to offer a dual-camera setup on the back.
