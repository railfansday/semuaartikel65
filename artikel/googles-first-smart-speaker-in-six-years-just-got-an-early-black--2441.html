The best smart speakers are some of Black Friday's most sought-after devices, and the deals are already rolling in, including Google's first audio device since it dropped the Nest name.

The Google Home Speaker, which was launched earlier this summer, is $20 off at Best Buy from $99.99 (now $79.99), and if you're already in the Google smart home space, it's a solid hub for combining all of your devices into one powerful ecosystem.

According to recent reports, when we tested the Google Home Speaker, the listening experience offered more than satisfactory audio quality because of the device's spherical design. Unlike previous Google speakers, its latest inductee optimizes audio output so that it reaches every corner of a room.

According to recent reports, but where the Google Home Speaker stands in its own lane is with built-in Gemini features, which can respond to more complex queries than the previous Google Assistant. Though Google's flagship AI assistant has been rolled out to previous models, this is the first Gemini-native smart home speaker from Google that doesn't just pack a better contextual understanding, but allows you to chain multiple requests together or even express complex commands.

On top of its technical smarts, its woven fabric look gives it a smart yet pretty look, which would look good on any bookcase or coffee table!

After six years, Google finally upgraded its lineup of smart speakers and relaunched it for its fresh era of Gemini. It's equal parts smart home hub and speaker, and can even be paired with your TV to create a surround-sound system.

✅Recommended if: You're looking for an Amazon Echo alternative or want to upgrade from older Nest models. If you're already deep into the Google smart home ecosystem and are fully equipped with Nest security devices, then integrating a Google Home Speaker into your setup will be a piece of cake.

Gemini for Home features are built into the software out of the box too, which, if AI-powered tools are vital to you, is another reason to look into this deal.

❌Skip this deal if: Unlike other smart speakers, the wired connection is built into the device, meaning you'll have to splice it when it eventually breaks or toss the speaker and buy a new one.

Additionally, we found that the speaker can sometimes be inconsistent when responding to voice commands, but it's almost instant most of the time.
