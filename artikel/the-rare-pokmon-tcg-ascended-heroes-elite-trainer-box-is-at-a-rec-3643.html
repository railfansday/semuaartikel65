TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box is on sale for $124.92 at Walmart, down from its $199.95 list price for Black Friday.

Having tracked deals on the Pokémon TCG's Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box since early 2026, we've finally seen the coveted collection break the $125 barrier. Walmart, to our surprise, is dropping the best price we've come across this Black Friday — which is great timing, since it’s been hard to find any for less than $200 on Amazon.

As of Nov. 28, the Pokémon TCG Ascended Heroes Elite Trainer Box is now going for just $124.92 at Walmart. With $75.03 shaved off its $199.95 list price (38% off), you're essentially paying $13.88 per booster before considering the extras you get. That's $17.05 cheaper than the early Black Friday Amazon deal we covered, and $4.53 below its current TCGplayer market price.

Not only is this Ascended Heroes ETB under-market price, but it is also cheaper than any actual listings on the dedicated trading card selling site.

Alongside nine Ascended Heroes booster packs, each ETB comes with a full-art foil N's Zekrom promo card, 65 card sleeves, 40 Energy cards, and assorted accessories.

In a recent development, there are still some exceptionally valuable trading cards to hunt for. The expansion's Special Illustration Rare Mega Gengar ex and Pikachu ex are still commanding market prices of around $500 each on TCGplayer.
