Apple’s smart glasses are among the worst-kept secrets in the tech world. Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman states the firm’s first model, which will lack augmented reality features, will be confirmed at June’s WWDC next year. They likely won’t ship right away, however, with Gurman saying only that “glasses shipments would begin before the end of the year.”.

Key to their success will be the newly revamped Siri AI, which is adding fine-grained control over apps. By adding those features now and letting developers add support on the iPhone first, it’s hoping that its smart glasses will be able to hit the ground running.

The prototypes, dubbed N50, are currently running a pared-down version of visionOS and a fresh processor based on its older smartwatch chips. They also have a MagSafe-like charger Gurman describes as “flat, rectangular” and “roughly the size of a Tic Tac,” which can be used even while still wearing the glasses.

In addition to Apple putting an emphasis on style, privacy, and audio quality, the organization is also hoping that Wi-Fi connectivity will grant them a certain amount of freedom, despite relying on an iPhone to actually execute many tasks:.

Industry observers note that the glasses have Wi-Fi, allowing certain functions outside of third-party apps to continue working when the iPhone isn’t nearby. Owners could theoretically keep listening to audio over Wi-Fi after walking away from their phones, for instance, though the eyewear won’t operate as a fully independent device. Apple is also emphasizing the sound quality, which employees have remarked is industry-leading, with support for phone calls, music playback and FaceTime audio communication.
