You’ve got $50 to spend on a friend or loved one this shopping season, and you’re not sure what to spend it on. First off, that’s actually really generous of you. While this price tier can sometimes be challenging to shop for — caught between affordable accessories and big-ticket items — there’s plenty of gold to be mined for clever shoppers. And we’re here to help.

This guide includes a wide range of cool finds at $50 or less, from mainstream hits like KPop Demon Hunters on Blu-ray and popular tech like the DJI Mic Mini to more eccentric picks like VidaBay’s E Ink fridge magnets and the Werewolf USB-C power adapter. We think you’ll be surprised how far your $50 can stretch.

Sources indicate that if someone on your list collects Game Boy, Game Boy Color, or Game Boy Advance cartridges, the GB Operator might be the perfect gift. The slick, transparent USB-C device makes it easy to play and manage classic titles on a macOS or Windows machine via emulation, with or without achievements.

Recent findings suggest that pricey yet endlessly useful, the Grip Stand is the ultimate phone accessory. The ultra-thin magnetic attachment is a better gift than simpler PopSocket-style holders, especially since it can serve as an ad hoc kickstand. That makes it convenient for both video calls and YouTube.

It has been revealed that espresso in the woods? Yes, please. The GR2’s magical mechanics make it possible, letting you turn 12 ounces of grounds into espresso without power. Its carrying case houses all the accessories needed to get that sweet shot of bean, making real coffee available virtually anywhere.

Fresh Zealand artist Aldous Harding has another eccentric album on her hands with this year’s Train on the Island. The slow-burning LP is gorgeously recorded and delightfully weird, and it feels tailor-made for vinyl, making it an easy pick for Harding fans (or anyone who loves quirky indie jams).

Rechargeable AA batteries are great for the environment and your budget, but not all models are created equally, so we did our own testing. With top-tier capacity, breezy usability, and a compact USB-C charging case, Zepath’s batteries are the best we’ve tried.

It has been revealed that this affordable dongle kit provides an almost magical way to power nearly any DC device from a USB-C charger or portable battery. Program it from a mobile app, connect it to your old gear, then everything from a PC monitor to an original Game Boy can accept USB-C charging.

Hoto’s electric screwdriver is a thoughtful gift for the builder in your life. The compact, easy-to-use tool includes 12 S2 steel bits, a circular LED to illuminate your project, and a soft, yet grippy handle. Three torque settings allow for a variety of jobs, and a long-lasting battery (with USB-C charging) keeps the driver going for hours.

It can be hard to keep in touch with friends, but Big Walk might be the game that gets the gang back together. The cooperative multiplayer title drops players into a wacky world as spherical humanoids who must solve puzzles by chatting and teaming up. You’ll want at least two copies, and sessions support up to 12 players.

According to recent reports, this handy Japanese organizer is a thoughtful gift for folks who prefer a physical way to keep track of everything, from upcoming events and projects to random thoughts and creative ideas. Also available in a rechargeable configuration, the app-controllable button pusher clips onto almost anything with a switch and lasts up to 600 days on a single 3V CR2 battery.

It’s an excellent entry point for folks living in small homes or apartments. Headlined by a 32-bit screwdriver set, it includes two pairs of tweezers, dual spudgers, and more.

That makes it simpler to connect and customize its back paddles than its rivals. The Lite skips a few features, like amiibo support and high-quality rumble, but otherwise provides a solid value for those on a budget.

They’re a bit pricey, but worth it.

The story about three pop stars who band together to save humanity from a demonic boy band is an instant classic, with fantastic music and striking animation.

It feels like magic, but it’s just a really cool application of some modern tech.
