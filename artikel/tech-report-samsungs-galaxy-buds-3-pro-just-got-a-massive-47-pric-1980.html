Black Friday is almost upon us, and if you're looking to snag a great deal ahead of the main event without spending too much on a pair of premium wireless earbuds, Samsung's Galaxy Buds 3 Pro are worth checking out.

Recent findings suggest that they're presently down to just $100 on Amazon, a massive 47% discount from their usual $190 price. In fact, this is the lowest price we've seen for Samsung's flagship earbuds yet, making them an even more tempting buy ahead of Black Friday.

Sources indicate that samsung's Galaxy Buds 3 Pro are down to just $100 on Amazon, a massive 47% discount from their usual $190 price. These premium wireless earbuds offer excellent sound quality, adaptive ANC, wireless charging, and up to 26 hours of battery life with the case.

✅ Recommended if: You own a Samsung Galaxy phone, want premium wireless earbuds with ANC and excellent sound quality, or are looking for flagship features without spending more than $100.

❌ Skip this deal if: You use an iPhone, want the longest possible battery life, or prefer earbuds that offer the same features regardless of which Android phone you use.

Samsung may have newer earbuds in its lineup now, but the Galaxy Buds 3 Pro are still a fantastic pair of wireless earbuds, especially if you're using a Samsung Galaxy phone. They offer pretty much everything you'd expect from premium earbuds, including great ANC, excellent sound quality, wireless charging, and plenty of Galaxy AI features that make them worth it even today.

Industry observers note that samsung moved away from its bean-shaped earbuds design to a stem-style design with the Galaxy Buds 3 Pro, and many people who've used these earbuds have praised their fit and comfort. That's also partly why the ANC works so well, as a good seal helps block out more background noise.

Recent findings suggest that but one of the biggest reasons to get these earbuds is that they pack pretty much everything you'd expect from a flagship pair without the flagship price tag. They originally rolled out for around $250, and even though they're no longer the latest model, they're still among the best wireless earbuds you can get right now.

Recent findings suggest that battery life is pretty decent as well. Samsung claims up to six hours of listening with ANC enabled, or up to 26 hours with the charging case. You also get an IP57 rating for the earbuds, which is a nice bonus if you plan to use them during workouts or while traveling.

It has been revealed that of course, you'll get the most out of these earbuds if you own a Samsung Galaxy phone. Galaxy AI features and Samsung's higher-quality audio codec work best with a Galaxy phone. That mentioned, you can still use the earbuds with other Android phones, though some features won't be available.

On the whole, if you've been waiting for a good deal on a pair of premium wireless earbuds, I wouldn't overlook these. At their usual $190 price, there are plenty of alternatives worth considering, but at just $100, you're getting flagship-level features for almost half the price.
