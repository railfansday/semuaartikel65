Instagram is beginning to roll out a “content protection” feature that lets creators see and review copies of their videos posted to the platform.

It has been revealed that “For eligible creators who enroll, content protection is a tool that automatically looks out for visual matches of your original videos, alerts you when we find a match across Instagram and Facebook, and lets you decide what happens next,” Meta says in a blog post. Meta launched a similar tool for Facebook last year to help consumers “safeguard” their Reels and “maintain control” over their creative work.

When an Instagram creator is notified of a matched video, they can see how many views the match has and decide if they want to track the video, hide it from everyone on Instagram and Facebook, add a credit link, or release their claim on the match.

Sources indicate that after a creator enrolls, every “original video” they post on Instagram is protected, and creators can retroactively protect older videos manually. Creators can also put accounts on an allow list that lets them use their videos.

In addition, Instagram will start flagging AI-generated videos that “match the spoken words” in a creator’s original video but have visuals replaced with AI. Initially, Instagram will test this with a “small group of creators” in “the coming weeks.” The platform states it’s exploring ways to limit the reach of content and profiles that “use AI tools to post this type of content repeatedly.”.
