📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that elon Musk compared Jensen Huang's departure from Nvidia to kicking Michael Jordan off a team on a winning streak, while also expressing regret over how much he spent on the U.S. midterms.

Sources indicate that "You've got a winning streak ... and then you kick Michael Jordan off your team," Musk told Andrew Ross Sorkin at The Upcoming York Times' DealBook Summit on Wednesday. He added, "Doesn't seem like a smart decision to me.".

Huang resigned last week following pressure after disclosing an affair with a woman who reportedly was suspected of being a Chinese spy, alongside reports of relationships with employees. He is expected to testify before the U.S. Congress next month around national-security and export-control issues.

The SpaceX and Tesla chief executive, who previously mentioned it would be an "honor" to hire the Nvidia co-founder, added that no one could really replace Huang.

Musk noted he had heard similar views about Huang's departure from Nvidia, including from President Donald Trump.

"I think the president has voiced publicly that he thinks Jensen should have stayed as CEO, and everyone I know thinks the same ... this is, at the end of the day, the Nvidia board decision," he noted, adding, "We're not on the board, right?".

It has been revealed that musk and Huang have known each other for years through Nvidia's work with Tesla and OpenAI. Tesla has been a major Nvidia customer, while Huang has repeatedly praised Musk's AI strategy, including Tesla's self-driving work, xAI and the Optimus robot.

Asked about the midterms, Musk mentioned he felt "pretty foolish" about spending at least $474 million with little to show for it, though he added that "maybe it would have been much worse without that.".

Musk stated his goal had been to prevent the presidency, House and Senate from all being controlled by one party. "If I could rewind the clock, I would have spent less money," he said.

According to recent reports, in the same interview, he also appeared to distance himself from the MAGA movement. "I'm not MAGA," he remarked, adding that he wasn't sure what the term means.

On a possible SpaceX-Tesla merger, Musk said areas of overlap now exist that "didn't exist before," pointing to AI, robotics and the AI software and chip-design teams at both companies.

He said any deal would require shareholder approval and "may or may not succeed." Musk added that he could not push through a transaction that was bad for shareholders because he would "obviously get sued.".

Asked whether he should receive his Tesla pay award if a merger were completed, Musk said he should not be rewarded "just for doing a merger" and should receive stock-option awards only if Tesla meets its performance targets.

Musk also said SpaceX rents computing capacity to Anthropic under short-term agreements that either side can terminate, adding that the Claude AI chatbot-maker had expressed interest in an additional 10 gigawatts.

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share on Wednesday, raising $81.3 billion in what it said was the largest public offering on record, surpassing SpaceX.

Asked how he feels a year after describing his mind as "a storm," Musk said he feels "relatively at peace.".
