After sitting out Black Friday completely last year, Peacock has come crawling back.

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free right now with T-Mobile.

Recent findings suggest that what I like to see in this Black Friday streaming deal is the option to lock in a full year at the discounted rate. Most deals that drop at this time of year offer a reduced rate for 60 or 90 days, and then you're asked to pay the full price to continue. Not Peacock. They are letting you lock in 12 months of streaming for just $29.99.

Peacock is probably the best spot to binge NBC and Bravo shows like The Office, Parks and Recreation, Yellowstone, and The Real Housewives. What else are you supposed to do at this time of year? Connect with your family? No, thank you. I think I'll rewatch The Office again.

I should note that Peacock Select is a stripped-down tier. It includes ads and completely excludes live sports, Universal movies, and Peacock Originals. If you shop the Peacock Select deal thinking you’ll watch Premier League matches or Thanksgiving NFL games, you will be disappointed. Don't come crying to me when you hit that upgrade wall, because I warned you.

In a recent development, also note that neither discounted blueprint is ad-free. If ad-free streaming is a non-negotiable for you, the Paramount+ Black Friday deal has your name written all over it.
