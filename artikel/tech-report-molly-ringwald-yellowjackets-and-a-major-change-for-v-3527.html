You might have clocked it in the Season 4 premiere, but with episode 2 it's very clear: Molly Ringwald has joined Yellowjackets.

The '80s icon who fronted movies like The Breakfast Club, Sixteen Candles, and Pretty in Pink takes over the role of Van's (Liv Hewson) mom, Vicky Palmer, from Deborah Vancelette. And this signifies a major change in Van's life, post-rescue.

Recent findings suggest that in the Season 1 pilot, Vicky (Vancelette) is presented as a bad mom. In a montage of the Yellowjackets readying for that fateful flight, Van walks through a dark, cluttered house to find her mom passed out on the couch, next to a nearly empty alcohol bottle. She tries to wake her, presumably because she needs a ride to the plane. But calling to her and shaking her isn't enough to rouse Vicky. So, Van slaps her, and she does so with a world-weariness that suggests this isn't an isolated incident.

In Season 4, episode 2, Vicky (Ringwald) is transformed. The living room is sunny and tidy upon Van's return, with a wall of VHS tapes carefully preserved. Later, Vicky shows she cares by assuring Van she taped every episode of The X-Files. To Tai (Jasmin Savoy Brown), she'll say of Vicky, "My mom is completely different.".

No shade to Vancelette, who got little to do in the role of Vicky. But if any Yellowjacket was going to get a mom played by a movie idol of the '80s, it ought to be Van. Her love of pop culture is a defining characteristic, as she'd quote and allude to movies and highlights to brandish her wit and wisdom. Of course, if her mom's going to get a glow-up, she's going to become the "princess" from The Breakfast Club. Where other mothers wear furrowed brows before their recovered kids (or don't even show up, as is the case for poor Nat (Sophie Thatcher)), Van's mom is radiant with joy to bring her girl home for a fresh start. When Van picks up the home phone to listen for a dial tone, Vicky knows what's up, asking if she's checking to see the phone's connected in case someone calls. This shows that Van's mom knows she's up on her bills, and she knows her daughter.

More than that, Vicky gives Van the kind of heart-to-heart she's probably only ever seen in the movies before. "I know that I haven't been someone that you can count on. And that kills me," she confesses on the couch — this time sitting up, having put together a sprawling feast of snack foods for them to enjoy. "But this last year and a half has had a profound effect on me. And I think that it has for you too, and I would really love to understand how.".

But, first, a dentist appointment, because — much to Van's surprise — they have dental insurance now.

Deadline reported on the recasting back in February. Now, seeing Ringwald in action in the world of Yellowjackets, we can't wait to see what comes next.
