Meta's Muse AI comes to life on Wear OS, and it's even on the Apple Watch

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you're all about Meta's Muse AI, you might want to grab your Wear OS watch. The company announced that its cute AI assistant is rolling out.

It has been revealed that on X this week, the Muse account announced that the AI is now available on Google and Apple wearables. The post states, "Works around the clock, and now on your wrist." Meta's AI now supports Wear OS and the Apple Watch. Consumers will find the cute, cuddly-looking creature that Meta revealed in a previous Muse AI announcement.

We can infer much of Muse AI's capabilities through this short post on X. The AI will almost certainly help users with daily tasks and queries, while also bringing certain capabilities to apps, messages, and the like.

Funnily enough, this seems like another expansion for Meta's Muse AI. If you remember, this cute creation debuted with the "Muse Charm" in September. The Muse Charm looks like a Tamagotchi, and the creation only adds to that comparison. Meta stated the charm was designed to keep customers connected with Muse via 5G. The Muse Charm features a 2-inch OLED touchscreen, a camera, a microphone, and speakers.

During its reveal, Meta's CEO, Mark Zuckerberg, said, "We’ve packed a lot of technology with this little guy. So, if you’re not wearing glasses, this is going to be by far the fastest way to talk to your Muse and to show it what’s going on around you." What's more, the included camera gives the Muse Charm some real-world practicality. Consumers can ask it about what they see, such as a sign in another language.

The Muse Charm was also said to encroach on smartwatch pricing (which sounds a little weird following this Wear OS announcement).

The Muse Charm, or rather Muse AI in general, is a part of Meta's ambitions for a "people first" era.

Meta revealed Muse Spark earlier this year as its newest LLM, marking a latest direction. The company claims the AI is "small and fast by design, yet capable enough to reason through complex questions." Muse Spark uses multiple AI agents to tackle tasks accurately and rapidly.
