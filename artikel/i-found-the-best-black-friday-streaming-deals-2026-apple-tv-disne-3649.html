I Found the Best Black Friday Streaming Deals (2026): Apple TV, Disney, HBO Max

💡 Key Takeaways
  • In a recent development, most of the biggest streaming services are offering Black Friday deals this year, as they do every year.
  • Either way, here are the best Black Friday streaming deals I found.
  • Updated November 28, 2026: I’ve added HBO Max's Black Friday deal to the list and updated the Disney Plus Hulu bundle deal.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, most of the biggest streaming services are offering Black Friday deals this year, as they do every year. Nearly all of these deals are for fresh and former customers only. Easy enough if you've canceled a subscription recently and want it back, but a little trickier if you aren't exactly a “new” customer. The ol' second (or third … or fourth) email address sign-up trick still works sometimes, but duplicated credit cards can sometimes flag an error.

Either way, here are the best Black Friday streaming deals I found. I'll patch this list periodically through Cyber Monday as things change. And if you're looking for more deals, check out our guides to the Absolute Best Black Friday Deals and Best Black Friday TV Deals.

Updated November 28, 2026: I’ve added HBO Max's Black Friday deal to the list and updated the Disney Plus Hulu bundle deal.

Deal Until December 1: $6/month for six months, billed monthly.

Look, this list is alphabetical, but that works in my favor because I get to put Apple TV first. It's all killer, no filler. Widow's Bay, Pluribus, Ted Lasso, Slow Horses, Severance, Shrinking, Silo, and other series that begin with the letter S—Apple TV just has the most memorable shows. And six months is just enough time to binge most (if not all) of them. (See WIRED's Guide to the Best Apple TV+ shows.).

Deal Until December 1: $4/month for six months, billed monthly.

I may have fallen off the anime train, but I do find myself subscribing to Crunchyroll for at least a month every year, just to catch up on a series or two. Either way, this is a great discount if you've been meaning to try out or catch up on some anime.

Current offer: $5/month for 12 months, billed monthly.

This is a solid deal on the ad-supported tier of Disney and Hulu, and a great excuse to catch up on all things Marvel and Star Wars, plus great series like Adults, The Bear, and Abbott Elementary. (See WIRED's guide to the best highlights on Disney+ and Hulu.).

Also on sale is the Premium version, which means no ad breaks and offline downloads, but it's at a slightly less enticing 31 percent off ($15 a month, down from $22 per month).

Current offer: $5.50/month for 12 months, billed monthly.

Sure, Apple TV may have taken up the prestige series crown, but HBO Max is no slough, with a wide breadth of quality demonstrates across genres. We wrote about the best 50 shows on HBO Max earlier this year, and it's full of heavy hitters worth scooping up this discount to watch. Just keep in mind that this offer is only for the Basic With Ads monthly plan, which means HD video resolution and no downloads for offline viewing.

Deal Until Dec. 1: $12/month for four months, billed monthly (locked in).

In a recent development, watching all your local sports team’s games online in 2026 is an M.C. Escher-ian nightmare that somehow requires four different subscriptions and possibly a live-TV add-on package. Any deal that can soften that blow is a good thing.

Deal Until December 2: $3/month for two months.

This is more of a trial subscription than a bona fide deal, but hey, let's all take whatever savings we can get. After all, two months might be all you need to binge the new Dexter: Resurrection or watch Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender.

Regular Price: $70/year billed annually.

Black Friday Deal: $12/year billed annually.

In a recent development, this is a steep discount for a whole year of Starz streaming. Though keep in mind you're paying for the full year up front ($12), and you better set a calendar reminder 11 months from now if you don't want to end up paying $70 for Starz next year.

Deal Until December 2: $50/month for six months.

If you're really trying to watch all the sports games (plus recreate that classic TV-package feel with local channels), YouTube TV's Sports+News package is a good option. You can get about $20 off each month's rate for six months with this deal.
