iOS 27 could be messing up your CarPlay experience with Google Maps and Waze

Some navigation apps are broken in CarPlay, and iOS 27 might be to blame.

In a recent development, apple's CarPlay has gained some interesting latest features since iOS 27 released, including the ability to watch videos while parked. But the update has also been giving some drivers headaches with third-party navigation apps. When using Google Maps or Waze, guidance banners end up overlapping the map on the screen, making turn-by-turn navigation difficult or nearly impossible.

You're not alone if this has been happening to your car — there are lots of complaints from drivers all over Reddit. Although many are blaming app developers, it actually seems to be a bug caused by iOS 27.

Industry observers note that with iOS 27, Apple has redesigned the template that third-party navigation apps use in CarPlay. Unlike on iOS itself, apps such as Google Maps and Waze cannot design their own interface from scratch for CarPlay. Instead, the apps send the information to iOS, which renders the next turn and ETA cards in the car. In theory, the apps should move the center of the map to the side when these banners appear, but that's what seems to be failing after the iOS 27 patch.

A user in this r/CarPlay thread described the bug after installing iOS 27 on their phone — CarPlay simply stopped reporting the safe area to third-party apps. As a result, navigation banners appear on top of the map, preventing you from seeing what's on the screen. A developer described the same behavior on Apple's Developer Forums, and an Apple engineer replied that it's a known glitch in iOS 27. The bug doesn't affect Apple Maps because Apple's native apps have their own interface in CarPlay.

The problem also gets worse depending on the size of your car's screen. Wider screens provide more space for the map, so even with the cards overlapping, you can still see navigation clearly. However, compact multimedia units are more affected by this matter.

Recent findings suggest that since the bug appears to be caused by iOS and not apps, updating Google Maps or Waze probably won't help — nor will reinstalling the apps or re-pairing your car. Those solutions only work when the apps aren't showing up in CarPlay at all, so if that's the case, go to Settings > General > CarPlay on your iPhone. Choose your car from the list, tap Customize and make sure the apps you want are enabled; otherwise, they won't appear on your car's display.

That said, there is one CarPlay setting worth changing. Open the Settings app on your CarPlay screen, then go to Display and turn off Smart Display Zoom. It doesn't seem to work for all vehicles, but some folks have said that changing this setting made the banners on the screen smaller. It's not a permanent fix, but it should help until Apple fixes the bug — which still seems to be present in the first iOS 27.3 beta available to developers.

Recent findings suggest that since some Apple engineers have already publicly acknowledged the bug on the organization's discussion forums, it's likely that a fix isn't too far off. Until then, it's best to rely on voice guidance for navigation, or even use your phone's screen instead of CarPlay. Or give Apple Maps a try in the meantime — it might be better than you expect.
