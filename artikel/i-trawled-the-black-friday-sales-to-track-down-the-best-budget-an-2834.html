One of the best things about the Android ecosystem is the number of options available to more cost-conscious buyers, and no time is this more evident than Black Friday. With this year's sale event in full swing this week, we're seeing some incredible discounts on a wide range of phones.

Motorola is right now dominating the budget end of the spectrum in the US, with the prices of some of its most compelling entry-level models slashed. You can find the Motorola Moto G Play on sale for under $100, which is absolutely crazy value. Obviously, a phone this cheap has plenty of limitations (the small amount of RAM and low storage will be sticking points for most customers), but the fact that you can find a fully-fledged smartphone for under 100 bucks in 2026 is worth celebrating, and it's a good choice if you have very basic needs or are shopping for a young child or older relative.

According to recent reports, the Motorola Moto G Stylus is also an absolute steal at $200 off, too. It's one of the few modern phone models that comes with its own built-in stylus and is very competitive at this price. If you're willing to pay a little more, you can also find discounts on models from TCL, Samsung, and Nothing, so without further ado, let's dive into the best.

According to recent reports, your options are quite limited if you want a fully fledged smartphone for under $100 this Black Friday, but this model from Motorola is a good choice if you have very basic needs. Expect plenty of limitations given the rock-bottom price, including a modest chipset, 4GB of RAM, just 64GB of storage, and basic cameras. The standout feature is the 90Hz screen, just 720p, but it should feel snappy thanks to the higher-than-usual refresh rate.

You also get 128GB of storage. This is a solid deal, but the Moto G Stylus below is a better value pick for the same money, given its higher RRP.

This mid-ranger from Motorola is a bargain at half price and is one of the few affordable options that includes a built-in stylus. It's got 8GB of RAM, a decent chipset, 128GB of storage, and a high-refresh-rate 120Hz display. This would be my personal pick under $200.

Part smartphone and part e-reader, the NXTPAPER 70 Pro boasts a unique 120Hz matte display with an in-built paper mode designed to reduce eye strain when reading. It's perfect for catching up with books or comics on the go, and has decent all-round specs for the price, including 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. Perfect if you want something a little different.

It has been revealed that this Samsung mid-ranger is a good alternative if you have more basic usage requirements and don't want to splash out on the higher-spec Galaxy 26 FE (also discounted, as you'll see later on). It's got strong battery life with a 5,000 mAh cell and runs an octa-core SoC that's perfectly capable for daily tasks and light mobile gaming. Samsung also promises security updates until 2032 in some regions - which is a significant plus.

Industry observers note that if you want something more stylish than your usual fare, then Motorola has you covered with its fashion-focused Edge. Industry observers note that the Galaxy S26 FE is a great way into the Samsung ecosystem if you don't want to empty your wallet on a high-end phone. How much would you pay for Nothing? The Snapdragon 8s Gen4 chipset delivers solid performance in a wide range of apps and games, and the fact that storage starts at 256GB rather than the usual 128GB is a nice bonus. This is also a newly introduced lowest-ever price at Amazon, and it would be my model of choice from this under-$500 lineup.
