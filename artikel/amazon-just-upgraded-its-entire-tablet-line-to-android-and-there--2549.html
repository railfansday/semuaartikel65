📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Amazon recently revamped its entire tablet line, ditching the Fire name and operating system in favor of a smattering of proper Android tablets. The Amazon Alexa Tablet family covers all form factors and usage needs, ranging from the affordable and compact Amazon Alexa Tablet 8 all the way to the high-end Amazon Alexa Tablet 12 Pro.

It has been revealed that the best part? They're all heavily discounted for Black Friday. The entry-level Alexa Tablet 8 now costs just $149.99 (down from $229.99), with a portable 8.7-inch form factor, 4GB of RAM, and 64GB of on-board storage.

If you want something a little more premium, then there's the Amazon Alexa Tablet 11 at just $219.99 (was $329.99), which upgrades the display to an 11-inch 2.5K panel and boosts the RAM to 8GB for smoother multitasking performance.

Finally, there's the top-of-the-line Alexa Tablet 12 Pro on sale for $449.99 (was $549.99), with its 12-inch 2.8K display boasting an innovative Nanomatte coating, which dramatically cuts down glare. This is on top of a 120Hz refresh rate, 8GB of RAM, and 12GB of storage.

All three models run Android, with full support for all of your favorite Google Play Store apps. Read on for everything you need to know.

This entry-level tablet is a great choice for families or those after something more compact. It's powered by an 8-core chip and 4GB of RAM, with an 8.7-inch HD screen. Despite the low price, its case is constructed with aluminum rather than plastic.

The Amazon Alexa Tablet 11 upgrades the screen to a larger and higher-resolution 11-inch 2.5K panel with a 90Hz refresh rate, which is better suited for media consumption. At just over $200, it's fantastic value if you're seeking an all-round media machine.

Recent findings suggest that this is Amazon's highest-end Android tablet, geared towards powerful multitasking performance. The 2.8K Nanomatte screen cuts down on glare, while 128GB of storage offers plenty of room for downloaded apps.

Industry observers note that ✅Recommended if: The Amazon Alexa Tablet line is worthwhile if you're searching for some good-value Android tablets. I can't think of any model from a major brand that delivers more bang for your buck than the affordable Alexa Tablet 8, while the mid-range Alexa Tablet 11 offers plenty of meaningful screen upgrades for an excellent viewing experience. The Alexa Tablet 12 Pro, meanwhile, delivers strong performance and a spacious screen for more serious productivity.

It has been revealed that ❌Skip this deal if: You're not already somewhat invested in the Amazon ecosystem. Although the Android operating system of the Amazon Alexa Tablet line is a lot more open than FireOS ever was, with full support for Google Play out of the box, it still comes pre-loaded with Amazon apps like Prime Video, Kindle, and Amazon Luna. There are better options out there if you are already all-in on a different ecosystem.
