Meta Horizon+ is rumored to be changing its name to Meta VR+ and may start including movies in addition to games with the subscription price.

In a recent development, meta’s quest (ahem) to distance itself from the Horizon era is in full swing, and the latest rumor indicates it’s time for the organization’s Game Pass-like subscription to change its brand name. VR leaker Nori posted a screenshot on X showing a handful of 3D movies coming to the Meta VR Plus service. Considering there is no such Meta VR Plus service currently, the obvious conclusion is that Meta Horizon+ is being renamed once again.

Meta Horizon+ currently includes over 100 games with its $7.99 per month subscription and is similar to Microsoft’s Xbox Game Pass service in many ways. Unironically, Meta Horizon+ also includes the basic Xbox Game Pass tier with over 50 Xbox games playable via the Game Pass cloud. Meta offers a 3-month trial with every new Meta Quest purchase.

Sources indicate that the new Meta VR+ service is purported to include 3D movies as well, although we don’t yet know if this will be included in the regular $7.99 per month rate, or if Meta will be introducing additional content tiers, as services like Netflix, Disney+, Xbox Game Pass, and others do. Based on the screenshot, these will be substantial-name movies and are likely positioned to enhance the value of the upcoming $1,300 Meta VR Glasses, although all the same content will work on existing Meta Quest 3 units.

Recent findings suggest that meta has been slowly distancing itself from the “Horizon” name ever since the beginning of this year, when its VR division got a big reset. In a February blog post, Samantha Ryan, the VP of Content at Meta’s Reality Labs, spoke about the changes to the company’s strategy, including the loss of most in-house VR gaming studios. Since then, several prominent VR developers from Meta have gone on to create independent studios, including the super-popular Supernatural exercise app.

Meta then decoupled the unpopular Horizon Metaverse from its headsets, moving it to mobile-only, and has been removing the “Horizon” name from just about everything since then. Meta Horizon+ looks to be the latest big change, as the company moves toward “Meta VR” branding, which is clearer and separates its VR division from mobile and smart glasses efforts.
