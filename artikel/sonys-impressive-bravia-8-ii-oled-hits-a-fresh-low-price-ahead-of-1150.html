It has been revealed that black Friday is a great time to save on a upcoming OLED television for your winter movie nights. Though, it turns out that you can already get a great deal on the 65-inch Sony Bravia 8 II, one of our favorite OLED televisions. It’s currently discounted by $1,100 at various retailers, including Amazon, Best Buy, and directly from Sony. That discount brings the price under $2,200, the lowest we’ve seen. You can also save $800 on the 55-inch model, which is typically $2,599.99 (Amazon, Best Buy, Sony), but now costs just under $1,800.

In a recent development, the Bravia 8 II was chosen as the “king of TV” by a panel of judges (including The Verge’s own Nilay Patel) in Value Electronics’ 2025 shootout. The OLED TV has excellent color accuracy, a bright panel, and a 120Hz refresh rate.

In a recent development, my colleague John Higgins praised the Bravia 8 II for its excellent image quality and engaging picture, though those positive traits are consistent with almost all OLED TVs. What sets this one apart is its impressive image processing, the bold colors from its quantum dot-equipped OLED panel, and that it looks great right out of the box (although he recommends using Professional mode). The screen was awarded the top spot at 2025’s Value Electronics TV Shootout, beating out similar offerings from competitors like Panasonic, LG, and Samsung.

For gaming, the panel can reach up to 120Hz at 4K, with both variable refresh rate and automatic low-latency mode to ensure that your content looks and feels good to play. The connectivity includes two HDMI 2.1 (one with eARC), and two HDMI 2.0 ports, giving you plenty of inputs for consoles and a Blu-ray player, among other gadgets. We spotted some color fringing of certain colors in dark scenes, and lamented the lack of a but it’s an otherwise excellent screen we can easily recommend.
