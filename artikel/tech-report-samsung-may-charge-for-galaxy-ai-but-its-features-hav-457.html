Tech Report: Samsung may charge for Galaxy AI, but its features have a value problem

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Samsung may soon be charging for Galaxy AI, potentially ending the uncertainty surrounding how long its artificial intelligence tools will remain free.

A new leak, courtesy of SammyGuru, reveals an interface for Galaxy AI Premium, a monthly strategy that adds more AI capabilities. It appears Samsung is working on it, but existing features will likely be spared from the paywall.

According to the leak, customers will have to pay $4.99 a month or $47.99 annually to get access to the premium package.

The existing tools, including Now Brief, Browsing Assist, Creative Studio, and AI-powered Samsung Keyboard features, are likely to remain free.

Industry observers note that the latest developments are not shocking. The Korean tech giant had previously said Galaxy AI features would remain free through the end of 2025, leaving the door open to future charges. The firm later said, however, it had no plans to roll out paid features.

Samsung has not yet confirmed pricing, feature lineup, or if the paid subscription is coming soon or when.

If the plan goes official, Galaxy owners won’t necessarily lose the AI tools they’ve been using already. But Samsung will have to convince customers that $5 a month is a worthwhile price to pay for its new features.

Sources indicate that samsung’s got a potentially bigger problem than subscription fatigue: some Galaxy owners aren’t sold on its AI tools being worth paying for in the first place. Comments in Reddit’s r/Android community indicate users don’t understand the need for another subscription when services such as Gemini and ChatGPT already provide more capable alternatives. One commenter even noted that some Galaxy S25 Ultra owners aren’t even aware of the AI features their phones have.

According to recent reports, others stated they'd pay if Samsung could deliver a more unified, reliable experience. That puts the pressure on Samsung to prove that Galaxy AI Premium offers something genuinely useful, rather than just putting a price tag on features that many owners rarely touch.

It's stated that the Galaxy S27 series could be the initial supported lineup, but it could come more broadly later.
