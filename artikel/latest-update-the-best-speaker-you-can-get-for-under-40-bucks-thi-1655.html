In a recent development, A small speaker doesn't have to mean small sound, and audio maker JBL is the king of squeezing a lot of volume out of a tiny package. The JBL Go 5 is one of the most compact models in its flagship lineup, but still brings plenty to the table.

It's the speaker that I personally rely on whenever I need to bring a super compact model with me on holiday or out and about, and now it could be yours for just $39.95 (down from $54.95) in the Best Buy Black Friday sale.

I've been browsing the early Black Friday sales extensively over the last few days, and for my money, this is by far the best speaker you can get for under 40 bucks in the sale. The sound is excellent given its small size; it's extremely durable thanks to a waterproof cloth and rubber construction, and it's even got a cool light feature with two bright bars that flash as your tunes play.

It also comes in a wide range of colors, from basic white and black to more exciting purple and baby blue, or even a cool camo (all of them have the same discount, so you can choose your favorite).

At this price, I would even consider getting two, as you can pair them up using the companion app for full stereo sound.
