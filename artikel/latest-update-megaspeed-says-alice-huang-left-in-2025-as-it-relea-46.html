Megaspeed told CNBC Sunday that Alice Huang left the company in August 2025 and said it has launched an internal probe into media reports concerning its business and supply chain.

The Singapore-based cloud computing company has faced heightened scrutiny since former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang said he had been unfaithful to his wife following reports linking him to former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang.

In a recent development, U.S. intelligence officials believe Alice Huang is a Chinese intelligence asset, according to The New York Times.

Recent findings suggest that jensen Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia on Nov. 12 amid fallout from reports about his extramarital relationships, one of which was reportedly a direct report.

"Ms. Alice Huang officially left Megaspeed effective August 30, 2025 and currently has no role with the Manufacturer," Megaspeed said in an emailed statement shared with CNBC. "Megaspeed does not comment on Ms. Huang's personal and private matters," it added.

Megaspeed describes itself as a "core business partner" of Nvidia's computing power cloud business in the region, allowing customers to rent computing power from Nvidia GPUs, or graphics processing units — specialized chips designed to do many calculations at the same time.

The tech giant, which has offices in Singapore and Indonesia, has previously faced media and government scrutiny over whether chips supplied through its business were redirected to customers in China.

Megaspeed said Sunday that media reports about its business, supply chain and global operations are not supported by verifiable evidence and it has retained external legal counsel to conduct a "thorough internal investigation.".

"Megaspeed designs and maintains its global operational and supply-chain control frameworks to comply with all applicable laws and regulatory regimes, including United States export control requirements and local jurisdiction rules in every market where we operate," it said.

The firm added that it will review the reporting and communicate further with stakeholders as appropriate.
