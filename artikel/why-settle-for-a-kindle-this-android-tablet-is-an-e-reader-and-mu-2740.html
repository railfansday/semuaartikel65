Why settle for a Kindle? This Android tablet is an e-reader and much more for under $200

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that if you've been thinking about buying a Kindle or another e-reader, you might want to take a look at this Android tablet before spending your money. The TCL NXTPAPER 11 Plus gives you a paper-like display that's designed to be easier on your eyes, but unlike a traditional Kindle, it can also do pretty much everything you'd expect from a regular Android tablet. And the best part? It's now available for less than $200.

The TCL NXTPAPER 11 Plus combines a paper-like display with the flexibility of a full Android tablet. It features an 11.5-inch 2.2K 120Hz screen, three display modes, 256GB of storage, and an 8,000mAh battery with 33W fast charging.

✅ Recommended if: You want a paper-like display for reading, prefer a larger screen for books and PDFs, or want an affordable Android tablet that handles streaming, browsing, and everyday tasks.

❌ Skip this deal if: You want the weeks-long battery life of a dedicated Kindle, prefer a smaller e-reader, or need a powerful tablet for demanding games and heavy multitasking.

According to recent reports, TCL has been making NXTPAPER devices for a while now, and the NXTPAPER 11 Plus is one of the more interesting tablets in its lineup. Unlike other NXTPAPER tablets, this tablet lets you switch between three different modes, including Regular Mode, Color Paper Mode, and Ink Paper Mode, simply by pressing the dedicated NXTPAPER Key on the tablet.

You can use the regular mode when watching videos or browsing the web, switch to Color Paper Mode for a more comfortable reading experience, or use Ink Paper Mode for a more traditional black-and-white e-reader feel.

Speaking of display, the NXTPAPER 11 Plus comes with a 11.5-inch display with a 2.2K resolution and a smooth 120Hz refresh rate. That noted, the best part of this tablet is probably its design.

Industry observers note that it is only 0.26 inches thin and weighs around 1.08 pounds. That makes it easy to toss into your backpack before a flight or carry around throughout the day without adding much weight. TCL has also given it a metal body, so you're not getting a cheap-feeling plastic tablet despite the affordable price.

Battery life shouldn't be much of a concern either. The NXTPAPER 11 Plus packs a sizable 8,000mAh battery, which should be plenty for long reading sessions, watching movies, or getting through a day of regular use. And when you do need to charge it, there's support for up to 33W wired fast charging.

According to recent reports, of course, there are a few compromises at this price. The tablet is powered by the MediaTek Helio G100 chipset, which isn't exactly a flagship processor. It's perfectly reasonable for everyday tasks like reading, browsing, and streaming, but don't expect the same performance you'd get from a more flagship Samsung Galaxy tablet or iPad.

That mentioned, if you're looking for a relatively inexpensive tablet this Black Friday that also doubles as an e-reader, this is probably one of the best deals you can get right now.
