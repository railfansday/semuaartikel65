Samsung may have already rolled out the Galaxy S26 series, but you don't necessarily need to buy one of its latest smartphones to get a flagship experience.

Industry observers note that in fact, one of Samsung's most underrated smartphones from last year, the Galaxy S25 Plus, is now down to just $684, a massive 32% discount from its usual $1,000 price. And at this price, it's the only Samsung phone I'd buy this Black Friday.

According to recent reports, the Samsung Galaxy S25 Plus offers much of what you'd expect from a flagship smartphone, including a 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, 12GB of RAM, 256GB of storage, and a sizable 4,900mAh battery. It's now available for just $684, saving you $316 ahead of Black Friday.

✅Recommended if: You want a massive-screen Samsung flagship with excellent performance, solid battery life, versatile cameras, and years of software support without spending $1,000.

❌Skip this deal if: You prefer compact smartphones, want the S Pen and more advanced zoom cameras of the Ultra model, or need the absolute latest hardware.

The Galaxy S25 Plus often gets overlooked in Samsung's flagship lineup, largely because most people either go for the more affordable base model or spend a little extra to get the Ultra. But the Galaxy S25 Plus strikes a pretty good balance between the two, especially at its discounted price.

In a recent development, for starters, you get a big 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with an adaptive 120Hz refresh rate. Samsung arguably makes some of the best smartphone displays on the market, and the Galaxy S25 Plus is no exception. The phone has also already been updated to Android 17 with One UI 9, so you're getting much of the same software experience as Samsung's newer flagships.

Recent findings suggest that and while the Galaxy S25 Plus may no longer feature Qualcomm's latest chipset, the Snapdragon 8 Elite is still a very capable processor, even in 2026. Paired with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of onboard storage, the phone should have no trouble handling pretty much anything you throw at it.

You're also getting a pretty versatile camera setup on the back. The Galaxy S25 Plus features a 50MP primary camera, a 10MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom, and a 12MP ultrawide sensor. Sure, you're missing out on some of the more advanced camera hardware of the Ultra model, but for most people, this setup should be more than enough.

One of the best things about the Galaxy S25 Plus is its sizable 4,900mAh battery, which is still among the largest you'll find on a Samsung phone. It also supports the same 45W wired charging as the Galaxy S25 Ultra, along with 15W Qi2-ready wireless charging with a compatible magnetic case.
