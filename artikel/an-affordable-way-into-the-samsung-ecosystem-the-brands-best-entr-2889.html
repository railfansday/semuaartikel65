📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that you might associate Samsung with its high-end flagships, but the brand also makes plenty of good hardware aimed at more budget-conscious buyers. One such model is the Samsung Galaxy Tab A11+, a popular and relatively affordable 11-inch tablet that's on sale for under $200 at Amazon right now, despite specs that punch above that price point and reflect its usual going rate of around $300.

Industry observers note that the 11-inch display comes in at a 1920x1200 resolution, which is perfect for streaming, and a 90Hz refresh rate for smoother scrolling. The octa-core MediaTek MT8775 is by no means the fastest chip around, but it is sufficient for day-to-day tasks like media consumption, web browsing, emailing, and some casual gaming. It's paired with 6GB of RAM too, so you can have a few tabs open at the same time without losing your progress thanks to an app refresh.

This under-$200 model ships with 128GB of storage, so enough for a few apps and some local videos, but there is thankfully a microSD card slot if you want to expand it in order to fit more downloaded media.

This large tablet is ideal for day-to-day tasks, be that watching movies on the go, browsing the web, or enjoying some casual games. It's not the fastest out there, but it's an affordable way to get into the Samsung ecosystem.

Key specs at a glance: Display - 1920x1200 90Hz LCD, RAM - 6GB, Storage - 128GB, SoC - octa-core MediaTek MT8775, MicroSD card slot - Yes.
