Samsung is working on a long overdue redesign for its Galaxy S27 series and, this week, we’re getting a fresh look.

We’ve already seen it in renders, but leaker Sonny Dickson took to Twitter/X today to present an image of Galaxy S27 Pro, Ultra, and S27+ dummy units, and it tells us a few things.

In a recent development, first, we get to see the redesign again, this time in “real life.” That’s, of course, with the caveat that these aren’t real devices, and probably aren’t reflective of the final design either. The Pro and Ultra both have a full-width camera island akin to Apple’s latest iPhone design language, and a substantial departure from the vertical camera array that Samsung insisted was its “core identity” mere months ago.

Recent findings suggest that importantly, we can also see the size difference between Galaxy S27 Ultra and S27 Pro, with the Pro being noticeably smaller compared to both the Ultra and the S27+. For small(er) phone fans, it’s looking great.

All of that said, adding a bit of controversy to this image is leaker Ice Universe, who indicates “this picture is wrong.”.

Sources indicate that ice points out that the Galaxy S27 Ultra and Pro both do not have a fourth camera lens, which is correct based on recent leaks. That remarked, it’s not much of a leap to assume these dummy units are based on an older design, or are confusing a sensor. After all, these are mainly used to test accessories and, this early on, there’s plenty of room for inaccuracy.
