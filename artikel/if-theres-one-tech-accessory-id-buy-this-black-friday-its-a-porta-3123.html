Black Friday is a great time to pick up some tech accessories, and one thing I'd recommend getting if you don't already own one is a portable charger. Sure, smartphone brands have started packing bigger batteries into their phones (surprisingly, even Samsung), but having a power bank on hand still gives you some peace of mind.

It's a great smartphone accessory to have on hand, especially when you're traveling, as using your phone for navigation, taking pictures, and staying connected throughout the day can drain even the biggest smartphone batteries pretty rapidly.

Thankfully, some of the biggest charging accessory brands, including Anker, Baseus, and Ugreen, are offering pretty good discounts this Black Friday. There are plenty of options on sale right now, including fresh Qi2 magnetic power banks, portable chargers with built-in USB-C cables, and even portable chargers for laptops.

I've rounded up five of the best deals worth checking out, with prices starting at just $19.

Recent findings suggest that if you don't like carrying around bulky power banks, this Baseus PicoGo magnetic charger is worth checking out. It's only 0.3 inches thick and packs a sizeable 5,000mAh battery, making it a great option for topping up your phone throughout the day. You can simply snap it onto the back of a MagSafe-compatible phone or case and continue using your phone without a cable getting in the way.

If you want a simple power bank for everyday use, this is probably the one to get. It comes with a 10,000mAh battery, supports up to 30W fast charging, and has a built-in braided USB-C cable that doubles as a carrying loop. There's also a small digital display that shows exactly how much battery you have left.

Sources indicate that this Anker power bank is particularly useful if you travel frequently. Alongside its 10,000mAh battery and 30W charging speeds, it comes with a built-in USB-C cable and a foldable wall plug. This means you can plug it directly into a wall outlet to recharge it, and it can also double as your regular wall charger. That's one less charging accessory to carry when you're traveling.

Sources indicate that if you want a power bank that can charge more than just your phone, the Ugreen Nexode Pro is worth spending a little extra on. It packs a massive 25,000mAh battery with up to 200W of charging output, making it powerful enough to charge compatible laptops. It also comes with built-in USB-C cables and a color display for monitoring charging speeds and battery levels.

According to recent reports, if you spend a lot of time outdoors, this 10,000 mAh rugged power bank from INIU might make more sense than a regular portable charger. It features a rugged exterior and a carabiner that lets you attach it to your backpack. There's even a built-in flashlight, which could come in particularly handy when you're camping or hiking.

On the whole, you don't necessarily need to spend $90 on a power bank if all you're going to use it for is charging your phone.

A 10,000mAh portable charger should be enough for most people, and I'd particularly recommend getting the Anker one with a built-in cable so you don't have to remember to carry one separately.

That mentioned, if you frequently travel with a laptop, spending a little extra on something like the Ugreen Nexode Pro makes a lot more sense.
