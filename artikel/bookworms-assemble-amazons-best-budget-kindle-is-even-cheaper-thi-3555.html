Bookworms, listen up: Amazon's huge Black Friday event is officially in full swing, and you know what that means — it's time to see what discounts there are on the best e-readers.

Amazon's range of Kindle devices are some of the most popular devices out there for those who are keen readers but don't particularly want to lug around tons of physical books with them. That stated, some Kindle models out there cost a lot more than you think (the Amazon Kindle Scribe Colorsoft can be upwards of $600 on a normal day), but luckily one of Amazon's budget-friendly options is now even cheaper.

Here we have the standard Amazon Kindle (2024), which you can score for $119.99, down from $149.99 from the retailer itself, a solid starting point for those who are thinking about picking up their first ereader.

According to recent reports, the best thing about the Amazon Kindle (2024), aside from its Black Friday price cut, is that it keeps things very simple, focusing primarily on the reading experience. With 16GB of storage, which we think is more than enough for an ereader of this size, you have room to store thousands of titles which you can purchase from the on-device Kindle store.

I know what you're probably asking yourself: 'so I have to pay for books separately on top of the $120 device?'. Another tip is to keep an eye out for Kindle's monthly sales, and any free titles that crop up — this is particularly the case for older releases, so if you're really into your classics, definitely take advantage of this!

All things considered, if you enjoy reading from physical books more and take satisfaction in lining them up neatly on your bookshelf, e-readers take away that novelty.
