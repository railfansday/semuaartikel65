It has been revealed that thanksgiving doesn't have to be a stressful holiday. Thanks to several chain restaurants and retailers, you can save some time, effort, and money this year on your meal. We've rounded up a handful of places you can grab all the ingredients you need at a discount or a pre-made meal for a locked-in price. There's no shame in grabbing a pre-made Thanksgiving feast, BTW.

Check out some of our favorite Thanksgiving 2026 meal deals below:.

Choose from several pre-made Thanksgiving meals at Bob Evans, including the Ham Farmhoue Feast that feeds four for $94.99 or the Turkey Farmhouse Feast that feeds four for $104.99.

Cracker Barrel's Thanksgiving Heat & Serve Feast feeds up to 10 people for just $144.99 and includes two oven-roasted turkey breasts, cornbread dressing, gravy, cranberry relish, sweet potato casserole, and more.

Denny's is bringing back its limited-time Turkey Bundle, which includes a carved turkey breast, bread stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy, and cranberry sauce. Order from Denny's website for participating locations between Nov. 20 and Nov. 25.

Customers can get a limited-time "Triple Treat Box" for just $21.99, which includes two medium, two-topping pizzas, breadsticks, and cinnamon sticks. The deal runs through Dec. 28.

Popeyes' Cajun Turkey is back for another year and starts at $54.99 for pickup only.

Ruth's Chris Steak House is offering a $196 heat-and-serve Thanksgiving feast this year. Orders are open until November 20.

A Thanksgiving meal for six at Target is just $30 and includes a whole turkey, potatoes, green beans, gravy, and more.

In a recent development, walmart's Thanksgiving meal deal includes a whole turkey, plus almost all the ingredients you need to make your side dishes this holiday season, all for just $35. Walmart estimates the meal should serve 10 and it's valid through Dec. 24.
