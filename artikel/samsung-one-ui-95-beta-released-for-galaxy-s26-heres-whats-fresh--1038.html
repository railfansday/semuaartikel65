Samsung One UI 9.5 beta released for Galaxy S26: Here's what's fresh [Changelog]

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Samsung has officially released the One UI 9.5 beta for Galaxy S26 customers.

Industry observers note that after weeks of testing and rumors, Samsung has officially announced the One UI 9.5 beta for Galaxy S26 owners. According to the company's announcement, the beta will give users an early look at One UI 9.5 ahead of its stable release. We expect the stable version of One UI 9.5 to arrive alongside the Galaxy S27 series launch next year, but until then, the beta will give users a taste of what's coming.

As for what's newly introduced, Samsung states One UI 9.5 introduces updated camera controls that give customers more flexibility and creative control when taking photos and videos. According to the tech giant, enhanced Pro and Pro Video modes offer customizable controls and presets for different shooting conditions.

There's also a fresh Mindful Note feature that allows people to express their feelings and journal their thoughts, similar to what's already available through Apple's Journal app and Google's Pixel Journal.

Alongside this, Samsung is introducing a new medication tracking feature that allows owners to add prescriptions, vitamins, and supplements, set customized reminders, and keep track of their medications directly from their phones.

Probably the best feature Samsung has introduced with One UI 9.5 is the ability to lock individual apps. Until now, people had to rely on Secure Folder to keep apps behind biometric authentication. But Samsung is finally giving users more flexibility with App Lock.

The company indicates users will be able to keep individual apps private by locking them behind biometric authentication or their screen lock. Samsung also says that when an app is locked, its notification content and widgets will be hidden as well.

According to recent reports, in addition to this, One UI 9.5 introduces a dedicated floating shortcut button that gives users quick access to frequently used controls. There are also improved hearing aid controls, allowing users to adjust the volume directly from the notification panel.

Recent findings suggest that for now, Samsung has started rolling out the One UI 9.5 beta to Galaxy S26 people in select countries. You'll need to enroll through the Samsung Members app to try it out. However, if you'd rather wait for the stable update, you'll likely have to wait another couple of months until the Galaxy S27 series releases next year.

Recent findings suggest that tipster Ice Universe has also shared the One UI 9.5 changelog on X. Here's everything upcoming in the first build of One UI 9.5:.

- A cleaner interface makes camera settings easier to access and compare without excessive scrolling. The bottom control area has been redesigned for more convenient operation.

1.2 Enhanced Pro Mode and Pro Video.

- Improved shutter speed and frame rate controls.

- More precise shutter speed adjustments.

- A redesigned audio control interface in Pro Video mode.

2.1 Live Effects for the Lock Screen.

- Add dynamic depth effects to your lock screen.

- Elements in the wallpaper move at different speeds when the device is tilted, creating a sense of depth.

- Choose from 10-second, 30-second, 1-minute, 5-minute, and 10-minute time intervals.

- Customize these settings through the clock options in the upper-right corner of the lock screen editor.

2.3 Expanded Now Bar Animations.

More Now Bar animations are available across different scenarios.

- Enjoy dynamic visual effects for supported sports events, including team scores and victories.

- Set a waiting period of up to 10 seconds before selected apps open.

- Designed to reduce impulsive app usage and encourage more mindful screen time.

- Protect individual apps using a PIN, pattern, password, or biometric authentication.

- Keep sensitive app content secure when sharing your device.

4.2 Enhanced Privacy Display (Supported Models).

- Privacy Display can activate automatically when entering a PIN, pattern, or password.

- Additional support for private Gallery albums and locked Samsung Notes.

- Automatic activation options for selected apps and notifications.

4.3 Improved Biometric Authentication.

- Enhanced facial recognition and fingerprint authentication.

- Support for authentication flows involving both biometric methods.

- Fingerprint authentication can be completed directly on the sensor without an additional on-screen confirmation.

4.4 Redesigned Biometric Enrollment.

- Fingerprint and facial recognition setup screens feature a cleaner interface and refined animations.

5.1 Built-in Office Document Preview.

- Preview DOCX and PPTX files directly in My Files without installing Microsoft Office.

- PPTX slideshow playback is also supported.

5.2 Improved Storage Management.

- Easier selection and deletion of uncategorized files and files currently in use when managing storage.

6.1 Improved Maximized Window Experience.

- Maximized windows can occupy more screen space.

- Refined status bar and taskbar layouts provide a more immersive desktop experience.

- More window snapping options beyond traditional split-screen layouts.

- Arrange two, three, or four applications on the screen simultaneously.

7.1 Sleep Coaching Improvements.

- Start sleep coaching using sleep data collected over the previous two to three days.

- Get personalized recommendations based on sleep patterns and goals.

7.2 Mood Tracking and Journaling.

- Record emotions, thoughts, and personal reflections.

- Journal entries can be linked to mood records for easier tracking.

7.3 Improved Medication Management.

- Manage registered medications more conveniently through Samsung Health.

- Expanded medication management functionality.

- Rearrange health cards such as Activity, Sleep, Body Composition, Mood, and Food Tracking.

- Prioritize the information you use most frequently.

8.1 Floating Accessibility Shortcut.

- A dedicated floating button provides quick access to accessibility features.

- Improved positioning and transparency controls.

8.2 Enhanced Hearing Aid Controls.

- Adjust the volume of connected hearing aids.

- Choose which sounds to hear, including ambient audio, speech, and notifications.

- Search for settings and features more efficiently.

- Recently searched items are displayed for quicker access.

9.2 Downloadable Galaxy Wallpapers.

- Download classic Over the Horizon wallpapers from previous Galaxy generations.

- Set your favorite wallpapers directly on your device.

10.1 Discontinued Audio Features.

- Audio Broadcast and Enhanced Audio Services are no longer supported starting with One UI 9.5.
