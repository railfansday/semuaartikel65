📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Skydance CEO David Ellison says HBO will air Alex Gibney’s documentary about Elon Musk, even after news broke that the SpaceX founder had invested $1 billion in the newly formed tech giant following its Warner Bro. Discovery and Paramount merger.

Following news of that investment, questions emerged over how Musk’s sizable stake in the company might affect Skydance’s plans to distribute a documentary that is highly critical of him.

When asked by The Upcoming York Times editor and columnist Andrew Ross Sorkin whether HBO would still distribute the documentary even with Musk’s stake in its parent company, Ellison noted, “We have a pay-one deal with A24, who produced that movie. Of course, we’re going to honor the deal.”.

He went on to say, “We believe in independence. We don’t believe in censorship of any kind.”.

Co-CEOs David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz are tasked with bringing together two major streaming platforms, Paramount+ and HBO Max, along with a portfolio of networks, including CBS, CNN, MTV, TBS, Comedy Central, and Food Network, under a single corporate umbrella.
