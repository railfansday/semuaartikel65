Industry News: The Pixel Watch 5 is finally below $300 thanks to this early Black Friday deal

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google raised the starting price of the Pixel Watch 5 by $50 this year, making its latest smartwatch more expensive than previous generations. But if you've been waiting for a good deal on Google's newest smartwatch, Amazon has just knocked $100 off its price ahead of Black Friday.

The Pixel Watch 5 is now down to just $299, a massive 25% discount from its usual $399 price.

Industry observers note that google's latest Pixel Watch 5 is down to just $299 on Amazon, saving you $100 on its usual $399 price. It features a bright 3,000-nit AMOLED display, Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 chipset, Wear OS 7, Gemini integration, and improved battery life.

✅ Recommended if: You want a premium Wear OS smartwatch with a bright display, Gemini integration, and a compact design, especially if you're upgrading from an older Pixel Watch.

❌ Skip this deal if: You already own a Pixel Watch 4, prefer a larger 45mm smartwatch, need LTE connectivity, or use an iPhone.

The Pixel Watch 5 doesn't look much different compared to previous-generation Pixel smartwatches. You still get the same circular design with a domed glass display that we've seen on previous generations. That's not necessarily a bad thing, though, as the Pixel Watch remains one of the better-looking Wear OS smartwatches out there.

It has been revealed that but Google has made some meaningful improvements under the hood. The Pixel Watch 5 is powered by the upgraded Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 chipset with an accelerated co-processor, alongside 3GB of RAM and 64GB of storage. It runs Wear OS 7 out of the box, and one of the biggest upgrades is deeper Gemini integration.

You can now talk to Gemini directly from your wrist using the Raise to Talk gesture, while basic tasks can also be handled on-device without requiring an internet connection.

According to recent reports, and, of course, one of the biggest reasons to get a Pixel Watch is its health and fitness tracking. You get plenty of Google Health-powered features, including heart rate monitoring, sleep tracking, ECG, and blood oxygen tracking.

Google has also added new health insights on topics such as blood pressure trends, insulin resistance, and sleep breathing quality, although some of these features require a Google Health Premium subscription and may not be available everywhere.

On the whole, if you're looking for a premium Wear OS smartwatch that works well with your Android phone, the Pixel Watch 5 is definitely worth considering. It offers a great display, solid performance, and plenty of useful health tracking features. And now that it's down to $299, you're getting Google's latest smartwatch for $100 less than its usual price.
