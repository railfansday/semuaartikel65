Audiophiles, listen up; there are some great Sony headphone Black Friday deals already kicking about, but it's the previous-gen Sony WH-1000 XM4 model that's caught my attention.

In a recent development, amazon is listing the Sony WH-1000 XM4 model for $198 from $299.99, knocking a generous $100 off what were one of our best wireless headphones at the time of release some six years ago. And yes, Sony has released two iterations since then — I've been using the WH-1000 XM5s for a few years and have no complaints — but there's a reason the XM4s are still a fan favorite.

According to recent reports, sony's XM5s, and more recently its WH-1000 XM6 headphones, have noticeable upgrades; the design is more ambitious and polished, and the audio quality is slightly more refined with each upgrade. But in the case of the XM4s, they boost audio performance that is way above satisfactory and not totally different from the XM5s, with above-average ANC that does the job at shutting out noise while on a commute.

Recent findings suggest that but that's not the only reason why audiophiles still love them to this day. The headphones' folding ear pads, a hardware feature that was abandoned in the XM5 release, aren't just handy for reducing to a compact travel size but are a huge advantage for hardware care overall. Though the XM5s have served me well, they're quite bulky to travel with, meaning I don't bother using the travel case, which results in discoloration.

✅Recommended if: You're sticking to a budget and don't want to spend the best part of $300+ for a pair of Sony's latest headphones.

If you travel a lot and haven't got loads of space to carry around a bulky pair of headphones, the folding earpads reduce the headphones' size, so you can easily squeeze them into your bag. They also pack a solid battery life, making them ideal for longer journeys.

❌Skip this deal if: You have the money to spend and prefer the stylish aesthetics of the XM5 or XM6 headphones.
