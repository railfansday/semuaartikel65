While you may already be using Google’s Gemini AI platform to set up your deal alerts for Black Friday savings this year, why not take advantage of an offer that makes the massive G’s artificial intelligence agents more useful in the process?

As revealed over on X by Google’s own product lead for Gemini subscriptions, Vikas Kansal, the organization is slashing the prices of its Gemini AI Pro and AI Plus prepares by 50% for the Black Friday period.

The Gemini AI Pro Initiative is particularly good value, as it gets you more generous access to the Gemini 4 AI models, Carbon, Hydrogen, Neon and Argon. The plan bags you four times the usage limit for the Neon model, which specialises in software development and complex scientific tasks via its ‘leave-them-to-figure-it-out’ agents.

According to recent reports, not only that, but the AI Pro plan also throws in a good selection of freebies for when you’re kicking back and relaxing, including YouTube Premium Lite, Google Health Premium, and Google Home Premium Standard, as well as 5TB of cloud storage to share with your family. Even at the plan’s usual $20 per month cost, it's a good value, making this Black Friday offer a real steal.
