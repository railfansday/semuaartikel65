Storage can be a constant problem if you're using a more budget-friendly or older device, but downloading the latest games doesn't mean you have to delete loads of pictures from your gallery or uninstall your favorite apps to create space.

That's because developer Level Infinite has just released a new version of PUBG Mobile designed specifically to be the lightest version of the popular battle royale game yet. The initial download of PUBG Mobile Flash takes up just 0.96GB, with multiple tools available to ensure subsequent content downloads are limited to the maps and modes you want to play – keeping as much free space on your phone as possible.

PUBG Mobile Flash also supports a wider range of devices than the base game, including those with just 2GB of RAM, and promises better graphics optimization for smoother frame rates.

The file size may be small, but Level Infinite makes it clear that this is still a fully fledged version of what it calls "one of the world’s most popular mobile games.".

It has been revealed that PUBG Mobile Flash players will be able to squad up with their friends on regular PUBG Mobile. There is also seamless account cross-progression, so you'll be able to pick up where you left off if you already play the regular version of the game.

“Expanding access to PUBG Mobile has always been an vital part of our long-term vision, and we’re continually looking for newly introduced ways to connect players around the world,” explained general manager and PUBG Mobile head Vincent Wang.

"With PUBG Mobile Flash, we’re taking another step towards that goal, making it easier for more players to enjoy the core PUBG Mobile experience, reconnect with friends, and remain part of our global community.".
