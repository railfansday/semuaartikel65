The best smart speakers have always been part of my tech ecosystem, but since smart displays have grown in popularity, I've been asking myself: am I missing out?

Industry observers note that when Apple unveiled its flagship smart display, the HomeView, I really liked what I saw — except for that price tag. Thankfully, it's Black Friday week, and Amazon's own Echo Show 8 is one of the many smart home devices that have turned my head.

For a record-low price, you can get the Echo Show 8 smart display for $99.99 down from $199.99, a generous $ 100 off. But what does a smart display do that a smart speaker can't?

Industry observers note that firstly, the Echo Show 8 is a hybrid between the two, offering robust audio and voice command features you get with a smart speaker, while giving you all the visual and touchscreen components you get with a tablet. Its 8.2" screen is also a nice halfway between the Echo Show 5 and Echo Show 10, meaning it's not huge but also not too small to navigate.

With the addition of the screen, this opens the door to some things you just can't do with a smart speaker. They work wonders for displaying recipes while you cook, and as someone who pretty much lives in their kitchen, this means no more reading the small print from a phone. I also play a lot of YouTube in the background while fixing dinner, so having the larger screen for that is another bonus.

That aside, if your home is kitted with video doorbells and home security cameras, you can view who's at your door and speak with people right through your smart display - once again eliminating the need to reach for your phone.

According to recent reports, for a previous-gen smart display, the Echo Show 8 still sits in first place for us. Though it doesn't pack the handy swivel function, it sports an 8.2-inch display fitted with a camera that doesn't fail to keep within the frame at all times. Its interface is also powered by Alexa+, doubling as a smart speaker with powerful audio capabilities.

✅Recommended if: You use video doorbells and other smart home security devices, and want another device that's not your phone to keep an extra eye out.

If you love the features of Echo smart speakers and tablets respectively but don't want to spend money on two separate devices, smart displays give you the best of both worlds.

❌Skip this deal if: You want a smart display that has Dolby processing support and a display that you can rotate. If HD viewing is the priority, opt for something like the Echo Show 10 as an alternative.
