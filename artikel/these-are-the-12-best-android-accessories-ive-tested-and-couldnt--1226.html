Once you have one of the best Android phones, or maybe you're hanging onto an older device, you'll want to get the most out of it. You can enhance the experience with accessories, and these run the gamut from earbuds (check out our related guide) to protective cases, power banks, and more.

Sources indicate that the first item I always get for a latest phone is a fitted case, and there's one that's almost my first buy. From there, I'll eventually switch the case to something more stylish, bolder, or whatever fits my needs. But having a solid starter case is essential.

Recent findings suggest that another essential I won't go without is a power bank, and I can't count how many I have. I'm still on the hunt for the perfect one, but there are a few awesome ones I love.

There's lots more you can get to complement your phone, whether it's to charge it promptly or on the go, mount it to a surface, enhance fitness, take better photos, provide solid grip, and more.

We're here to help with some recommendations for fantastic and affordable Android phone accessories. We've used many of these ourselves and can confirm they are worth the money.
