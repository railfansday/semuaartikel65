📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Somehow, Jensen Huang returned.

Recent findings suggest that A mere 12 days after the controversial, embattled Nvidia CEO was ousted from his position, the big tech firm confirmed that he would be reinstated. Coincidentally or not, this decision followed Donald Trump's public endorsement of Huang's return. Regardless of how or why it happened, the big question now is how NVDA will respond on the stock market.

Needless to say, if you're an Nvidia shareholder, you're probably feeling pretty all right on Monday morning. At the time of publication, NVDA is up a staggering 35 percent from the start of trading. This came after a tech sell-off last week, which might explain the huge percentage jump. The current price of $170 per share isn't much higher than the $162 level it was at early last week before the sell-off, but it's obviously significantly higher than the $125 price it ended the week at.

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free right now with T-Mobile.

The timeline of Huang's resignation is messy and convoluted. It started with the revelation that he was having an extramarital affair with a suspected Chinese spy, though there is reason to believe she may not have been an intelligence asset. Regardless, a second affair with a subordinate Nvidia executive came to light, which led to his resignation almost two weeks ago.

Industry observers note that after his resignation, reports came out detailing an abusive work culture that allegedly existed at Nvidia under Huang's direction. Despite that, Nvidia's board decided to reinstate Huang.
