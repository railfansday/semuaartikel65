It looks like wide-screen phones are the new massive Android phone trend of 2026, with the popularity of the passport-style foldable Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 and the arrival of more recent, non-folding contenders like Huawei's new OSTA O1 Pro.

In a recent development, now, rugged smartphone brand Doogee is bringing the form factor to a more affordable market segment with its upcoming Wide Core model. It looks quite similar to the existing, more premium Huawei Pura X View, with a similar maroon color option and visor-like camera island design.

Industry observers note that its key specs include a 6.38-inch 16:10 display and the entry-level octa-core Unisoc T750 chipset. Doogee has confirmed that there will be multiple memory configurations available, ranging from a super low-end 3GB RAM and 64GB storage setup to more reasonable 4GB+128GB, 6GB+128GB, and 6GB+256GB options.

According to recent reports, the Wide Core will also feature a 6,5000mAh battery and NFC support. It will run Android 17 out of the box.

Recent findings suggest that doogee hasn't mentioned the price, but based on these specs, it will undoubtedly sit on the more affordable end of the spectrum. The brand already produces a few phones running that same Unisoc T750 chipset with similar storage options, and prices start at under $300 / £200.

Even considering a premium for the wide-screen display, I think it's reasonable to suggest that the Wide Core could come in below the $400 mark – though you should bear in mind that this is just an educated guess at this stage.

In a recent development, there's no word on when the Doogee Wide Core will come out either, so I'll be keeping a close eye on the brand's website for updates. Even so, the prospect of a more affordable wide-screen phone is definitely interesting and gives a wider range of consumers access to this rapidly emerging form factor. Expect more companies to come out with their own wide-screen contenders in the coming weeks and months.
