It has been revealed that if you're a stylus lover, then there haven't been many great options when it comes to Android phones as of late. Sure, the pricey Samsung S26 Ultra comes with a built-in S Pen, but if you're after something much cheaper, you might assume that you're out of luck.

Thankfully, Motorola has you covered with the Moto Stylus – one of the few budget-friendly models that comes with a pen. Now, the handset is cheaper than it's ever been on Amazon, with a Black Friday discount that takes it down to just $199.99 from $399.99.

🐲 Chipset: Snapdragon 6 Gen 3🐄 RAM: 8GB💾 Storage: 256GB🔋 Battery: 5,000mAh⭐ Star feature: Built-in stylus.

Despite the low price, it's not a low-end phone by any means. It's got a bright AMOLED display at a butter-smooth 120Hz refresh rate, 8GB of RAM, and (in the case of this particular unit) 256GB of built-in storage. It's powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 3, which, while firmly a mid-range chip, delivers smooth day-to-day performance and casual gaming.

Industry observers note that the real start of the show is that stylus, though, and you can pop it out for easy sketching or note-taking. Oh, and the phone is a cool textured blue color, helping it really stand out from the crowd.

According to recent reports, A decent mid-range phone that stands out thanks to its included stylus and current low price. This is the cheapest this model has ever been on Amazon, and a killer price for something with a decent chipset, 8GB of RAM, and 256GB of storage. There's also a microSD card slot, so you can add more room if you need it.

✅Recommended if: The Motorola Moto G Stylus is one of the few phone models that come with a built-in stylus and doesn't cost a fortune. If you're looking for a more affordable phone that lets you draw and take notes easily, then the Moto G Stylus is a serious contender.

In a recent development, ❌Skip this deal if: You are concerned about the patch support. This 2025 model only comes with two major OS upgrades (though Motorola has at least promised three years of security patches).

This isn't too unreasonable at this ridiculously low price, but if you're the kind of person who really cares about getting the latest updates over the long term, you should consider buying something else.
