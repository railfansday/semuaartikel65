Sources indicate that the countdown to Black Friday has officially begun, and with less than 24 hours to go, we're really starting to feel the adrenaline. And as a team of phone-obsessives, our eyes have lit up at the sight of the live Android phone deals.

Black Friday Android deals started a few days ago, but if you've been patient and suppressed your impulse-buying urges to find better discounts, we salute you. It was probably for the best too, because prices for flagship models across Samsung, Google, and more have dropped even more.

According to recent reports, out of all the Black Friday deals we've found, it's clear that the Samsung Galaxy S26 series is the star of the show, with price drops across both its mid-range and premium models. At the same time, Google has also pulled through with record-low deals on the Pixel 11 line-up that will be gone by the end of the weekend.

Sources indicate that that remarked, we're still a few hours away from Black Friday itself, so these deals could literally go in any direction. If it were to go our way, we'd like to see one more small price drop to steer us into the weekend — but one can only dream.

Out of all the devices in Samsung's S26 series, the Galaxy S26 FE is the most affordable of the bunch — the perfect option to go for if you want a new model but have a budget to stick to. Though it's thin in size, the Exynos 2500 chipset gives it a valuable performance boost for a mid-range smartphone.

Sources indicate that compared to the Pixel 10 Pro, the camera features are by far the biggest upgrade, especially Magic Capture, which took center stage in our review. On top of the hands-free image-capturing tool and bright 6.8-inch display, the Pixel 11 Pro packs other handy Gemini features such as Rambler, which uses your voice to form clean text as opposed to transcribing word-for-word.

In a recent development, when we reviewed the Galaxy S26 Ultra, the device's Privacy Display was the clear standout feature, and it's even better with a $300 Black Friday saving. As well as this added layer of reassurance that no one is snooping on your screen, the S26 Ultra's AI features are second-to-none — and the built-in S Pen stylus is a bonus.

It has been revealed that we loved the regular Pixel 11 Pro slightly more than the Pro model for a number of reasons. For one, it really cranks up the AI features from the previous model by weaving in handy social features such as its built-in Creator Suite and Screen Reactions. This is likely the lowest price we'll see it drop this Black Friday, so score it while you can.

Sources indicate that can't justify the price of the Pixel 11 Pro XL? You can still save over $200 on the standard 6.3-inch model. Just like its sibling, it packs all the same handy features, like Magic Capture and Rambler, just on a slightly smaller scale.

It has been revealed that samsung made waves with the Galaxy Z Fold this year, solidifying its status as a top foldable phone maker. The 7.6-inch folding screen (and a 5.5-inch front panel) is bright and clear, running 12GB of RAM and the flagship Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy processor, and, for a foldable phone, it's surprisingly pocketable.

Recent findings suggest that best Buy's $200 saving on the Galaxy Z Flip8 is one of the better Black Friday phone deals that are still live, available in three unique shades. Though it's a small upgrade from its predecessor, it still makes a great device if you're after a trendy phone that's light and thin, with powerful software running through its veins.

For those of you who aren't too fussed with keeping up with the latest models, the Pixel 10 packs similar AI features to the Pixel 11 (albeit not to the same level), but that shouldn't put you off — especially not at this price tag. Its support for Qi2 allows you to take advantage of wireless charging, and its improved battery can take you through the entire day on a single charge.

It has been revealed that nothing has nailed it with its brand identity, and it's done the same with the performance of its smartphones. For a previous-gen model, the Nothing Phone (3) is a solid performer thanks to the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset, and the battery life isn't too shabby, either.
