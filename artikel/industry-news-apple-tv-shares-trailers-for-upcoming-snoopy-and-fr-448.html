With the holidays fast approaching, Apple TV has shared trailers for two upcoming specials featuring Snoopy and the Fraggles. Watch them below.

Recent findings suggest that “Snoopy Presents: 12 Days of Snoopy” continues Apple’s partnership with Peanuts, which was recently extended through 2030. Beyond Apple TV, Apple has also brought Snoopy to watch faces on the Apple Watch, Apple TV screensavers, and the new Kid Cue feature on the iPhone Duo, which displays Peanuts animations on the outer screen to help capture children’s attention when taking photos.

The new special joins other Apple TV originals featuring the beloved Peanuts characters, including “It’s the Small Things, Charlie Brown,” “Welcome Home, Franklin,” and “A Summer Musical.”.

Here’s Apple TV on “Snoopy Presents: 12 Days of Snoopy”:.

It has been revealed that in the latest special of the acclaimed “Snoopy Presents” banner, the Peanuts kids are on their best behavior — but Snoopy has landed on Santa’s naughty list. With just 12 days until Christmas, he sets out to prove he’s not such a bad beagle after all, discovering along the way that the season is about more than gifts.

As for “Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift,” it is the latest collaboration between Apple TV and The Jim Henson Organization. The all-new holiday special will join other productions such as the Emmy Award-winning “Fraggle Rock: Back to the Rock,” the 2020 series of shorts “Fraggle Rock: Rock On!,” and last year’s holiday special “The First Snow of Fraggle Rock.”.

In this all-new holiday special, Wembley is named Spirit Captain and sets out to spread cheer throughout the Rock. But when the Fraggles unknowingly take a cherished snow globe from Doc’s workshop, the holiday spirit begins to fade. Wembley must find the courage to return it, learning that kindness, giving and helping someone in need are the true gifts of the holidays.
