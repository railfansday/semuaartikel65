According to recent reports, back in my day, the only “Doorbuster” sales were at physical retail stores. But now, you can get ultra-low prices before Black Friday whether you want to shop in-store or online. One that stands out is the previous-generation Sony WH-1000XM5 noise-canceling headphones, which are marked down to their lowest price ever, just $169.99 at Best Buy. While their MSRP is $399.99, they’ve been spotted as low as $198 in the last few months.

The WH-1000XM5 rolled out in 2022, and despite having been succeeded by the WH-1000XM6, they’re still an excellent set of headphones. Their active noise cancellation effect is one of the best out there — competitive with the latest models from Bose and Apple — and the sound profile is tighter and more balanced than the older Sony headphones. There are some minor downsides, like a bulky case and all-plastic design, but those seem easier to overlook at this lower price point.

It has been revealed that the newer Sony WH-1000XM6 improve the sound and noise cancellation, with quality of life upgrades not present in the XM5, like a folding design and more comfortable headband. However, they’re significantly more expensive, currently discounted to $348 during Black Friday (Amazon, Best Buy), down from their MSRP of $450.

To make shopping a little more confusing, the newer WH-1000XM4C sit between the XM5 and XM6 in terms of price, and are a slightly updated version of the older XM4 model with its foldable design. Our reviewer John Higgins thinks the XM4C’s noise cancellation lags behind, so I’d opt for the XM5 over them, especially at the sale price.
