Amazon has won the smart home Olympics with its range of Echo devices, but it's tricky to settle on one when there are so many options out there — and the Black Friday madness doesn't help, either.

There are two star devices in Amazon's Black Friday event: the 5th-gen Echo Dot and the newer Echo Dot Max, both of which are some of the best smart speakers out there. On a regular day, you'd be paying the best part of $100 for each, but the current Black Friday offers are hard to ignore.

Industry observers note that as for the previous-gen model, the Echo Dot is now $39.99 down from $79.99, while the upgraded Echo Dot Max is $49.99 down from $199.99. So what's the difference between the two devices, and how do you decide which one is best for you?

Sources indicate that similarities-wise, both are compact smart speakers that any bookcase, media unit, or countertop can adorn. Both support Amazon's upgraded AI voice assistant, Alexa+, and both speakers have fine-tuned inner workings that boost mighty audio performance - but the Echo Dot Max's upgrades could be the reason you spend a little more.

Though the 5th-gen Echo Dot is far from a poor smart hub, its newer sibling is more advanced not only on the audio side, but also on the smart home front, which you can dive into below. For those who aren't too fussed with getting all the bells and whistles, the Echo Dot is plenty to get you by, but for just $10 extra you'll be getting a lot more — I know which one I'd be spending my money on this Black Friday.

It's not the lowest price we've seen for this Echo device, but who can say no to a $40 smart speaker? It's compact, fits nicely in any room, and doesn't fall flat on offering a bold audio experience. Despite being a previous gen model, it's a top pick for us, and one we always keep an eye out for during Black Friday.
