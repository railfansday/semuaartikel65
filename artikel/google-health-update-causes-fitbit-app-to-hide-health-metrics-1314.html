The latest Google Health app revision was supposed to bring a slew of performance improvements for some of the best fitness trackers, but instead, it unleashed a bug that's been plaguing Fitbit consumers for the past few days.

In a recent development, according to the slew of reports on Reddit, a number of basic health metrics have gone missing from the view in the mobile app on both Android and iOS, including overnight vitals such as resting heart rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, and HRV. Google rolled out its 5.12 update last week, which appears to be the common link between user reports online.

Industry observers note that the bug isn't limited to just one device either — consumers on the newly introduced Fitbit Air and Fitbit Edge models, the Fitbit Charge 6, and Fitbit Sense 2 are all experiencing this absence. These vitals are tracked daily to establish patterns and highlight changes in your health, so if they miss a day or two, it could have a huge impact on your readings.

Sources indicate that but despite these health metrics vanishing from thin air, it doesn't necessarily mean that your device has stopped tracking your data altogether. In some user cases, the device has continued monitoring health patterns, but the app has just failed to display them.

Still, it's not ideal, and consumers have been quick to jump on Google's case — but the tech giant has issued some reassurance that it's looking into it.

Among the wave of reports on Reddit, one user went the extra mile and reached out to Google directly, sharing the email they received in response: 'it's a joke' they shared.

Other users turned to Google's support forum for answers, where one of Google's product experts remarked they had escalated the issue.

However, the challenges in the app are persisting, and Google has yet to find a solution, leaving consumers to find their own workarounds for the time being.

One method you can try is simply logging out of the Google Health app and logging back in again. Some customers have found this helpful, but it hasn't been the golden ticket for others. In that case, try uninstalling the Google Health app altogether and redownloading it, which is a bit of a pain, but it's worth a shot.
