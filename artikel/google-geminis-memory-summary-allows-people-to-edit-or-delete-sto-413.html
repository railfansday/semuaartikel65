Google is boosting the Gemini experience with a latest upgrade that's designed to give you more control over how you manage the information the chatbot saves about you.

The fresh Memory summary tool gives you all the information Gemini has gathered about you from your chatbots, allowing you to edit its summaries to provide additional data or delete things Gemini gets wrong, as spotted by Android Authority.

It's a pretty handy way to tailor your Gemini experience exactly to your liking, but it presents your data in a specific way. Instead of compiling everything Gemini thinks it knows about you into one massive chunk, Memory summary divides your information into distinct sections that cover specific areas.

For example, the Essentials tab functions as a quick one-stop area showcasing your background, location, and key relationships. Gemini also has other other sections for giving you a snapshot of what it remembers about your career, interests, etc, all of which you can chop and change to tailor its knowledge of you more accurately.

Gemini is rolling out a upcoming feature called "Memory Summary"! 🧠✨ This enhancement lets you easily view and edit the key details that Gemini remembers about you. 📝👀 https://t.co/kXO4HjQEanNovember 9, 2026.

It has been revealed that however, as helpful as it sounds, Google's support page says Memory summary is presently available to users in the US with an active Google AI proposal who are over the age of 18, and it needs Keep Activity and Memory enabled to work. According to the organization, it's also restricted to English-only, and it's unclear whether Google will expand its language availability.

But despite the apparent limitations Google lists, it doesn't seem to be the case with Gemini's web version. That stated, Memory summary appears to be working for some free owners on web and mobile browsers, but not in the Gemini mobile app. We've tried to access Memory summary on Gemini's web and mobile browser versions, but have had no luck so far.

As far as finding it goes, it's quite simple. Head to Gemini and tap the Settings icon, then tap Personal Intelligence and then Memory summary. It's available in both the Gemini app and web browser, though it won't appear in the former unless you're based in the US and signed up to a Google AI strategy.
