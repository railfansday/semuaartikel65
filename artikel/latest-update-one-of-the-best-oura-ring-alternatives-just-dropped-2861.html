Industry observers note that black Friday is here, and if you've been thinking about getting a smart ring instead of a smartwatch to keep track of your health, there's a particularly good deal on Ultrahuman's latest smart ring right now. The Ultrahuman Ring Pro is down to just $299, saving you a massive $180 (38%) from its usual $479 price.

The Ring Pro brings several improvements over the Ring Air, including considerably better battery life and a portable charging case. And unlike Oura's smart rings, you don't need to pay a monthly subscription to access your core health metrics.

The Ultrahuman Ring Pro is a titanium smart ring that offers comprehensive tracking of sleep, recovery, stress, and activity without a mandatory subscription. It's usually $479, but it's down to just $299 for Black Friday.

✅Recommended if: You want a discreet health tracker with excellent battery life, comprehensive sleep and recovery insights, and no mandatory monthly subscription.

❌Skip this deal if: You primarily want to track workouts in real time, prefer a wearable with a display, or need built-in GPS.

Sources indicate that one of the reasons I prefer smart rings over a smartwatch is that there's no display constantly asking for your attention. You can simply wear the Ultrahuman Ring Pro throughout the day and let it do its thing in the background. And whenever you want to check your health stats, you can just open the Ultrahuman app on your phone.

According to recent reports, the Ring Pro tracks pretty much everything you'd expect from a premium smart ring, including sleep, recovery, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), stress, skin temperature, movement, and menstrual cycles. Ultrahuman has also improved the heart rate sensor compared to the Ring Air, particularly for tracking sleep and recovery.

Sources indicate that but arguably the biggest reason to get the Ultrahuman Ring Pro over other smart rings is its battery life. Ultrahuman claims up to 15 days on a single charge, which means you could potentially go on a two-week vacation without having to charge the ring even once.

It has been revealed that what's even better is that, unlike most other smart rings, the Ultrahuman Ring Pro comes with a portable charging case. This means that, similar to a pair of wireless earbuds, you can simply pop the ring into the case to charge it on the go. Ultrahuman claims up to 45 days of combined battery life with the charging case.

In a recent development, the biggest reason, however, to purchase the Ultrahuman Ring Pro over the likes of Oura Ring 5 remains that there's no mandatory subscription to access your everyday health and recovery data. The ring also works with both Android phones and iPhones, so you're not tied to a particular smartphone ecosystem.

At its usual $479 price, the Ultrahuman Ring Pro is admittedly quite expensive for a smart ring. But at $299, especially considering the battery life, included charging case, and lack of a mandatory subscription, it's much easier to recommend.
