Google Maps wants to be your one and only trip planner this holiday season

It has been revealed that google Maps wants to help you blueprint everything from small getaways to full holiday itineraries. The tech giant's latest Ask Maps update rolls out with a few vital changes.

Industry observers note that planning a big trip can be difficult, but Google's Ask Maps patch aims to make it easier. In a blog post, the manufacturer states owners rely on Ask Maps to plan road trips or a day out eating at various restaurants in their area. Google's AI will provide a "day-by-day schedule with insider tips and numbered map pins for each stop.".

Ask Maps operates similarly to Gemini's chats. Consumers can revisit previous chats with the AI by searching for an "itinerary badge." Each stop the AI selects for your trip includes traffic details, opening/closing times, and options to edit your schedule yourself. If you don't want something there, you can kick it. Users can also tell Ask Maps to add a "scenic stop" or something else you'd find more interesting.

After you set your trip, you can share your itinerary with others.

According to recent reports, elsewhere, Google says it's brought its agentic AI capabilities from Gemini into Maps. This lets people handle more complex tasks. Users can tell the AI to find a place for a specific appointment. It will reportedly display options that fit what you're looking for (nails, hair, dentist, time, etc.). These capabilities extend to users searching for restaurants at a specific time for a set number of people.

Google indicates these Ask Maps updates are rolling out today (No. 16) on Android and iOS. What's more, the company is bringing this feature to thirty additional countries in Europe.

Sources indicate that once upon a time, Google Maps' "smart suggestions" were what users relied on for quick, on-the-spot ideas while traveling. It also planned trips when you were looking for inspiration for a night out. Essentially, Gemini handled the heavy lifting when users asked, "What's there to do with friends tonight?" The AI would surface "relevant" places, such as restaurants and more, alongside review summaries, times, and more.

Industry observers note that we also received Gemini's "Ask about place" chip. The chip functioned similarly to what we've seen before. People could ask about a specific place they're visiting, and Gemini would provide directions, location info, and other key details. With Google's latest updates to Ask Maps, many of its older features have been consolidated into one.
