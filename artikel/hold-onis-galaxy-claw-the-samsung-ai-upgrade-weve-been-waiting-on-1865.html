📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Samsung is reportedly working on a feature called "Galaxy Claw" that wants to do everything for you—well, within reason.

Recent findings suggest that tipster AssembleDebug with Android Authority highlighted a new direction Samsung could take Galaxy AI's everyday assistance in One UI 9.5. Rumors claim to have discovered the driving force behind Galaxy Claw: the Personal Agent Core. Supposedly, this PAC will help the phone's AI "complete tasks for you by working with apps and tools" on your device.

Industry observers note that the PAC would bring Galaxy Claw into Messages, Gmail, Contacts, and the Phone app. The tipster notes that customers would have to let this AI "view and manage" much of their data in these apps. What you're getting is an AI that's active and busy when you need it to be. Task creation is a huge feature that the tipster allegedly discovered.

Industry observers note that it seems Samsung is preparing a way for people to create a "scheduled task" that includes the task itself, frequency, and timing. The tipster's report spotted several frequency options—hourly, weekly, weekdays, and daily. What's more, they spotted an editing feature, a "run now" option, and "history." Galaxy Claw seems poised to give users more control over their personal AI experience; however, it could also affect another major feature: "Daily Brief.".

Galaxy AI already features Now Brief, which keeps users on top of their priorities and offers morning round-ups of emails, news, and the like. The publication suggests Daily Brief would "summarize emails and notifications" from a user's third-party apps.

According to recent reports, let's not forget something: a similar enhancement for Bixby was rumored not long ago. One UI 9.5 was rumored to introduce more agentic AI capabilities for Samsung's assistant, similar to what Google has done with Gemini. It was alleged that AI could better handle tasks or "actions." These actions would involve booking restaurants or appointments on the user's behalf (and per their instruction).

Recent findings suggest that some have suspected Samsung would introduce these capabilities in Bixby sometime in 2027, alongside its major One UI 9.5 upgrade. Only time will tell if Bixby will still receive these upgrades, or if Samsung is packaging them in Galaxy AI with "Galaxy Claw.".
