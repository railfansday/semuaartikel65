Foldables continue to gather steam. Motorola's next wave of 2027 foldables is expected; however, a rumor suggests we might see them earlier.

According to recent reports, this morning (Nov. 24), an Android Headlines report alleged what the Motorola Razr 2027 could look like. Rumors suggest upgrades for Moto's 2027 foldable, and it looks like what we were hoping for. The publication alleges the Razr 2027 will finally get rid of the ugly border on its cover display. This has been there since its display was smaller; however, rumors say it could feature a 4-inch cover screen.

Recent findings suggest that A leaked render claims this would expand the Razr 2027's cover display to its hinge, mirroring a Galaxy Z Flip 8 experience or a Razr Ultra. Elsewhere, the leak claims Motorola wants to boost its battery cell. The Razr 2027 could feature a 5,000mAh cell, a 200mAh upgrade from the 2026 variant's 4,800mAh. The foldable's charging might also increase to 45W (up from 30W).

Industry observers note that since these rumors discuss the base Razr model for 2027, Motorola's chip of choice was a hot topic. It seems Motorola will stick with MediaTek's mid-range Dimensity chip line next year. It's not a terrible thing, but given the price of the Razr 2026, which the 2027 version might not be far from, the chosen chip could be better.

Internally, the 8GB of RAM and 256GB of stroage could grace the next Razr. If these rumors sound exciting, they suggest we might not have to wait long. Supposedly, Motorola is gearing up to reveal its Razr 2027 during CES 2027 in early January.

Sources indicate that our wants and desires for the Razr 2027 also mentioned a "mid-range Razr." What we meant was a true mid-range Razr. I agree with this. It'd be nice if all of the Razrs had a stronger, more powerful chip. It kind of sucks having this weaker chip on an otherwise nice foldable phone.

Industry observers note that this is a substantial leak, and it's got us thinking back to our original wants and desires for the Razr 2027. The 2026 version wasn't that exciting. It was a decent foldable, yes, but Motorola's "upgrades" weren't all that better. One upgrade we wanted was 256GB of stroage. It's nice to see this rumored, but we need it to actually happen. This year's model cut its storage in half while somehow still increasing its price. It was a distasteful change compared to the 2025 version.

Another area we wanted Motorola to do better in was software upgrades. The 2026 model will only receive three years of OS upgrades and five years of security patches. Unfortunately, according to the publication, that might not change. It sucks when you consider what Google and Samsung are doing: a seven-year promise.
