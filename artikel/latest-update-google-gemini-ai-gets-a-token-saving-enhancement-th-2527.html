If you’ve ever come up against a usage limit when sending your Gemini AI assistants off to get a job done, a new capability for Google’s artificial intelligence platform could save you the hassle — and a bunch of time in the process.

The manufacturer has just confirmed a fresh ‘File Slicing Controls’ feature, rolling out across all its models. In a nutshell, it lets you specify which elements of a file you want Gemini to focus on before it gets to work, rather than having the model do a deep scan of an entire file up front, saving you costly usage tokens in the process.

Though it’s primarily aimed at developers, there are elements here anyone can benefit from. The ‘Skim Then Zoom’ workflow is particularly useful — you can, for instance, tell the AI to summarise and explain just the text of a select range of pages in a PDF, and it’ll skim over the other pages and any associated images, just focussing on the passages you request, clawing back time and tokens.
