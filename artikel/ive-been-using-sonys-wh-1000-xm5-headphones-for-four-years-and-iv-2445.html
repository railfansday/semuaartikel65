About four years ago, I was anointed with a pair of what were the best wireless headphones at the time, and all this time later, my Sony WH-1000 XM5s are still going strong, and I've never looked back.

In a recent development, now that we're creeping even closer to Black Friday season, I cannot stress enough how perfect an opportunity you have to get one at a discounted rate. As it stands, the best Black Friday 2026 deal I've come across is at Best Buy, where Sony's WH-1000 XM5 headphones are now $299.99 down from $398.

And yes, Black Friday is still a week away, but stock is much higher during early sales, and if you're not one to wait for the possibility that a better deal will arise, it's best to act sooner rather than later.

Recent findings suggest that compared to its listing price at the time of release, a sub-$250 offer on some of the best headphones I've used isn't terrible at all. While it's still not exactly a budget-friendly option, the newer XM6 model is still on the pricier side even during early Black Friday sales.

If you're an audio novice and the price tag still scares you, allow me to reassure you.

According to recent reports, as a beginner, you may think that a cheaper pair of headphones with standard audio features will be enough to get you by. And yes, there are plenty of budget-friendly alternatives with above-average performance, but I was also a newbie, and I can confidently say that introducing yourself to audio tech through a brand like Sony will go such a long way.

Sure, $229.99 is still a lot of money even when it's a deal, but once you take the plunge, you won't have to spend this money for a good few years before the headphones start to show signs of wear and tear. I'm four years in, and the audio output is still as punchy and bold as it was when I first purchased them.

Recent findings suggest that speaking of audio, the Sony WH-1000 XM5 headphones pack a versatile listening experience, bringing out the very best of lossless and hi-res music streaming. Its Active Noise Cancellation succeeds at drowning out pretty much everything around you, making them perfect for noisy commutes. At the same time, the Ambient Sound setting is useful for letting in outside noise while you listen to music, which you can easily enable and disable via the button on the left ear pad.

If there's anything to note, it would be the lack of collapsible ear pads, which are a fan-favorite feature from models past (it just makes it a lot easier for travelling). The Platinum Silver version is also prone to discoloring, so Black is probably the better shade to opt for, but other than that, they've presented zero disappointments.
