📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It feels like tech everywhere is getting more and more expensive, but the Black Friday sales give you the opportunity to secure some rare bargains. If you're looking for a tablet and have a budget of just £100, then your options are usually extremely limited: entry-level models like the Lenovo Tab One or the Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 often go for more than £150.

According to recent reports, luckily, Amazon has come to the rescue with its brand-fresh Alexa Tablet line, which has seen a huge swathe of discounts in the ongoing early Black Friday sales. The cheapest option is the Amazon Alexa Tablet 8, the brand's entry-level model, which has plummeted to just £99.99 from £199.99 thanks to a massive 50% price cut.

Although the specs are modest, you're still getting a fully functional, working tablet for under £100, and in 2026 that's worth shouting about. The low price makes this a fantastic pick if you're shopping for kids, as you won't need to worry about them damaging something that will cost a huge amount to replace.

Despite the low cost, there are some premium elements to the design too. The case is a durable aluminium rather than plastic, and the chipset powering it all is the same MediaTek 8-Core processor found in the much pricier Alexa Tablet 11 and is capable of delivering reliable day-to-day performance.

Amazon's most basic Android tablet is a great little budget buy at under £100. The octa-core MediaTek SoC is perfect for most basic use cases, though the 64GB of storage and modest 4GB of RAM could be limitations if you intend to download a lot of apps or multitask often. Also be aware that this model comes with lock-screen ads by default, but you can at least pay a small fee to remove them.

Key specs at a glance: Display - 1340x800 60Hz LCD, RAM - 4GB, Storage - 64GB, SoC - octa-core MediaTek, MicroSD card slot - No.

Industry observers note that ✅Recommended if: you want a bargain-basement, compact tablet for basic day-to-day use or to give to the kids. With its modest specs, the Amazon Alexa Tablet 8 isn't going to be a good fit for power owners – but if you just want to browse the web, respond to emails, read e-books, or watch movies via streaming, it will more than suffice.

The octa-core SoC and robust aluminium case also mean that it's better than some more expensive competitors in certain regards.

Sources indicate that ❌Skip this deal if: you require more storage or RAM, or want a better screen. The substantial limitations here are that it has 64GB of storage and 4GB of RAM. The storage unfortunately cannot be upgraded, as there is no microSD card slot, so you will likely run out of space rapidly if you want to download Android apps. The RAM will also limit your ability to multitask.

Finally, the screen is just 1340x800 resolution – HD but not Full HD. It won't be too noticeable given the small 8.7-inch size, but might look a bit fuzzy to those with sharp eyes.
