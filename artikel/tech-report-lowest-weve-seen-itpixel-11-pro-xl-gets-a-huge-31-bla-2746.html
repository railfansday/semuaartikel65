Tech Report: Lowest we've seen it—Pixel 11 Pro XL gets a huge 31% Black Friday price knock that's too hard to ignore

  • Black Friday is about a week away, but that's not stopping Google's Pixel 11 Pro XL from dropping 31% to $899.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google's Pixel 11 series was highly sought after this year. At times, it felt like there was more anticipation for this series than for the ones before it. Nevertheless, we'll still go over everything this phone has to offer in this massive Amazon deal.

Black Friday is about a week away, but that's not stopping Google's Pixel 11 Pro XL from dropping 31% to $899. This is the lowest we've seen it, and it's probably a good time to check out what it has to offer.

It's hard to look away from this one. The Google Pixel 11 Pro XL is the lowest we've seen it at, with a 31% discount on Amazon. The phone drops by a whopping $400, and that includes a captivating 6.8-inch display and all of Google's Pixel magic.

There's a lot to cover, but the Pixel 11 Pro XL is ✅ recommended if you want the top of the line from Google's Pixel series. The 11 Pro XL offers the largest screen of its slab phones at 6.8 inches. Google keeps its iconic camera bar design with the 11 Pro XL; however, a standout feature is Pixel HiLight. This is a small, circular LED inside the camera bar that lights up for notifications. If someone is calling you, Pixel HiLight will let you know.

On the other hand, you'd likely ❌ skip this deal if you're a bit conscious about certain technical/hardware decisions. One of the Pixel 11 Pro XL's biggest drawbacks is its smaller battery than the Pixel 10 Pro XL's. Unfortunately, the cell drops from 5,200mAh to 5,115mAh. What's more, Google hasn't done a great job with the phone's PWM dimming. If you're prone to headaches or eye strain, the Pixel 11 Pro XL won't help.

Another major drawback for us is the price—the official price. Google raised prices this year for its Pixel 11 series, including the Pixel 11 Pro XL. This discounted price on Amazon looks all the more intriguing, remembering that.

There are aspects that we wish Google had done better on the Pixel 11 Pro XL. That goes without saying. However, there's plenty of good, too. If you're looking to snag this huge 31% discount on the Pixel 11 Pro XL from Amazon and make it your fresh daily driver, you'll find a pretty sweet camera experience. The Pixel 11 Pro XL feels like a return to the great cameras Google used to have on older Pixels.

A 50MP wide and 48MP ultrawide lens will carry you through a lot of your daily photography. Google has also included Magic Capture. To us, this is one of its standout features. Magic Capture helps ensure the best moments are captured, so you never forget them. If you're looking to capture smiles, laughter, or insane moments, Magic Capture does its best to solidify them. Even the video quality on the Pixel 11 Pro XL took a major step forward.

Sources indicate that on the hardware side, Google integrated its Tensor G6 chip, which is pretty good. The chip is much better at helping customers multitask between apps, and swiping/scrolling remains incredibly smooth. Qi2 wireless charging magnets are also integrated directly into the Pixel 11 Pro XL's body, so you won't need an external accessory.
