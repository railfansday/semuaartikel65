Of all the big Black Friday tech deals out there, smart glasses is the one category that can easily slip your mind - but that's not the case for us at Android Central.

When it comes to smart glasses, they're a relatively upcoming area of tech, and owners are still figuring them out. But following our series of tests and reviews, there's no denying that everyday tools such as language translations and media playback. The best smart glasses are also great for capturing live footage hands-free - especially if you're quite the athletics enthusiast who spends a lot of time on the track.

Right now, the pool of Black Friday deals on smart glasses is a little shallow, and we'll be keeping our eyes peeled in the hope that more offers surface the closer we get to the event itself. However, Meta has already pulled through with some price drops across Amazon and Best Buy, so that's your best place to start hunting if you've been itching to step into the world of AI glasses.

Meta's flagship AI glasses are down to their lowest price at both Best Buy and Amazon, packing an excellent 12MP camera for capturing live moments, two open-ear speakers, and a ton of AI features. One thing to be aware of is the model's thickness and heavy weight, which may not be suited to non-tech-savvy owners.

It has been revealed that meta's Adventurer AI glasses are just like its Ray-Ban siblings, but without the fashion-forward focus. They're slightly cheaper purely because of the lack of Ray-Ban branding, but still pack the same capabilities. These will save you a couple of bucks if you're happy to miss out on the Ray-Ban name.

Recent findings suggest that if you want a pair of smart glasses with a thicker frame for stability, that's where the Meta Fury glasses come in. Similarly, these also come with two open-ear speakers and AI tools such as language translation and wellness features, with 8 hours of battery life on a full charge. Its charging case also provides an additional 40 hours of charging on the go.

Recent findings suggest that while Meta's classic smart glasses are best suited for casual, everyday use, the Oakley Vanguards are designed for those who enjoy high-intensity sports and activities. We gave them 4.5 stars and dubbed them the perfect athletic companion for capturing action while providing solid eye protection.

It has been revealed that the Oakley HSTN glasses are another good choice for sports glasses, especially if you're a keen runner and want a hands-free way to snap videos. We tested them on several running sessions and were surprised by how stable the video results were, even when passing through bumpy terrains.

Sources indicate that if you're not a fan of the Meta brand, then try RayNeo as an alternative. The biggest difference, apart from the slightly higher price tag, is support for 12-bit Dolby Vision, which currently available Meta models don't have. The RayNeo glasses also have double the number of open-ear speakers.
