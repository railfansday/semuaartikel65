Smartphones and social media have completely changed the way youngsters communicate and interact with each other, but one tech organization wants to resurrect a classic method of communication so kids can chat with each other just like the good old days.

Seattle-based brand Tin Can has been reinventing the landline phone for the younger generation for the past two years, and now the kid-friendly mobile devices are finally making their UK debut on November 30, mimicking the design and feel of landline devices that are now a thing of the past. But these aren't fake Fisher-Price toys — these things actually work.

In a recent development, vibrant and bold in design, Tin Can phones are wired to ensure your child has access to a safer means of communication. Each Tin Can device is Wi-Fi enabled, deliberately leaving screens and algorithms behind for a basic, simplified experience. If you're a parent of a child who's been begging for a smartphone, Tin Can adds a layer of reassurance that your kid is using tech that's safe and foolproof.

Each Tin Can phone operates over a private network, and only contacts approved by adults can call the device, and vice versa. Parents can approve contacts through the Tin Can companion app, where they can also set quiet hours for studying and winding down time. But the safety aspect is just one factor behind Tin Can's development.

Yes, shielding young customers from the dangers of digital malice in the age of social media is important, but the brains behind the Tin Can brand want to promote real conversations and independence for its young users. Stripping the device of screens, games, texting, and other means of distraction puts the focus on meaningful communication.

Recent findings suggest that despite the simplicity of Tin Can, you might find its pricing a little more complex. Each Tin Can phone is priced at £100, which, given its simple features, can be a little steep for some. That mentioned, your child can contact other Tin Can users for free on the Can 2 Can plan, which also includes calls to emergency services and calls to Tin Can users in other countries, including the US, Canada, Australia, and Newly introduced Zealand.

If you want to access wider communications, you'll need a Party Line blueprint membership (£8.99 a month or £89.99 a year), where kids can call outside UK numbers with adult approval.
