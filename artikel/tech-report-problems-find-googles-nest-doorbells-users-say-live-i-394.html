Tech Report: Problems find Google's Nest doorbells: users say Live is 'broken' and there's no clear cause

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Homeowners with Google's O.G. Nest Doorbell are reporting problems with the device, which is leaving owners in a state of limbo.

Recently, on the Google Home subreddit, people have started voicing their frustrations and experiences with a string of Nest Doorbell problems (via 9to5Google). The OP (original post) states the doorbell's Live View has been broken "for over a week now for older Nest doorbell cams." The user tested their newer (wired) outdoor cam, and that's working fine.

The user states they've raised this problem with Google, only to receive a "we are aware of many issues" response. After reaching out, many others have recounted their troublesome experiences. One user says their Nest Doorbell quit in the past few days; however, they're still receiving "movement alerts and clips, but no live video." Then comes a user who tried to troubleshoot the problem, only to wind up with a more serious issue.

It has been revealed that the user reports that their Live View broke on their older Nest Cam. They proceeded to reset the device using the pinhole on the back; however, they say, "...now the unit is dead." There was also a reported instance of an Arlo camera suffering from this Live View bug.

Google has responded to the thread, asking owners to follow these troubleshooting steps. It hasn't appeared to work, though. Curiously, at the time of writing this post, one report notes Live View has started working again. It's unclear if Google has rolled out an patch. If it did, it didn't list it in its changelog.

We've run into this matter with faulty Nest doorbells and cameras before. At one point, owners encountered an "offline bug." Users of first-gen Nest doorbells and cameras reported issues shortly after migrating from the Nest app to the Google Home app. The devices indicated an offline error, which removed many features. Live video feeds were dismantled, as were the doorbell's ringers with connected speakers.

Alternatively, Google Home and Nest speakers and displays became unresponsive, failing to answer the most basic of questions.
