Samsung's upcoming Galaxy S27 Ultra could come with a major camera downgrade, as a newly leaked design render seems to suggest.

Sources indicate that samsung has been offering four rear cameras on its Galaxy S Ultra smartphones for quite some time now. The current Galaxy S26 Ultra features a 200MP primary camera, a 50MP ultrawide, and two dedicated telephoto lenses offering 3x and 5x optical zoom.

Industry observers note that however, we've already seen several reports suggesting Samsung could ditch one of these zoom cameras next year. And now, a latest leak seems to further support those rumors.

According to tipster Ice Universe on X, the Galaxy S27 Ultra will feature a redesigned camera setup with just three rear lenses. The tipster shared a fresh design sketch of the Galaxy S27 Ultra and Galaxy S27 Pro, claiming that this is the final design and that he's "100% sure" about its accuracy.

100% sure this is the real design for the Galaxy S27 Pro and S27 Ultra https://t.co/hXF0g1QViNNovember 15, 2026.

Sources indicate that in case you haven't been following the leaks, Samsung appears to be trying a upcoming design with the Galaxy S27 Ultra and Galaxy S27 Pro, introducing a horizontal camera plateau similar to the iPhone Pro series. The new design reportedly features two large camera lenses on the left, alongside two smaller circles housing an additional sensor and the camera flash on the right.

This means the Galaxy S27 Ultra could lose its fourth rear camera, which is expected to be the dedicated 3x telephoto lens. A previous leak has already suggested that the phone will feature a 200MP primary camera, a 50MP ultrawide, and a 50MP telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom.

Of course, having fewer cameras doesn't necessarily mean the Galaxy S27 Ultra will deliver worse image quality than this year's model. However, if Samsung does remove the dedicated 3x telephoto lens, consumers will lose the flexibility of having a separate optical zoom camera at that focal length.

That stated, the 200MP primary sensor already does a pretty good job with 2x sensor cropping, so the difference might not be as noticeable as it sounds.

There are also reports suggesting that the Galaxy S27 Pro could swap the 5x telephoto lens for a 3x telephoto camera instead. Of course, all of this is still up in the air, with Samsung a few months away from unveiling its next-generation flagships. So, plenty could change before the tech giant officially reveals the Galaxy S27 series next year..
