Major retailers, including PlayStation Direct, Best Buy, Amazon (including bundle offers), Target, and Walmart, are sold out. With the upcoming release of Grand Theft Auto VI, the reseller market has gotten a little ridiculous (as has everything else).

It has been revealed that beyond the obvious hype for Rockstar's next release, Sony is dealing with broader supply chain bottlenecks. According to distributor reports, these supply constraints could keep stock tight well into 2027.

Naturally, resellers are having a field day. Sony is taking the problem so seriously that in Japan, they're forcing buyers to enter a lottery — and prove they've clocked at least 60 hours of PS4 or PS5 playtime over the last two years — just for a chance to buy a PS5 Pro.
