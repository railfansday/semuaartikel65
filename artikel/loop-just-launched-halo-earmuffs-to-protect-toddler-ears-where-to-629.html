According to recent reports, TL;DR: Loop Earplugs launched Loop Halo, a $54.95 pair of certified hearing-protection earmuffs for toddlers aged 1 to 4, arriving Nov. 16 on Loop's website (and soon after on Amazon) in cloud and berry.

In a recent development, finding the right hearing protection means you can bring your toddler to loud, busy places without worrying about them getting overstimulated or overwhelmed. While Loop Earplugs has spent the last decade making concert and sleep earplugs for adults, the Belgian brand is officially narrowing its focus to toddlers.

According to recent reports, loop introduced Loop Halo, its first certified hearing protection made specifically for children aged 1 to 4. Instead of bulky, bright, primary-colored headphones, the Halo arrives in muted tones, cloud (blue) and berry (lilac).

Getting a proper seal on a squirming toddler is usually half the battle, since little kids cannot tell you when earmuffs feel too loose or are pinching their ears. To take the guesswork out of sizing, Loop built a FitGuide directly into the earmuffs so parents can easily check strap tension. Paired with soft microfiber cushions and adjustable hook-and-loop straps, the earmuffs adapt as your child grows from age 1 to 4 while reducing noise by up to 25 dB SNR / 19 dB NRR.

It has been revealed that if you are unsure whether a room is too loud for little ears, Loop Halo works alongside the Loop Sphere app. The app uses a real-time sound meter to track noise levels around you and pings you when it makes sense to put on the earmuffs — letting kids stay free of the gear until the environment calls for it.

Recent findings suggest that cleaning up is simple, too, thanks to wipe-clean cushions and washable straps. Just keep standard safety rules in mind: Loop notes the earmuffs are meant for supervised daytime use — up to 90 minutes per session (or three hours total per day) — and should never be worn during sleep.

It has been revealed that loop Halo launches on Nov. 16 for $54.95, with availability on Amazon rolling out shortly after.
