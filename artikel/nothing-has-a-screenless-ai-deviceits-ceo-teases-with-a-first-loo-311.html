Nothing has a screenless AI device—its CEO teases with a first look

Industry observers note that nothing is reportedly working on a "screenless AI" device, which has remained shrouded since its earliest teasers. However, the company's feeling playful, and we've gotten our first glimpse of its design.

Early this morning (Nov. 11), Nothing's CEO Carl Pei teased this screenless AI device on X. Pei posted a short clip of the device, alongside a tagline that reads, "Context doesn't live in a chat box." The design is a bit vague (as you'd expect from a Nothing teaser). It's clear the device is curved and has a metallic aesthetic. There are also two glowing LEDs: one green and one orange.

It certainly gives old-school sci-fi vibes (we're thinking Lost in Space). The device also features a white button with a red circle in its center. It's largely unclear what the intent behind most of these elements is. However, the centered button could be what users press to voice their queries or tasks to the AI.

According to recent reports, carl Pei didn't say or confirm anything else—not that we expected him to. Curiously, this teaser follows roughly a week after Pei's "Toolmakers" article on X. Pei's post started by saying, "Every few generations, a technology arrives that changes how everyone lives and works. Fire. The printing press. Electricity. The internet. Now, intelligence." We can gauge where things are headed from here.

Toward the end, Pei gave the briefest, vaguest teaser for Nothing's screenless AI device, shrouded in shadow. The post says, "It has no new screen, and it couldn't have been an app." Additionally, Nothing intends to launch this screenless device "next month," in December.

I'll be real with you: I have no clue where we're headed with this. You'd imagine this device would connect to your phone—likely via Bluetooth. You'd speak your query, and the AI would respond. I'm being extremely basic because these teasers have been extremely basic. All we can do is wait.

In a recent development, let's not forget something notable here: Nothing prioritized AI over the Phone 3 two years ago. To explain, its CEO, Carl Pei, noted that the company would focus on building its AI presence rather than launching the Phone 3 in 2024. The phone launched the year after, so it wasn't that much of an issue (unless you were really waiting on it in 2024).

At the time, Pei said, "There's been a lot of hype around AI. Some great, some confusing. It’s great to see new companies rethinking the user experience and form factors." He then teased a few features customers would expect, and we've seen the majority of this debut with Essential Space and the like. In late 2025, Nothing declared it had secured $200M in Series C funding. The company would use it to develop AI-native devices and an OS for future phones.
