Industry observers note that the Black Friday offers on streaming services are rolling in, and the latest deal includes Disney Plus and Hulu for a steeply discounted monthly rate. There are sales happening for both the ad-supported and ad-free tiers that offer full access to both services, and either will save you quite a bit of money.

According to recent reports, for those who despise ads, you can opt for the ad-free version for $14.99 per month (was $21.99) for a year, costing you $180 instead of $264 over the duration. This deal is located in a small banner near the bottom of the sale page via Hulu or Disney Plus. The ad-supported tier drops from $12.99 to just $4.99, so you’ll pay $60 over the course of the year instead of $156. You have through Cyber Monday, November 30th, at 11:59PM PST to take advantage of the lower prices.

Sources indicate that you can get an ad-supported Disney Plus and Hulu bundle for $4.99 a month ($8 off) for an entire year. Both streaming services feature a deep catalog of newly introduced and classic TV shows and movies, including The Simpsons, Andor, Toy Story 5, and The Fantastic 4: First Steps.

Sources indicate that the Hulu and Disney Plus bundle is one of the best values in streaming, especially if you have kids who can’t get enough Disney movies and other cartoons. On the other hand, Hulu is home to plenty of shows made for adults, including Furious (a crime drama that gripped this household like no other show this year), The Bear, Shōgun, Alien: Earth, The Lowdown, and many more. It’s worth the subscription just for those alone.

It has been revealed that if you’re considering other subscription services, we covered discounts on Paramount Plus (I might snag this to watch Cupertino), HBO Max, and Peacock. (The Fall and Rise of Reggie Dinkins is fun!).
