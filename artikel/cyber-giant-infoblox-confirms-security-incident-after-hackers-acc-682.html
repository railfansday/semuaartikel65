📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Cybersecurity and automation giant Infoblox has confirmed a cybersecurity incident, during which hackers accessed company information through one of its technology vendors.

Infoblox is a privately held cyber giant that provides companies cybersecurity and networking services, such as domain name resolution and identity and user access management.

In a statement, a spokesperson for Infoblox told TechCrunch that the tech giant is “investigating a security event involving unauthorized access to certain company information through a compromised third-party data migration vendor.”.

“We immediately activated our incident response procedures, took steps to contain the incident and are working with leading outside cybersecurity experts to investigate the facts, understand the scope and address any confirmed risks.”.

TechCrunch has learned that the data breach involves a vendor that provides SharePoint data migration services, according to a notification sent by Infoblox about the breach, two people with knowledge of the incident told TechCrunch.

One of the people said sensitive customer data was taken in the breach.

Industry observers note that according to public internet records seen by TechCrunch, Infoblox hosts much of its files, emails, and infrastructure with Microsoft, including a SharePoint server, which TechCrunch confirmed is accessible through Infoblox’s single sign-on provider. SharePoint is web-based software that companies use to share and host internal files and documents.

Infoblox has not named the third-party migration vendor that was hacked, but one person with knowledge of the incident said that Infoblox has terminated its work with the vendor.

The firm’s spokesperson did not comment on this, but added that the company has “no indication that any Infoblox products, cloud services, software updates or build-and-release processes were involved” in the breach.

Industry observers note that infoblox would not say how many customers were affected by the incident, or if the manufacturer is aware at this time. The company also did not say, when asked, if it had been contacted by the hackers and if they had made any demands.
