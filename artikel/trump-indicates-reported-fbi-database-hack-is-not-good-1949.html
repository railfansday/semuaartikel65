📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of the National Crime Information Center (NCIC), according to reports in CNN and the Associated Press.

Recent findings suggest that the NCIC is the online database used by federal and local law enforcement to share information about warrants, background checks, and more. The scope of the breach does not yet appear to be clear.

A former FBI official told CNN that they’re not just concerned about “whether hackers gained access to sensitive information, although that is an matter, but whether they could compromise the accuracy of the information that officers rely on.”.

President Donald Trump addressed the reports during an interview Saturday with Fox News, saying, “We just heard about it and I will say that it’s not good.” At the same time, Trump also mentioned that as a result of the breach, “maybe we’ll learn things that we would have never learned otherwise because, you know, you had the Russia, Russia, Russia hoax.”.

In September, the FBI reportedly told agents and support staff that their personal information had been stolen in a cyberattack.
