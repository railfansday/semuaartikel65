One of the easiest ways to make your old smart TV feel new again is to get a streaming stick. If your TV's software has gotten noticeably slower over the years and lags a bit too much even when you're just scrolling menus, you don't necessarily need to replace the entire TV. And thanks to Black Friday, two of Amazon's best Fire TV Sticks are now $40 off.

It has been revealed that the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus is down to just $30, while the more powerful Fire TV Stick 4K Max is available for $45. Both are pretty good deals, but which one should you get?

The Fire TV Stick 4K Plus offers 4K streaming with Dolby Vision, HDR10+, Dolby Atmos, Wi-Fi 6, and Alexa+ integration. It also supports Xbox cloud gaming and is now available for just $30, a massive 57% discount.

✅Recommended if: You want an affordable 4K streaming device with Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, and access to all the major streaming apps.

❌Skip this deal if: You want more onboard storage, faster performance, or Wi-Fi 6E connectivity.

If all you're looking for is a streaming device to watch Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, and other streaming services, the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus should be more than enough for your needs. It supports 4K streaming with Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and Dolby Atmos audio, so you're not missing out on much in terms of picture or sound quality compared to the more expensive Max model.

Recent findings suggest that it also comes with the redesigned Fire TV interface, which lets you pin more of your favorite apps. There's Alexa+ integration here as well.

Recent findings suggest that amazon's most powerful Fire TV Stick features a faster processor, 16GB of onboard storage, Wi-Fi 6E, and the Fire TV Ambient Experience, alongside 4K streaming with Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos. It's now $40 off for Black Friday.

✅Recommended if: You want the fastest Fire TV Stick experience, install plenty of streaming apps, or have a Wi-Fi 6E router at home.

❌Skip this deal if: You only use a handful of streaming apps and don't particularly care about the extra storage or performance.

For just $15 more, you can get Amazon's most powerful Fire TV Stick. The Fire TV Stick 4K Max comes with a faster processor, which should make navigating the interface, switching between apps, and loading content a little quicker.

On top of that, you get 16GB of onboard storage, which is double the amount on the cheaper model. This gives you more room to install apps and games without having to worry as much about running out of storage.

Recent findings suggest that you also get Wi-Fi 6E support, which can be particularly useful if you already have a compatible router at home. There's also Amazon's Fire TV Ambient Experience, which lets you turn your TV into a digital art display when you're not watching anything.

Of course, you still get all the features available on the cheaper model, including 4K streaming, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, Dolby Atmos, Alexa+ integration, and support for Xbox cloud gaming.
