According to recent reports, instagram’s latest revision will allow you to edit your Stories without losing your views, likes, or replies. You can now edit the text, music, stickers, and drawings for up to 15 minutes after sharing your Story, Instagram head Adam Mosseri has announced.

Reverse engineer Alessandro Paluzzi first spotted Instagram working on this feature in April, but now it’s official. Previously, you had to delete and repost your Story to fix a typo or add a song that you forgot to include.

You can edit your post by opening your Story and selecting the three-dot menu in the bottom-right corner. From there, tap “Edit Story” to start making changes.

In a recent development, in addition to editable Stories, Instagram announced that it’s launching new fonts, colors, and shapes for stickers, as well as presenting them on sticker sheets that are supposed to be easier to browse. The platform is also making changes to the way you select multiple photos and adding new blur, colors, and gradient background options.
