The countdown to Black Friday 2026 is officially on, and with just seven days left until the madness hits, we're knee-deep in early deals across the best smart speakers and audio devices.

I've been a loyal Amazon Echo user ever since the tech giant first launched its family of smart home hubs, and my beloved Echo Pop has served me well for the past few years. But now that I've had time to appreciate good audio, I fear it's time to bid farewell, take the plunge, and treat myself to a long-overdue upgrade.

One of Amazon's most recent additions to its audio lineup, the Echo Dot Max, is now $84.99 down from $119.99 in the retailer's early Black Friday sale, and it's one of the manufacturer's biggest audio upgrades yet.

With a designated subwoofer and tweeter, the Echo Dot Max packs bass that is three times more robust than the Echo Dot (5th Gen). But despite its compact size, it doesn't fall short on performance, supporting Dolby Atmos and spatial audio, which is ideal for filling smaller spaces in your home or for stretching across large rooms.

Sound aside, the latest Echo range of speakers are the first devices native to the AI-powered Alexa+ which, with the help of the new AZ3 chip, offers faster, smarter responses to your prompts. Although the lineup was refreshed over a year ago, last year's Black Friday offers weren't as generous as they are this time around.

Sources indicate that for less than $100, you can score one of Amazon's latest audio devices, and it's a serious upgrade from older models on the audio and smart home fronts. While it's not the lowest price we've seen (Prime Day beats it by $20), it's cheaper than last year's Black Friday listing, but whether it will get another price drop is another question.

Industry observers note that ✅Recommended if: You've been using an older Amazon Echo device for a long time and want to upgrade to a device with advanced inner workings. If you're looking for a device that's not only punchier on the audio side, but packs improved AI features and overall smarter home assistance, built-in Alexa+ will go a long way, especially if you have other Amazon devices such as a Ring doorbell.

❌Skip this deal if: Your previous-gen Echo speaker is running just fine, or you're not a massively keen audiophile. If you're a Prime Member, Alexa+ is available in your subscription and has been rolled out to older models, so you don't necessarily need to upgrade to a new device to take advantage of its smarts.

If you're in the Google smart home ecosystem and don't want to be a traitor, then the flagship Google Home Speaker is a solid alternative option, especially if you can find a good deal on it this Black Friday.
