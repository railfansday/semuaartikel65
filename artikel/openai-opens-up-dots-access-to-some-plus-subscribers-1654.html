OpenAI's intelligent agent, Dots, is now available on the ChatGPT Plus blueprint, but not to everyone.

Initially available only on ChatGPT Pro and more expensive plans, Dots will now be available on the ChatGPT Plus blueprint, which costs $20 per month. There's a catch, though: The company is only testing this with a small number of owners.

"Heads up, we’re starting to test dots with a small number of ChatGPT Plus customers. Looking forward to the continued feedback as we expand, and I’m recently seeing a lot more positives around how good the proactivity has been after many improvements," the head of OpenAI's core products, Thibault Sottiaux, tweeted on Friday.

According to Sottiaux, all ChatGPT Business Standard people are also getting access to Dots, with rollout starting today.

OpenAI's official pricing page doesn't reflect this change yet, though it does say the Plus plan provides "early access to fresh features.".

Announced in late September 2026 and powered by either GPT-6 Astra or GPT-6.1 Terra, Dots are always-on AI agents that are built to handle "everything." They learn from your feedback, work 24/7, and can connect to more than 4,000 apps through OpenAI's plugin system.
