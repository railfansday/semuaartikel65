If you're searching for a fun, family-friendly activity to keep everyone busy over the holidays, then look no further than the Nex Playground.

This adorable little game console is now just $209, down from $299, on Amazon thanks to an early Black Friday deal.

Recent findings suggest that it runs a customized Android-based operating system designed with kids in mind, offering convenient controller-free motion controls. It comes with a selection of five games built in, too, giving you plenty to play out of the box.

This includes some modern versions of classic mobile games, like a motion-controlled version of Fruit Ninja that's an awful lot of fun.

More games are then available via a relatively cheap $89 per year Play Pass subscription, with franchises like Barbie, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, and How to Train Your Dragon all represented in the line-up.

In a recent development, the Nex Playground is a compact game console designed for families and children. It's easy to set up and features easy-to-understand motion controls that encourage movement. With support for up to four players, it comes bundled with a selection of kidSafe+ COPPA certified titles, including versions of hits like Fruit Ninja.

✅Recommended if: You're after a family-friendly console that can keep the kids entertained and comes in cheaper than other options. At this discounted price, the Nex Playground costs less than half that of the Nintendo Switch 2 — which retails for $499.99. You don't even have to splash out for extra games or controllers, as five titles are built-in and you can control everything through motion.

Industry observers note that ❌Skip this deal if: You're after a more traditional gaming console. The Nex Playground is recommended for children between five and ten, so while it is a lot of fun, older kids will likely prefer a more traditional gaming console.
