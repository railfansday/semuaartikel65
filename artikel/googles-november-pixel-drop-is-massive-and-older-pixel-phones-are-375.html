Google has announced the November Pixel Drop for Pixel phones, bringing a massive list of newly introduced features to both newer and older Pixel smartphones.

Google releases Pixel Drops every few months, bringing new features and improvements to its Pixel devices beyond the usual security patches and bug fixes. While some of these updates are relatively minor, the latest November Pixel Drop is a pretty big one, especially if you're using a Pixel 10 series smartphone.

That's because Google is bringing several features that were previously exclusive to the Pixel 11 series to last year's flagships.

According to Google's official announcement, the November Pixel Drop brings Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series, including features like Rambler, Gemini's ability to handle multi-step tasks, and personalized suggestions.

For example, if someone sends you a text asking about your ETA while you're already using Google Maps, your Pixel can provide timely suggestions and actions based on what you're doing.

In a recent development, another interesting addition is Create My Widget, which lets consumers create home screen widgets with natural language prompts. Instead of relying on existing widgets, you can simply describe what you want, and Google will generate one for you.

The feature is available on the Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series, although the Pixel 10a is notably missing from the list.

It has been revealed that google has also introduced new custom icon styles with the November Pixel Drop. The company has added 10 different styles to choose from, which use its Nano Banana AI model to generate matching app icons and give your home screen a more consistent look.

Recent findings suggest that beyond customization, Google is bringing its Pixel Search experience to older devices, allowing customers to search across apps, messages, photos, emails, and device settings directly from their phones. The feature is now available on the Pixel 6 series and newer devices.

Sources indicate that google hasn't forgotten about its latest phones either, and there are a couple of features exclusive to the Pixel 11 series. Google is upgrading Call Screen with Gemini, allowing the AI assistant to have more natural conversations with unknown callers while showing a live transcript on your phone.

Sources indicate that there are upgrades to the HiLight feature on the Pixel 11 Pro phones as well, which can now light up when you receive a Google Messages notification. Unfortunately, there's still no support for third-party messaging apps like WhatsApp or Telegram.

In addition to the Pixel Drop, Google has also announced the availability of the Pixel Tag, which it first unveiled alongside the Pixel 11 series in August. The tracker is now available individually for $29.99 or as a four-pack for $99.99. It uses Google's upgraded Find Hub network and supports ultra-wideband (UWB) for precision tracking.

Sources indicate that as for availability, Google indicates the November Pixel Drop started rolling out on November 10 and will continue reaching supported devices over the next several weeks. We haven't received the OTA upgrade on our Pixel 11 Pro just yet, but considering Google's usual rollout schedule, you should hopefully see it arrive on your device within the next few days.
