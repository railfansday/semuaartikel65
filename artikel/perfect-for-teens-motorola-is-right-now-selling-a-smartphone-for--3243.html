Sources indicate that there's nothing more frustrating than buying a family member a shiny new gadget only to find out they've broken it within just a couple of weeks. If you're using the ongoing Black Friday sales to pick up a handset for someone a little clumsier than most, whether that's a teen or someone much older, then there's no reason to break the bank splashing out on something they might, well, break.

Sources indicate that that's why this entry-level smartphone from Motorola has my attention. The Moto G Play is on sale for just $99.99 at Amazon right now, a $50 price cut compared to its usual asking price. That's right, you can get a fully fledged smartphone from a significant name brand for under 100 bucks right now, and it's even unlocked so it can be used with every carrier.

According to recent reports, what exactly do you get for that money? A basic, all-round phone geared towards day-to-day use cases like making calls and browsing social media or the web. No, it's nothing fancy, but I'd argue this actually works in its favor: this is a phone for keeping in touch rather than playing games all day.

Sources indicate that this dirt-cheap smartphone is geared towards those with basic needs who just want something that enables them to keep in touch. Its specs are very basic, and the construction is plastic, but it still makes calls, can browse the web, and download apps all the same.

Industry observers note that ✅Recommended if: You want one of the cheapest phones available from a major brand. The Moto G Play is one of the cheapest phones that you can get, especially when it's discounted to under $100 like this, which is reflected by the specs and build. It does have some highlights, though, like the 90Hz refresh rate of its screen for a smoother feel and the presence of a 3.5mm headphone jack and microSD card slot.

❌Skip this deal if: You're after more than the bare minimum. The Moto G Play is a cheap phone for a reason: it has low-end specs and a decidedly budget feel compared to more premium options.

If you want to spend more on something that's more capable, then you can find better options in my list of the best budget Android phone deals right now.
