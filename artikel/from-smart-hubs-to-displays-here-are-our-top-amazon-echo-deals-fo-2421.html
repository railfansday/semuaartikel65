It's Black Friday week, so you know what that means — deep discounts on basically anything with the word 'Amazon' on its box.

Though Amazon is just one of the many retailers that's kick-started its roster of early deals, I'm enjoying the sight of all its price drops on Echo devices. It really is the best — maybe only — time of year to be thinking of buying one, as these deals prove.

From speakers to displays, Amazon has given us some of the best smart home devices out there, and it continues to up the ante on the features, functions, and design with every latest lineup launch.

Industry observers note that just last year Amazon refreshed its best smart speakers with the fresh Echo Dot Studio and Echo Dot Max, two powerful hubs with punchy audio and smarter home assistance thanks to Alexa+ integration. But it's not all about audio devices.

When it comes to the best smart displays, Amazon houses an impressive slew of devices that are tablet-speaker hybrids - perfect if you want a smart home device with a screen, but want a bold playback experience to go with it.

If smart home devices are on the top of your shopping list this Black Friday, start here.

Industry observers note that for less than $50, you can score one of Amazon's latest audio devices, and it just got another huge Black Friday price drop. If you're using an older smart speaker and want better AI smarts, boosted audio output, and support for Matter, Thread, and Zigby in a compact device, this is the model to add to your basket.

Though it's just shy of $5 from its lowest-ever price, a near-$100 saving on one of Amazon's newer smart home additions isn't bad at all. With two full-range drivers and a 2.8-inch woofer, its sound is second-to-none in the category, and we even dubbed it our best smart display for audio - not to mention its swivel function is a small but handy feature.

The Echo Time combines Amazon's classic smart speaker with its flagship Echo Spot display to make a customizable alarm clock that packs detailed audio, and Alexa+ smarts. Since its release last year, this is the lowest price we've seen it drop to, but we're holding out hope for another small reduction come Black Friday.

It has been revealed that for a previous-gen smart display, the Echo Show 8 still packs a punch. Though it doesn't pack the handy swivel function, it sports a 8.2-inch display fitted with a camera that doesn't fail to keep within the frame at all times. It's interface is also powered by Alexa+, doubling as a smart speaker with powerful audio inner-workings.

According to recent reports, I've been using the standard Echo Pop speaker for years, and it's still going strong today. If you're looking for an uncomplicated device to kit your child's bedroom out, the Echo Pop is simple and easy to use - and the Amazon Kids Parent Dashboard gives you added peace of mind that your child is using the device safely.

The Echo Studio was released just last year as a larger sibling to the Echo Dot Max, meaning its Dolby Atmos-support, high-excursion woofer and three full-range drivers pack quite an audio punch. If you're deep in the Amazon ecosystem, you can pair five Echo speakers to your Fire TV device for a complete surround-sound system.

An older model? Yes. But the Echo Show 5 has really stood the test of time. If you're on a budget or simply don't have the counter space for a larger display, this model is a good backup that can fit into pretty much any room. It hasn't matched its lowest-ever price for almost two years, so we're not expecting any major price changes come Black Friday itself.
