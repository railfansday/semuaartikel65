Breaking: Motorola Razr Plus 2025 drops to $500 in a record-low Black Friday deal

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, black Friday is a great time to check out a foldable phone without the flagship launch prices. If you’ve been intrigued by flip phones but don’t want to spend a fortune, the Motorola Razr Plus 2025 is now making a fairly compelling case.

The Motorola Razr Plus 2025 is $500 at Amazon, a $100 (17%) discount off its regular $600 price. That's the lowest price we've seen for this foldable, making it one of the most tempting Black Friday phone deals to check out. The deal is for the Hot Pink model with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage.

You get a pocket-friendly clamshell foldable with a massive 4.0-inch external display that runs full apps effortlessly. At $100 off its usual price, this is the lowest we've seen the 256GB Hot Pink model go.

✅ Recommended if: You want a stylish ultra-compact phone with a cover screen that can run full apps without having to unfold it all the time.

❌ Skip this deal if: You want long software support, a long-lasting battery, rugged durability, or a big upgrade from older models.

The appeal of the Razr Plus 2025 begins with its compact, pocket-friendly size. Closed, it is small enough to fit into tight pockets or small bags without the bulk of a traditional slab phone.

That convenience is only possible because you don’t have to flip it open for every quick task. The cover screen is good enough to run complete apps so you can actually use it for quick replies and checking things out during the day and not just as a gimmick.

According to recent reports, under the hood, Motorola has paired the Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 processor with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. You also get a 6.9-inch 165Hz main pOLED display, a 4.0-inch 165Hz cover screen, dual 50MP rear cameras, and a 4,000mAh battery.

It has been revealed that this makes the price even more attractive in context. The phone usually costs $600, but it launched at a $1,000 retail price, so you're getting flagship-style foldable hardware for half of what early adopters paid.

That stated, there are some caveats to consider before checking out. Motorola’s software support window is a bit short compared to some rivals, and you’ll have to live with a few camera omissions and minimal upgrades over its predecessor.

But at $500, the math is firmly on your side. If you’re happy with a shorter revision promise and you love the Hot Pink finish, this deal is worth checking out before stock runs dry.
