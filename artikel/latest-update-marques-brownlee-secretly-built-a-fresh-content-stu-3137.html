Marques Brownlee has been secretly working on a project, a colossal upcoming studio the content creator unveiled on Wednesday.

Industry observers note that "This is being recorded in secret, a year ago from when you're watching it," says Brownlee in the video. So this is going to be an empty building tour, and then I'm going to be able to snap my fingers, show you the finished tour, and walk you through it. So you'll see how close I get to my vision in the final thing. I hope I get really close.".
