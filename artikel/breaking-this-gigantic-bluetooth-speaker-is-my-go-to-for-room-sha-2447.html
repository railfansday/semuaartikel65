According to recent reports, you don't have to sacrifice sound quality if you're after a speaker that can get seriously loud. Case in point: the JBL Boombox 4. This portable Bluetooth boombox is an absolute beast, reaching around 104 dB at full volume, which is roughly as loud as a motorcycle. This makes it perfect for parties and social events, where it delivers true home-filling sound.

It's a great pick at full price, but right now Best Buy has knocked $150 off to bring it down to just $399.95. It's well worth the investment, as it's a highly versatile speaker. With an IP68 waterproof and dustproof rating and an extremely durable rubber and cloth exterior, you can take it outside in any conditions. Poolside cocktails? It's got you covered. Rainy BBQ? Handled with no sweat.

In a recent development, featuring two large, powerful woofers, two tweeters, and three passive radiators, the audio quality is formidable, offering both punchy bass and a surprising level of depth and clarity. I like to use mine while I work (it's actually playing right now as I write this), and find that the 1/3 volume mark is where it really shines – delivering beautiful sound that comfortably fills my large room without becoming too overwhelming.

It has been revealed that battery life is good too. JBL indicates that you can get about 28 hours from each charge, though you should expect something around the 10-hour mark unless you're playing music very quietly. This model features a user-replaceable battery as well, so you don't need to worry about your expensive purchase becoming unusable due to cell degradation in five or ten years.

This absolute audio behemoth is my go-to Bluetooth speaker when I want something loud that still sounds superb. It delivers thumping bass while remaining beautifully clear, and is practically designed with waterproofing and a useful built-in carrying handle.

Industry observers note that ✅Recommended if: You want a durable, portable Bluetooth speaker that offers incredible volume without sacrificing sound quality. It's ideal for social events, but is also a brilliant pick for at-home listening. Just make sure your phone volume isn't set too high, or the neighbours might complain!

❌Skip this deal if: You would rather go for an even more portable model. The JBL Boombox 4 is designed to be moved around, but it's still quite large and heavy, making it difficult to move over long distances despite the carrying handle.

If you want a portable speaker to take on your next hike or holiday retreat, then I would instead consider the brand's smaller models like the JBL Flip 7 or JBL Grip. They don't sound as good and are nowhere near as loud, but they are simply more practical in those kinds of situations.
