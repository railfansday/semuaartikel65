If you're serious about your music, then you might already know the Status name. This Latest York-based headphone maker has become one of the most significant boutique audio brands with a reputation for quality, audiophile-approved sound.

I use a pair of Status Pro X earbuds almost every day, as they're the best-sounding wireless model that I've ever owned. Right now, they can be the best earbuds you've ever heard, too, for an Amazon lowest-ever price of $179 (down from $249) thanks to a new Black Friday deal.

It has been revealed that their secret is the presence of three distinct drivers: a 12mm bass driver in addition to two tweeters. This produces crystal-clear, accurate sound that really elevates your listening experience. It's all wrapped up in a classy design and comes with a robust companion app that lets you create your own EQ or explore a range of presets for the perfect listening experience.

According to recent reports, this is a upcoming lowest-ever price for the Status Pro X buds. Designed for audiophiles, these wireless earbuds offer 24 bit / 96kHz Hi-Res sound with support for the LDAC codec and a superb companion app. They're not cheap, but you can hear every cent.

✅Recommended if: You want wireless earbuds that simply sound fantastic. The Status Pro X buds deliver crystal-clear, accurate sound with bass that's strong but never overpowering. They support a range of popular codecs, including the high-fidelity LDAC.

There are some disadvantages compared to competitors, which I'll outline in just a moment, but the sound is second to none.

❌Skip this deal if: You want the best noise-cancelling or battery life. I've tested plenty of wireless earbuds, and while none can beat the Status Pro X for sound, there are plenty of other options that offer better ANC and battery life.

I find my Status Pro X model tends to struggle to drown out intense wind or the sound of airplane engines. I also only manage to squeeze three or four hours of use per bud on each charge, which can be problematic on longer trips.

Still, these are small downsides in the face of sound that sounds this good.
