SAVE OVER $70: As of Nov. 20, the Lego Pokémon Iconic Trainer Moments Poké Ball has hit a newly introduced low price of $224.99 at Amazon. Compared to its full price of $299.99, that's $75 in savings.

Now that some Black Friday sales are fully up and running, we're keeping an eye out for any Lego deals worth shopping.

In good news for Pokémon fans, we've already spotted quite a few sets on sale at Amazon, including a record-low price drop on the 2,386-piece Lego Iconic Trainer Moments Poké Ball.

Recent findings suggest that as of Nov. 20, this brick-built Poké Ball — which only just dropped in October — has received its first major discount at Amazon, bringing its price down to $224.99. Overall, that saves you $75 off the list price.

Industry observers note that not only do the 2,386 bricks create a huge Poké Ball set atop a patch of grass as its display stand, but it actually features two scenes on the inside when you open it up: a battle arena and Professor Oak's laboratory. Of course, it also comes with minifigures of Red, Picnicker, and Professor Oak to fill out these scenes (plus figures of Eevee and Pikachu), alongside Poké Balls and Pokédex tiles.

If you've had your eye on this Lego Iconic Trainer Moments Poké Ball as a gift for a Pokémon fan over the holidays, or maybe a treat for yourself, don't miss out on this great deal at Amazon.
