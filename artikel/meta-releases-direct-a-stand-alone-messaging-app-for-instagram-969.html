Meta releases Direct, a stand-alone messaging app for Instagram

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, meta is testing Direct, a new stand-alone messaging app that houses Instagram’s direct messages. Direct is initially launching in Turkey and Thailand, and can be used with or without an Instagram account.

With Direct, Meta is likely looking to retain owners who use Instagram primarily, or even exclusively, for messaging. Over the years, people have often sought various ways to use Instagram’s direct messaging features without having to deal with the rest of the platform.

Direct appears to be Meta’s answer to that demand, as the tech giant claims it is designed for chatting one-on-one or in groups with friends and family.

The fresh app also gives the company a way to keep these owners within its ecosystem rather than risk losing them altogether, and count them as active users within its ecosystem, even if they don’t use Instagram itself.

Besides usual messaging features like reactions, voice notes, and emojis, Direct also features a “Draw” feature that lets you doodle and sketch directly inside chat threads. There’s also “Notes,” which are the short status updates that appear at the top of your Instagram DMs.

Meta says customers can create custom stickers, add audio effects to replies, and personalize chat backgrounds with custom themes.

In a recent development, meta says only people in your contacts or those you’re already connected with can start conversations or add you to group chats. Users can also report, block, restrict, or remove contacts at any time.

In a recent development, the manufacturer also says it will use its age-estimation technology to identify and address potentially inappropriate interactions involving young users. Parents will also have access to supervision tools that let them see who their teens are messaging, manage safety settings, and set daily usage limits.

Teens who use their Instagram username and password to access Direct will have to work with all the same messaging restrictions as Instagram’s Teen Accounts.

Meta did not say when or if it will bring Direct to additional countries.
