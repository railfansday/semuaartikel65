Google is giving millions of people access to Gemini's Tasks feature for free.

The tool can run multi-step requests in the background, and Google is expanding its capabilities to include shopping, phone calls, email, and deeper integrations into its apps.

Formerly known as Spark, Tasks is now rolling out to free Gemini owners in the US, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, as per Josh Woodward, VP of Product for AI at Google. This agentic mode previously required a paid AI Pro or AI Ultra tier.

Sources indicate that gemini can now do your tasks free of charge in six countries! We’ve built it for anyone who has tasks piling up during all the classes, meetings, and school drop-offs that fill the day. Our goal is for Gemini to get the job done and save you time and money along the way. 3 major updates: 1) Running on our newly introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, Gemini can get stuff done at no cost for hundreds of millions of people across the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. 2) Many highly requested features are rolling out now, starting in the US: * Call local businesses (via Google Duplex) * Pay and check out (via Google Pay) * Text Gemini on Android or iOS (via RCS) * Forward emails to Gemini (via Gmail) * Sign into sites without sharing passwords outside Google (via Google Password Manager) 3) We’ve also landed our biggest enhancement yet for the Gemini <> Google Workspace integration. The tools trigger more reliably, and you can do much more without ever leaving Gemini. Draft or send emails. Create and edit Docs or Sheets. Add multiple Calendar events at once. Ready to hand off your next task? We can’t wait to see what Gemini crosses off your list first.November 17, 2026.

According to recent reports, it runs on Google’s new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model and allows people to switch from Chat to Task mode to pass off work instead of babysitting every step. That could involve asking Gemini to plan a trip, research a topic, set up a morning briefing, or manage recurring tasks while you’re busy doing literally anything else.

To test it, flip the toggle above your mobile prompt box (or below on the web) from "Chat" to "Task." You can even bypass the app entirely by forwarding emails or texting Gemini directly through Google Messages and iMessage.

The Workspace integration is also receiving a major upgrade. Google notes Gemini can now more reliably draft or send emails, create and edit Google Docs and Sheets, and add multiple events to Google Calendar without toggling between apps.

In a recent development, as you can imagine, having an AI in your inbox, passwords, and payment methods does raise some security concerns. Google says when browsing the web, Tasks runs inside an isolated virtual machine, protected against prompt injection and Google Safe Browsing protections. Gemini also asks for confirmation before it buys anything and can ask for clarification if your instructions aren’t clear.

Industry observers note that that noted, Google still has some work to do. Some sites might prevent Gemini from completing a task and ask you to step in and finish the last step. For now, the promise of a digital assistant that can really take work off your hands is getting closer — but still isn’t quite ready to be trusted with everything.
