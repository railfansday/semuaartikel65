📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Following a past teaser, Motorola has mistakenly posted an early support page that not only confirms the name of its wide foldable as the “Razr Flex,” but also confirms it will be launching in 2027.

The wide foldable trend continues to expand, with Motorola set to launch the “Razr Flex” as its iPhone Duo/Galaxy Z Fold 8 competitor. While there’s been no official announcement, Motorola did directly tease the device around Apple’s launch event, showing it in a silhouette (pictured above).

Now, Motorola has, presumably by accident, confirmed the name and release window of that wide foldable.

Recent findings suggest that A support page on Motorola’s website lists the name of the device as “Razr Flex,” a name we’ve previously heard in connection with this wide form factor, while the page also directly states that the device is a 2027 release. There’s no firm release date, but it’s almost certainly not arriving before the end of the year, despite initial leaks around Motorola’s wide foldable hinting at a 2026 release.

Industry observers note that the Motorola “Razr Flex” is expected to have a very compact form factor and, based on Motorola’s initial plans, will likely have 3D glass and at least wants to have a creaseless display, seemingly with just two cameras on the back similar to Apple and Samsung’s wide foldables. The outer display might be a mere 4.97-inch panel, much smaller than others.
