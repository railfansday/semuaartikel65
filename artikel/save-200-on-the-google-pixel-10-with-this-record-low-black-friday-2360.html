Save $200 on the Google Pixel 10 with this record-low Black Friday deal

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Black Friday is the best time to upgrade your flagship smartphone without breaking the bank, as prices keep going up. Google’s regular flagship has to be a contender if you want a small Android phone with good cameras and useful software.

Right now, the unlocked Google Pixel 10 is on sale at Amazon for $599, down from its usual price of $799. That's 25% off, saving you $200 and tying the lowest price we've seen for this model. You’ll want to act fast, though, as this deal is live only while supplies last.

According to recent reports, the Pixel 10 combines Google's software experience with practical AI features, a versatile triple-camera system, and a bright OLED display. At $599, you're getting a phone that originally introduced at $799 for considerably less.

✅ Recommended if: You’re an average smartphone user, an iPhone switcher or a budget buyer who likes clean software, useful AI features and a simple design.

❌ Skip this deal if: You’re a heavy mobile gamer, a videographer, a power user who hates slow charging, or you own a recent Pixel already.

Sources indicate that the Pixel 10 isn't trying too hard to be different, with a 6.3-inch screen and a design that's pretty much "business as usual." The software experience is clean and a big part of the appeal, especially if you prefer Google's take on Android over heavily customized interfaces. It's also worth considering if you're thinking of moving away from an iPhone.

Recent findings suggest that one of the phone’s major selling points is its triple rear-camera setup, which adds a 5x telephoto lens with up to 20x Super Res Zoom. You also get Google’s Tensor G5 processor and 12GB of RAM, along with software features meant to make everyday tasks more convenient. That stated, the Tensor G5 isn't particularly powerful for demanding workloads, so heavy gamers may want to look elsewhere.

Industry observers note that the numbers make the device a more interesting deal. It has a 6.3-inch Actua OLED panel with a 120Hz refresh rate and peak brightness of up to 3,000 nits. Google claims you’ll get over 24 hours of battery life, and the Qi2 Pixelsnap magnetic wireless charger is yet another handy convenience. The phone is also IP68 water and dust resistant and has Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2 protection.

Of course, $599 doesn’t mean the Pixel 10 is without compromises. You get downgraded main and ultrawide camera sensors, slow charging speeds and buggy software updates that can make the experience frustrating. If raw performance, fast charging or heavy-duty video work are what you are after, the Pixel 10 isn't the phone we'd recommend.
