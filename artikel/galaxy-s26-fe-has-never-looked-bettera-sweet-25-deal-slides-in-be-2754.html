Galaxy S26 FE has never looked better—a sweet 25% deal slides in before Black Friday

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Let's get something straight right off the bat: the Galaxy S26 FE beat the S26 Ultra to One UI 9. Amidst those heavy delays, Samsung's latest FE phone launched with its newest Android OS upgrade well ahead of its 2026 flagship Ultra.

Industry observers note that jokes aside, the Galaxy S26 FE is still a darn good phone for its price. Black Friday is roughly a week away, and Amazon's already knocked the phone down by 25%.

Industry observers note that samsung's latest FE model—the Galaxy S26 FE—is down 25%, dropping its price from $799 to $599. The phone comes with 256GB of storage, the latest Android 17 (One UI 9) software, and Galaxy AI.

If you're asking us, the Galaxy S26 FE is ✅ recommended if you want the latest tech on a budget. Keep in mind that the S26 FE is $799, but with this 25% deal, you'll get it for $599. If you want a slightly bigger phone, the S26 FE offers a 6.7-inch display that outshines many other midrange devices. One UI 9 (Android 17) is another plus, with various UI updates, smoother animations, and AI privacy alerts. Samsung will also support the Galaxy S26 FE with seven years of OS upgrades.

You should probably ❌ skip this deal if you're looking for a more robust phone. The Galaxy S26 FE is a mid-range phone, so its performance isn't top-tier. The phone features Samsung's Exynos 2500 SoC, and while the chip has improved over previous iterations, it may still struggle graphically. What's more, the S26 FE's camera system pales in comparison to Samsung's flagships, as its main highlight is a 50MP main sensor.

According to recent reports, all in all, the Galaxy S26 FE is still a solid buy at 25% off. Samsung introduced the phone this year at a price $50 higher than in previous years. So, seeing it at this discounted price is all the more enticing. Another thing to remember is that the phone's screen size matches the Galaxy S26 Plus—a flagship phone. Samsung tried to bridge the best of both worlds (midrange and flagship), and it does that fairly well.

There are some caveats, but with 256GB of internal storage and a 120Hz display refresh rate, the S26 FE's early Black Friday deal isn't that bad.
