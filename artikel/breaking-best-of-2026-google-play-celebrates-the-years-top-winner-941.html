It's that time of year again when the cold air mixes with a year's worth of new apps, games, and books. Google Play is celebrating its 2026 "Best Of" winners; see if your favorites won.

In a recent development, this morning (Nov. 17), Google revealed its Best of Play award winners. As you'd expect, a few AI apps have made their way into the Best of 2026 celebrations. Google states a few winners highlight "a deeper need for technology that’s both highly efficient and genuinely supportive." The awards kick off with Best Overall Game and App. For the latter, Notability: AI & PDF Notes snags the top trophy for Best App, delivering a digital notebook experience for learning, work, and productivity.

Gamers, whether they're on PC or mobile, will recognize the Best Overall Game winner: Arknights: Endfield. Developed by GRYPHLINE, Arknights: Endfield offers a story-rich experience and an extensive factory-building function for gathering resources and crafting key supplies to level up characters, weapons, and more.

In a recent development, google emphasized the need for technology to keep progressing and better accommodate customers. That sentiment takes us into the next category: Best Cross-device Game and App.

Industry observers note that for Best Cross-device App, Google named Strava: Run, Bike, Walk as the winner. We've tested and experimented with Strava for a while at Android Central. We've even visited its San Francisco HQ in 2025 and interviewed its CEO, Mike Martin, and Chief of Staff, Shelby Rhodes, about Athlete Intelligence. Strava also recently partnered with Google's Gemini to bring upcoming insights through Connected Apps.

It has been revealed that for Best Cross-device Game, Moonlight Peaks took the trophy. It's described as a slower, slice-of-life game where players often tend their magical crop garden or "woo werewolves.".

Let's not forget all of the books that have come out this year. For fiction, Google awarded The Everlasting by Alix E. Harrow the "Favorite Fantasy" award and Anatomy of an Alibi by Ashley Elston the "Favorite Thriller" award.
