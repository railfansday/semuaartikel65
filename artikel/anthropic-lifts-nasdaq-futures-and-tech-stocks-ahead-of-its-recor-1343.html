Hello, this is Leonie Kidd with today's edition of Morning Squawk.

A historic IPO for markets and an exclusive for CNBC.

It has been revealed that andrew Ross Sorkin will speak to Anthropic co-Founders Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei at 10 a.m. ET, as the biggest public listing in history starts trading on the Nasdaq.

Anthropic has priced its IPO above the expected range, at $83 per share. With around 980 million shares sold, it has raised $81.3 billion, surpassing SpaceX's record IPO back in June.

Anthropic's fully diluted market cap sits at around $1.5 trillion. The stock is expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq today under the ticker symbol "ANT." CNBC's Leslie Picker will report from the Nasdaq throughout the trading day.

It has been revealed that nasdaq futures are higher in anticipation, as traders gear up for this historic moment. Shares in chipmakers and companies that are invested in Anthropic are also higher premarket.

Shares in Nvidia are gaining in premarket trading, alongside other tech heavyweights Amazon, Alphabet and Broadcom.

Sources indicate that palo Alto shares are jumping over 9% in premarket trading, after the group raised its annual revenue forecast. The move is also pulling cybersecurity shares higher too.

In a recent development, an unprecedented trilateral summit has taken place between U.S. President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

After 13 long years, gamers can finally play "Grand Theft Auto VI" as its global rollout gets underway.

It's not just players rejoicing; investors are also applauding the blockbuster release. Shares in the studio behind the game, Take-Two Interactive, traded higher on the revenue boost.

Kash Patel skips House hearing after Mar-a-Lago gala with $50,000 access.

Industry observers note that after a string of outages disrupted airlines, banks and even the National Security Agency, U.S. House members gathered Wednesday to question the nation's top security officials. But FBI Director Kash Patel was nowhere to be seen.

The night before, Patel and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin had been advertised as "honored guests" at the Massive Blue Bash, a lavish charitable fundraiser at President Donald Trump's Mar-a-Lago club, where attendees were offered access to a "private roundtable" and a stay at the venue for $50,000 a person.
