Samsung has finally declared Galaxy Sync for Galaxy smartphones, allowing users to automatically sync photos and videos from Samsung Gallery to Google Photos in the background.

It has been revealed that after Samsung ended its partnership with Microsoft OneDrive earlier this year, there were reports that the company would partner with Google to bring a upcoming Gallery Sync feature to Galaxy smartphones, allowing users to sync their photos directly from Samsung Gallery to Google Photos. And now, the feature is finally here.

Recent findings suggest that according to Samsung's official announcement, the fresh Gallery Sync feature allows users to back up their photos and videos directly to Google's cloud storage without having to switch to another app. All you need to do is sign in with your Samsung and Google accounts and enable the feature in the Gallery app.

What's interesting is that the newly introduced integration also makes it easier to access your photos across multiple Galaxy devices.

For example, if you take a photo on your Galaxy smartphone, you'll be able to see it in the Gallery app on your Galaxy tablet, provided both devices are signed in with the same accounts and have syncing enabled.

It has been revealed that samsung is also introducing a newly introduced "Free up space on this phone" feature that lets consumers remove photos and videos from their device once they've been backed up to Google storage. The Gallery app will continue showing thumbnails of those files, and you can download the original versions whenever you need them.

As for storage, the feature uses your existing Google account storage, which is shared across Google Photos, Gmail, and Google Drive. This means you get the same 15GB of free storage that comes with a Google account.

There is, however, one limitation worth keeping in mind. The fresh integration doesn't mean your entire Google Photos library will automatically appear in Samsung Gallery. For now, only photos and videos synced through Samsung Gallery after enabling the feature will show up across your supported Galaxy devices.

In terms of compatibility, Galaxy Sync has started rolling out to Galaxy devices running One UI 9, and Samsung indicates it will expand availability to more devices in phases.
