Anthropic hits Nasdaq exchanges at $111, 33% above IPO price

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Shares of Anthropic are now trading on the Nasdaq exchange, as the company successfully executes its initial public offering. In the early minutes of availability, shares were trading at $111.50, a 33% premium over the $83 IPO price.

Sources indicate that set on Wednesday, the IPO price was already higher than the projected range set by the organization, which anticipated a price between $70 and $78 per share. All told, the offering will raise over $81 billion, directed to a combination of employees and the company’s own coffers.

The IPO funding, together with existing fundraising and newly introduced private offerings timed to coincide with the organization’s public market debut, will leave Anthropic with an expected $220 billion in cash reserves. As one analyst told TechCrunch, “Anthropic’s going to have more cash on hand than any company has ever had.”.

The share price continues to fluctuate, but the opening price would put the manufacturer’s market cap over $2 trillion, making it more valuable than Meta, Tesla, or Walmart. Only five companies boast a higher valuation by market cap: Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, Nvidia, and Microsoft.

Industry observers note that in a speech before the opening bell, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei emphasized the tech giant’s commitment to safety. “Today puts the company in a stronger financial position, and in doing so, only strengthens our resolve to do the right thing,” Amodei stated. “If we can show it’s possible to make the right choices on safety and still build an incredibly successful company, others in our industry will be inspired to do the same. We call this a race to the top.”.
