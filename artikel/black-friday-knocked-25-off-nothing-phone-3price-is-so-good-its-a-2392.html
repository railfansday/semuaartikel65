There's a conversation to be had about the Nothing Phone 3. This phone is the manufacturer's "flagship." It's an exciting phone with a few noteworthy changes, but we'll get into the details in a moment.

The Nothing Phone 3 has dropped 29% on Amazon for Black Friday, and it's at a price that's got it in my cart already.

Recent findings suggest that the Phone 3 is Nothing's flagship. It's a phone that has upgraded its camera/video capabilities over previous generations. The Phone 3 also includes a new LED dot matrix that changes our perception of the old Glyph lighting.

There are deals on this phone all over (you can find a few more down below). However, if you prefer, Nothing's U.S. storefront is running a Black Friday sale. There's one week left. You can find the Phone 3 at the price listed above on its U.S. store.

The Nothing Phone 3 is ✅ recommended if you're looking for a fabulous phone. You might think I'm joking, but that's the reality. Nothing revamped the Phone 3's design, adding a dot-matrix panel on the back. If you love taking pictures or recording videos, the Phone 3 is definitely a keeper. Compared to the other devices in the series, the Phone 3's cameras have definitely taken a step forward. You'll also find a 5,150mAh battery, a 6.6-inch display, a triple 50MP camera array, and more.

Now, you can ❌ skip this deal if you need a phone that's actually a flagship. It's probably one of our greatest gripes with the Nothing Phone 3. The device, while the brand categorizes it as a "flagship," misses the mark. It features Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8s Gen 4, a mid-range chip with "accessible premium features." But it still doesn't carry the power you'd expect from a true flagship chip, like the Snapdragon 8 Elite.

According to recent reports, we were hoping for a newly introduced Nothing flagship this year—in 2026—but the tech giant said otherwise. In January, Nothing's CEO, Carl Pei, said the Phone 3 would be the brand's 2026 flagship. There were no aims to unveil another, as it moved ahead with the Phone 4a series. What's more, Nothing committed to introducing new features for the Phone 3 through Nothing OS 4 updates.

This wasn't necessarily a bad thing. The Phone 3 captured consumers when it introduced. The LED dot matrix was a new take on the Glyph Lightning we were used to, and its vibrant OLED screen was a welcome addition. Its design shake-up also keeps up those unmistakable Nothing vibes. However, the main thing is that the phone isn't truly a flagship. Its chip of choice and GPU could be better. The Phone 3 falls within a mid-range sector if you focus on that alone.

It's also another reason this Black Friday deal is so good. At its actual starting price, we can't justify the device. However, with this sweet price drop, it's worth it given the software, camera and video upgrades, display, long battery life, and fast charging.
