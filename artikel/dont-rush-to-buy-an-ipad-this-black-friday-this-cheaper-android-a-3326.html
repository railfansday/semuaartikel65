You might be tempted to pick up the 2025 iPad this Black Friday, but with prices starting at $379, there are far cheaper and, in some ways, more capable Android alternatives to consider.

Lenovo is my go-to brand for Android tablets in the US, as I've tested multiple models and have always appreciated their high build quality, capable software, and modern designs. Right now, the Lenovo Idea Tab Plus is a Black Friday highlight at just $229.99 (was $294.99) at Amazon – a saving of just over $149 compared to the iPad.

It might cost less, but you get some key upgrades compared to Apple's offering with the Lenovo tablet: namely, a faster 90Hz screen (compared to just 60Hz on the iPad) and the ability to expand your storage via the microSD card slot. You also don't need to worry about buying expensive accessories, as Lenovo has you covered with a free folio case and compatible stylus pen in the box. You even get some spare pen tips!

In a recent development, much cheaper than an iPad, but you would be hard-pressed to tell. The solid MediaTek Dimensity 6400 SoC is paired with a decent 8GB of RAM and 128GB of expandable storage. The screen has a higher refresh rate at 90Hz, and you get a bundle of accessories included for free.

In a recent development, ✅Recommended if: You want a cheaper alternative to the Apple iPad that doesn't disappoint. This Lenovo tablet doesn't have the best chip, but the 8GB of RAM will do plenty of heavy lifting. More importantly, it boasts expandable storage if the 128GB provided isn't enough for you, has a faster refresh rate than Apple's offering (leading to a smoother-seeming scrolling and browsing experience), and even comes bundled with a case and stylus.

Industry observers note that ❌Skip this deal if: You want the longest-term software support. Apple doesn't make any software promises, but it usually supports its iPads for around six to eight years of updates.

Lenovo only promises two major Android upgrades for the Lenovo Idea Tab Plus, though it provides security support until 2029.
