This Nothing Ear 3 Black Friday deal has us losing our minds—the audio is good, but the price drop is better

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

For a pair of third-generation earbuds, you'd imagine (or at least hope for) major upgrades. Nothing delivered in a few areas where we were looking for improvements. What's more, the Nothing Ear 3 introduces a new way to interact with the device and its case.

Before we get too ahead of ourselves, there's a pair of Black Friday deals for the Nothing Ear 3 that drop them by almost 40%.

The Nothing Ear 3 continues the classic Nothing transparent design with longer battery life, redesigned audio drivers, and support for LDAC hi-res audio. The Ear 3 introduced an aluminum case with a dual-microphone system.

If you prefer, you can also find these deals on Nothing's U.S. website during its Black Friday sale.

These earbuds are ✅ recommended if you value a device that won't drop out on you. The Nothing Ear 3 battery life is a major highlight. Compared to the Ear 1 and Ear 2, the Ear 3 delivers roughly 5.5 hours of listening time per bud (Adaptive ANC enabled). This jumps to 10 hours if you disable ANC, which just means more tunes or podcasts or demonstrates/movies. Another interesting feature with the Ear 3 is Super Mic. It is a dual-microphone system in the charging case. Pressing the TALK button switches audio from your earbuds to your case—more on this in a moment.

In a recent development, however, you should probably ❌ skip this deal if you need earbuds with more reliable ANC/transparency modes. The Ear 3 falls kind of flat in these areas, leaving you wanting more. What's more, while we've praised the upgraded battery life, the Ear 3 isn't for you if you want great audio and long battery life. That sounds like a no-brainer, right? If you want LDAC audio and ANC, your battery life drops to ~3.5 hours per bud.

Recent findings suggest that super Mic is the massive new thing for the Nothing Ear 3. Press the TALK button to switch your audio output to the case. This is intended for consumers on calls or taking a voice memo. One thing that we noted in our review is that the Ear 3 flips the switch on a classic sentiment. Wireless earbuds aren't renowned for "great" mics. However, with the dual microphone system in the case, it offers something with more substance.

The redesigned 12mm drivers are also a high point for the Ear 3. Factor in its LDAC audio support and you've got a pair of buds that are pretty handy. What's more, its original starting price wasn't terrible compared to the competition. Since it's Black Friday, that price is even more attractive if you're looking for a new pair.
