Tech Report: Honor's new smartwatch has the brightest display I've ever seen on a wearable — beating the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 at a fraction of the price

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Honor just launched its latest smartwatch, the Honor Watch 6 Pro, and it looks like a formidable contender in the wearable space.

Its standout spec is a 1.5-inch AMOLED display capable of delivering a staggering 5,500nits of brightness. This is, as far as I can see per readily available spec information, the brightest smartwatch display on the market right now, beating the 5,000 nits offered by the high-end Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2 and Garmin fenix 8 Pro.

In a recent development, this should keep it legible in even the harshest sunlight, which every smartwatch user will know can sometimes be a challenge. Despite this brightness, Honor claims that the 515mAh battery will provide up to 18 days of typical usage.

The Honor Watch 6 Pro boasts a 5ATM water resistance (which should make it usable at a depth of up to 50 meters). This is in addition to IP68, IP69, and IP69K dust and water resistance ratings.

In a recent development, you're also getting a wealth of sensors for comprehensive health tracking, including an accelerometer, gyroscope, and optical heart rate sensor, plus dual-frequency GPS for accurate positioning. You can use the watch for contactless payments thanks to its built-in NFC capabilities, too.

All of this comes in an attractive package inspired by premium watchmaking, with a stainless steel front case and nanocrystalline ceramic bezel.

Sources indicate that in my eyes, Honor is one of the most exciting smartphone brands right now. Originally a Huawei subsidiary, the tech giant split off in 2020 and has steadily grown into a serious competitor to the big names like Samsung, Google, Motorola, and Xiaomi. I have tested countless smartphones and tablets, but it's the Honor ones that I keep coming back to because of their high specs and quirky phone designs.

Honor frequently undercuts its competition with attractive discount coupons and a bombardment of freebies too, and the Honor Watch 6 Pro is no exception. Its price technically starts at £339.99 (that's roughly $450), but the brand is already offering a £100 discount to celebrate the launch, which takes it down to just £229.99 (just above $300).

You also get a free pair of clip-on earbuds, a bonus brown watch strap, and a complimentary 'worry-free' service plan that offers one free repair for screen or liquid damage, as well as an extended warranty service (you can read the full terms of that here).

Recent findings suggest that it's a limited-time offer, but I expect it or similar discounts to return regularly in the coming months and, at this price, the value proposition is excellent on paper. It's going head-to-head with the likes of the Samsung Galaxy Watch8 or Huawei Watch Fit 5 Pro, and beats both on battery life and screen brightness.

I also think the design of the Honor Watch 6 Pro is more attractive, as it looks a lot less like a traditional smartwatch than those two alternatives, but that, of course, comes down to personal preference.

It's not a complete slam dunk, though, as Honor loses to Samsung when it comes to availability, as its products are not officially sold in the US. You also have the matter of Honor's MagicOS software, which tends to be less polished than Samsung's One UI Watch in my experience.

I've reached out to Honor to request a unit to see how it stacks up in the real world, so expect a full review soon.
