Industry observers note that as someone who devours live radio and podcasts, there's no smart home item I love more than a bedside smart speaker. I've owned loads of different models over the years, including options from Lenovo, Google, and, of course, Amazon, but my most recent upgrade involved swapping out my reliable Echo Spot in favor of the upcoming Echo Time.

It has been revealed that I bought both speakers for full price on their respective launch days, and have had plenty of time to test the two models and really get to grips with the many differences between them. Whether you should go with the Echo Spot or Echo Time is likely going to be a significant question this Black Friday. After all, the Echo Spot has dropped to just $44.99 (down from $109.99) as part of the sale, while the Echo Time has fallen to $99.99 (from $199.99) thanks to a mega 50% off discount.

Sources indicate that but is the Echo Time actually worth double the cost? Here's everything you need to know about the two models, and my take on which is the better pick in the ongoing sales.

Sources indicate that the Amazon Echo Spot is the better pick if you want a basic, distraction-free listening experience. It features a small screen that's used for little more than displaying a clock face and showing some simple animations. Of course, as an Alexa speaker, you can also ask it for the weather, to tell fun jokes, or set alarms.

In a recent development, ✅Recommended if: You want a simple, no-nonsense smart speaker for your nightstand. The Echo Spot has a compact display that is used to show you the time and, occasionally, a basic animation or two. It's the ideal model if you want something that functions like a standard clock, but with the added smart-speaker functionality of Alexa.

Sources indicate that ❌Skip this deal if: You're after a more advanced, customizable bedside companion with better sound. I frequently found myself frustrated by the limitations of the Echo Spot, particularly the limited number of very basic clock faces that you can choose from. The Echo Time blows it away in terms of customizability. The Time also sounds better, with more detailed and rich audio.

The newer Echo Time offers richer and more detailed sound than the Spot. It's also much more customizable, with a wide range of clock face options that you can tweak to your liking. The larger screen dims at night, so it doesn't keep you awake, and gently illuminates in the morning to help you wake up. In my eyes, all of this is well worth the extra $55 if you have the budget.

✅Recommended if: You want an all-round, more advanced bedside companion. While the Echo Spot was released back in 2024, the Echo Time came out earlier this year – and you can really tell, given just how much more advanced it is than its predecessor. It boasts a large, round display that you can customize with a wide range of clock faces, as well as noticeably upgraded sound that's both richer and more detailed.

I also think the design looks and feels much more premium in your home. The Echo Spot has a matte plastic finish and a basic cloth cover over its speaker grille, while the Echo Spot is covered with this lovely woven material all over.

It has been revealed that ❌Skip this deal if: You think the Echo Spot will meet your needs. If you are simply after a small screen that can display the time and decent, but not exceptional, sound, you will most likely still be able to make do with the more basic Spot – and save yourself some cash in the process.
