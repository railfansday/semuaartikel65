Google sets hard deadline for the Nest app and smart display Assistant

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google is finally pulling the plug on two legacy smart home holdouts next year.

Starting January 25, 2027, Nest customers will need to use Google Home to manage their compatible devices, per Google Home's support page. And that's not the only major change coming: Google Assistant is also being kicked out of smart speakers and displays later next year, making room for Gemini.

In a recent development, the Nest app shutdown is another step in Google’s long-running effort to bring its smart home products under one roof. The organization says that consolidating everything under Google Home will allow it to concentrate its engineering, security, and feature development on a single platform instead of having two distinct experiences.

Most users will not have to manually move their devices. Google Home will automatically display compatible Nest thermostats, cameras, doorbells, Nest Protect smoke and CO alarms, and Nest x Yale locks. Existing settings, schedules, and camera video history will also carry over.

But video clips you’ve saved in the Nest web app won’t automatically transfer. Google recommends that before Jan. 25, customers go to home.nest.com and use its one-click Download All option to save anything worth keeping.

Recent findings suggest that there is also a separate deadline for migrating your account. Consumers who keep logging into an old Nest account must link it to a Google account by March 25, 2027. Anyone who misses that deadline faces the risk of losing their Nest account, data, and subscriptions as Google starts processing deletions.

The Nest app is not the only familiar part of Google's smart home ecosystem that is planned to be retired. Starting August 2, 2027, Google Assistant will no longer be available on compatible speakers and smart displays, the organization announced. Gemini for Home will take its place.

Gemini is able to converse more naturally, understand follow-up questions, and respond to complex requests without requiring owners to remember specific voice commands — unlike the traditional Assistant.

Google Home also features customizable Favorites, better camera streaming, advanced automations, and expanded Matter device support. Existing customers of first-generation Nest Aware will be automatically upgraded to Google Home Premium Advanced at no cost.

At least on paper, the move should simplify management of Google’s smart home ecosystem.
